# Street furniture, roof props, knocks, shop fronts

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- Near lamps and signal poles from Blender (2026-09-29): `street.py` also models `lampNear` (an eight-sided tapered pole on a base with its access door, a curved arm, a streamlined head round the game's own lens: 140 triangles) for `pool:lamp-near` (the lamps nearest the camera; the rest keep the 50-triangle post), and `signal` (a tapered pole on a base, the arm and a brace, the housing at `HOUSING` with a white-bordered back plate and a visor over each lamp: 157 triangles) for `SignalHeads`' poles (vertex colours); the lamps' positions are unchanged. `FurnitureGeo.streetGeometry` is exported for both.
- Railings and the villa's mid level (2026-09-28): the railings (`pool:furn-rail`, 50 triangles each, ~1450 within 360 m) were 18% of a low-tier frame's triangles at the spawn. Past `RAIL_NEAR` (90 m) they are now a flat 6-triangle version in their own pool (`pool:furn-rail-far`: the baluster panel, top rail and post as bands; the rail material is double-sided), refilled with the rest every 20 m of travel: low 394k -> 337k triangles, no visible change from the street. 我家 gained a middle LOD level at 70 m (`home/landmark.ts` `VILLA_MID_DISTANCE`): the detail level's meshes without the interior-only materials (`INTERIOR`: upholstery, brass, books, rugs, art, mirrors, fireplaces, joinery, marble) - 20 meshes and 26k triangles instead of 32 and 44k, the same geometry shared - so from the street, the plaza or the spawn 300 m away the rooms are not drawn through the glass; from close by they are. The skyline boxes (`city/Skyline.ts`) are packed - the hidden ones (tiles drawn in detail, landmark footprints) were scaled to nothing but still drawn - and have no bottom face: 64k -> 50k triangles on low, pixel-identical; the ~5000 boxes that remain are the city to the horizon (a per-instance cull each frame would cost more CPU than it saves).
- Street furniture from Blender (2026-09-28): `scripts/blender/props/street.py` (reuses `roof.py`'s `Part`) models the shared bike (step-through frame, front basket, thin mudguards, chain cover, the smart lock and QR plate, three-spoke moulded wheels, kickstand; `frame` is white so the instance colour paints the operator's colour), the pair of sorting bins (tapered bodies, lids with lip and handle, labels, pedals, back wheels) and the bus shelter (posts with raked arms, an arched canopy - a closed slab, or its faces came out facing down - and its lit soffit, framed glass back and end, the lightbox, the route board, a slatted bench, the stop's name) -> `node scripts/vehicles/import.mjs .scratch/blender/street.glb src/city/visual/street.json --group`; `FurnitureGeo.ts` `streetGeometry` colours each material (`STREET_LOOK`, with night glow). They are drawn near only - bikes within `BIKE_NEAR` 50 m, bins 45 m, shelters 160 m (`Streamer.ts`) - and the old box versions in their own pools beyond (`pool:furn-*-far`): the 300-triangle bin pair everywhere within 150 m doubled the bins' triangles. Knocked things fly as the near model; the knock pools draw only up to their highest slot in use (`Knock.fitCount`: 16 empty Blender bikes scaled to nothing were 29k triangles a frame), and the far pools cast no shadow (three draw calls on low). The rideable bicycle (`TwoWheelers.ts` `bike`) is the same model's `bikeRide` variant (no wheels, saddle 0.755 m and bars where the rider's rig puts pelvis and hands, crank and pedals) and `bikeWheel`, turned from the model frame (along +x, ground at 0) into the body frame (+Z forward, hubs at 0); `bikeprobe.mjs bike` rides it. The crowd's near head grid went 20 x 10 -> 32 x 18 (the field's nose, brow and jaw were lost in the coarse grid; near LOD 2470 -> ~3100 triangles, `BodyMesh.test.ts` budget 3300).
- Roof-top things from Blender (2026-09-27): `scripts/blender/props/roof.py` models seven low-poly things in the game's frame - a water tank on its I-beam stand with a ladder, a 太阳能热水器 (evacuated tubes as prisms under the long tank), an AC condenser with its fan grille, an office cooling tower, a satellite dish, an antenna mast, an exhaust fan (32-184 triangles each) - converted by `scripts/vehicles/import.mjs ... --group` into `city/visual/roofprops.json` (per thing, per material name; `Buildings.ts` `ROOF_COLOURS` colours them, `model()` places one; tanks take a tint on their 'tank' material). `clutter` (tile worker) now lays them out the way Beijing roofs are: on the housing, rows of solar heaters facing south along the long axis on the south half and a row of condensers along the north parapet, tanks, dishes; on the offices, cooling towers, blocks of condensers and exhaust fans; masts anywhere; stair houses stay in the facade mesh (a tank on some). They are their own mesh per tile, `roofprops`, with a plain vertex-colour material (`CityMaterials.roofprops`) and their own bucket (`PropBucket`: position, normal, colour in growing typed arrays, a vertex per model vertex) - written through the facade's Sink, 21 floats a vertex a triangle at a time, they made a tile's buildings five times slower to build (3 -> 18 ms; now 1.5-4 ms, `npx tsx .scratch/roof/buildtime.mts`) and the extra CPU tipped `Crowd.test.ts`'s 1 ms timing test in the full suite. Drawn only on the near 3x3 tiles, on low only on the focus tile, no shadows; the far tiles show none - the old boxes are gone (a condenser is under two pixels from there, and keeping boxes for the far tiles cost a draw call per tile). A/B with `?roofprops=0` (`.scratch/roof/roofcost.mjs quality from to`): from the air high +0.1-0.2 ms and +58k triangles, on the street high +0.15-0.3, low ~+0.1.
- Knocking street furniture (2026-09-25, 「开车碰到垃圾箱、共享单车、公路围栏没声效和撞击画面」): bins, shared bikes and railings are instanced pools with no colliders (thousands per tile), so the car went through them in silence. `city/Knock.ts` (`StreetKnocks`, owned by the streamer as `streamer.knocks`, added as its own system) tests the player's chassis footprint (from `spec.chassis`) against the raw furniture of the 3x3 tiles round the car every fixed step (each kind a few circles in its own frame; above 1.2 m/s). A hit hides that instance (`isHidden`; the streamer refills its furniture pools when `knocks.version` changes), puts a dynamic PROP body of the same shape there with the car's velocity plus a shove and a tumble, throws debris (bin rubbish, chips) and HDR sparks off metal above 6 m/s, a dust puff through `fx.smoke`, and emits `prop:hit` - audio synthesises a hollow bonk and rattle (bin), a clatter and the bell (bike), a clang of inharmonic partials and a scrape (rail); the camera jolts a little. It deliberately does not emit `vehicle:impact` (damage, the delivery job's spill, hit-stop). Knocked things go at 150 m and the instance comes back at 250 m. Bus shelters are fixed instead: `addBody` gives each two boxes from the tile's raw furniture (the back row of posts, glass, lightbox and bench; the canopy, 2.54 m up, so a car passes under it and a bus does not), tagged `shelter` - a real crash; `node .scratch/shelterprobe.mjs [outDir] [speed]` drives into one through its open front and into its end. Probes: `SIDE=1 KINDS=bin,bike,rail node .scratch/knockprobe.mjs outDir [speed] [params]` coasts the car into the nearest of each and screenshots it (shot mode starts with no detail tiles: it ticks 90 frames first), `node .scratch/knocksound.mjs` finds the master bus by hooking `AudioNode.connect` and reads each knock's level.
- Lamp posts and bus shelters in the road (2026-10-02, 「电灯柱和候车亭会无故在马路生成，容易撞车」). **Lamps**: the build kept them 0.3 m clear of every carriageway and away from their own way's junction nodes only, so a side road crossing a road through a gap in its pavement, with no node shared (恒惠路 into 建国门外大街's side road), had a lamp standing in its mouth where cars turn; now no lamp within 12 m of any node where three car roads meet (`nearJunction`) or 2 m of another road's kerb (`nearOtherCarriageway`): 129k -> 112k lamps. The deck lamps stand only on a parapet that is there (`kind === 'rail'`): opened parapets had left them in the lanes of the road merging. **Shelters**: the worker places them checking its own tile's carriageways at the back row only; `scripts/city/shelters.mts` (run by `build.mjs` after the tiles) places each stop's shelter as the game does and takes the stop out of its tile if any point of the 7.4 x 2 m footprint lies in a car road of the 3x3 tiles, end caps included: 39 of 4664. `.scratch/sweep/streetposts.test.ts` (`LAMP_M` the lamps' margin) counts lamps, deck lamps and shelters in a carriageway over the whole map: 0 / 0 / 0 (shelters were 37 by footprint). Signal poles stand at the right-hand kerb and have no collider.
- Shop fronts (2026-10-11, the third item of the 「街道太空」 plan). The facade shader already drew shop units on street-facing ground floors (pillars, a lit sign, a glazed or shuttered front; `Materials.ts`, units of ~4.8 m hashed per unit). What was wrong: **(1) a tile only knew its own roads** - a building belongs to the tile its middle is on, so one whose street ran in the next tile got no shops (a 43 m tower on 恒惠西路, whole stretches; tile keys are by building middle - `Geo.tileOf`, not `floor(x/256)` of a wall): `CityStreamer.setNetwork` grids the network and every tile request now carries the edges within 120 m (`near`, as `RoadPiece`s), used by `buildBuildings` only (handed to `placeParking` too, each street's own network copy counted as "another road" and every parked car went); tile build time unchanged. **(2) coverage**: shops also on 75% of `low` buildings, on 60% of housing/offices from 3.3 m (was 7), and on 65% of hutong houses whose wall faces a tertiary-or-better street (`MAIN_STREET`) within 16 m (they stand back behind pavement and green); `gh` for a one-storey shop is its wall height less 0.3 (hutong 0.12), from 2.7 m, and the shader's sign band scales with it (`sTop`/`sBot`: 0.95 m under 4.3 m, smaller under low eaves). **(3) signs**: `visual/ShopSigns.ts` makes 96 (`SIGN_COLS` 4 x `SIGN_ROWS` 24) seeded names the street's way - a family, lucky word or place (place names like 兰州/沙县 only on food) + one of 50 trades with its colour schemes, a second line on 45% (24小时营业, a phone number), three layouts; drawn 384 x 72 (the old 512 x 64 cells stretched every name 1.6x tall; 32 names). **(4) props** (`visual/ShopFronts.ts`, system `shopFronts`, `?shops=0`): the worker lists each shop unit exactly as the shader lays them out (`BuildingMeshes.shops`, `SHOP_STRIDE` 10: wall point, normal, width, gh, sign, flags closed/hutong/taller, hash, `room` = metres to the carriageway in front, from `roadInFront`); near the camera (70/100/130 m by tier, refilled every 12 m) a unit gets by its hash an awning (45% of open ones with room > 1.6 m, eight colours, two-shade panels and a valance, under the sign band), a projecting lightbox with its trade stacked vertically (38%, `VERT_TEXTS` atlas, lit with `uNight`; per-instance cell via `aCell` in `onBeforeCompile`), and on wide pavements (room > 3.5 m, within 70 m, not on low) a table and stools outside food shops, fruit crates, an A-frame board in the sign's colour. Five instanced meshes, no colliders; cost a few draw calls, nothing measurable (`node .scratch/parking/shopcost.mjs quality "4262,4,300" "4295,1.5,370"`). Views: `node .scratch/tam/cityshots.mjs out "4279,1.7,330>4290,2.5,380"` (恒惠西路), 鼓楼东大街 `"-380,1.7,-3402>-300,2.5,-3412"` (its hutong houses mostly stand far back in the data).
- 路口拐角不放路灯和树（2026-10-23，「把每个路口的电线杆都尽量删除，拐弯的时候很容易碰到」）：路灯原来只在离路口节点 12 米内不放（`nearJunction`），可北京大路的半宽就有 12–25 米，大路口拐角的路灯都在 12 米外，正好站在车拐弯（尤其漂移）切进去的地方；行道树只避开本条路自己的路口。现在 `scripts/city/build.mjs` 的 `nearJunction(x, z, pad = JUNCTION_PAD 10, min)` 判断「拐角」：离一个三条以上车行道相交的节点伸出去的两条臂的路沿都不到 10 米（多宽的路都按它自己的路沿算；侧街里离大路路沿 15 米的路灯只靠近一条臂，留着）。路灯另外仍保留离节点 12 米内不放；所有树（行道树、OSM 标的树和树排、公园里撒的树、卫星树冠补的树）都走 `addTree` / `addCanopyTrees` 里同一条规则。第一版只砍了行道树，卫星树冠（`addCanopyTrees`，避开已有的树）马上在空出来的拐角补上了一半。砍掉的树照样消耗随机种子（`addTree` 的 `drop`），其他树的品种、大小不变。全城路灯 209,280 → 191,015，树 2,307,095 → 2,274,257（拐角砍掉 36,230 棵，有红绿灯的路口 25 米内：路灯 7,113 → 4,546，树 18,357 → 16,273）。红绿灯杆本来就没有碰撞体（`traffic/Signals.ts` 只画），没动。重建后跑过：`shortcutprobe` 12/12 通过、`underprobe` 正常、`jumpprobe` 坡道 0/4/8/9 和三个高架飞跃都落地得分。看路口：`node .scratch/title/junction.mjs out.png x z [tod] [高度]`（shot mode 俯视，`4435 413` 是出生点西边的路口）。
