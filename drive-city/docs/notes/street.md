Past `RAIL_NEAR` (90 m) they are now a flat 6-triangle version in their own pool (`pool:furn-rail-far`: the baluster panel, top rail and post as bands; the rail material is double-sided), refilled with the rest every 20 m of travel: low 394k -> 337k triangles, no visible change from the street. 我家 gained a middle LOD level at 70 m (`home/landmark.ts` `VILLA_MID_DISTANCE`): the detail level's meshes without the interior-only materials (`INTERIOR`: upholstery, brass, books, rugs, art, mirrors, fireplaces, joinery, marble) - 20 meshes and 26k triangles instead of 32 and 44k, the same geometry shared - so from the street, the plaza or the spawn 300 m away the rooms are not drawn through the glass; from close by they are. - Roof-top things from Blender (2026-09-27): `scripts/blender/props/roof.py` models seven low-poly things in the game's frame - a water tank on its I-beam stand with a ladder, a 太阳能热水器 (evacuated tubes as prisms under the long tank), an AC condenser with its fan grille, an office cooling tower, a satellite dish, an antenna mast, an exhaust fan (32-184 triangles each) - converted by `scripts/vehicles/import.mjs ... --group` into `city/visual/roofprops.json` (per thing, per material name; `Buildings.ts` `ROOF_COLOURS` colours them, `model()` places one; tanks take a tint on their 'tank' material). `clutter` (tile worker) now lays them out the way Beijing roofs are: on the housing, rows of solar heaters facing south along the long axis on the south half and a row of condensers along the north parapet, tanks, dishes; on the offices, cooling towers, blocks of condensers and exhaust fans; masts anywhere; stair houses stay in the facade mesh (a tank on some). They are their own mesh per tile, `roofprops`, with a plain vertex-colour material (`CityMaterials.roofprops`) and their own bucket (`PropBucket`: position, normal, colour in growing typed arrays, a vertex per model vertex) - written through the facade's Sink, 21 floats a vertex a triangle at a time, they made a tile's buildings five times slower to build (3 -> 18 ms; now 1.5-4 ms, `npx tsx .scratch/roof/buildtime.mts`) and the extra CPU tipped `Crowd.test.ts`'s 1 ms timing test in the full suite. Drawn only on the near 3x3 tiles, on low only on the focus tile, no shadows; the far tiles show none - the old boxes are gone (a condenser is under two pixels from there, and keeping boxes for the far tiles cost a draw call per tile). A/B with `?roofprops=0` (`.scratch/roof/roofcost.mjs quality from to`): from the air high +0.1-0.2 ms and +58k triangles, on the street high +0.15-0.3, low ~+0.1.
- Knocking street furniture (2026-09-25, 「开车碰到垃圾箱、共享单车、公路围栏没声效和撞击画面」): bins, shared bikes and railings are instanced pools with no colliders (thousands per tile), so the car went through them in silence. `city/Knock.ts` (`StreetKnocks`, owned by the streamer as `streamer.knocks`, added as its own system) tests the player's chassis footprint (from `spec.chassis`) against the raw furniture of the 3x3 tiles round the car every fixed step (each kind a few circles in its own frame; above 1.2 m/s). A hit hides that instance (`isHidden`; the streamer refills its furniture pools when `knocks.version` changes), puts a dynamic PROP body of the same shape there with the car's velocity plus a shove and a tumble, throws debris (bin rubbish, chips) and HDR sparks off metal above 6 m/s, a dust puff through `fx.smoke`, and emits `prop:hit` - audio synthesises a hollow bonk and rattle (bin), a clatter and the bell (bike), a clang of inharmonic partials and a scrape (rail); the camera jolts a little. It deliberately does not emit `vehicle:impact` (damage, the delivery job's spill, hit-stop). Knocked things go at 150 m and the instance comes back at 250 m. Bus shelters are fixed instead: `addBody` gives each two boxes from the tile's raw furniture (the back row of posts, glass, lightbox and bench; the canopy, 2.54 m up, so a car passes under it and a bus does not), tagged `shelter` - a real crash; `node .scratch/shelterprobe.mjs [outDir] [speed]` drives into one through its open front and into its end. Probes: `SIDE=1 KINDS=bin,bike,rail node .scratch/knockprobe.mjs outDir [speed] [params]` coasts the car into the nearest of each and screenshots it (shot mode starts with no detail tiles: it ticks 90 frames first), `node .scratch/knocksound.mjs` finds the master bus by hooking `AudioNode.connect` and reads each knock's level.
- Lamp posts and bus shelters in the road (2026-10-02, 「电灯柱和候车亭会无故在马路生成，容易撞车」). **Lamps**: the build kept them 0.3 m clear of every carriageway and away from their own way's junction nodes only, so a side road crossing a road through a gap in its pavement, with no node shared (恒惠路 into 建国门外大街's side road), had a lamp standing in its mouth where cars turn; now no lamp within 12 m of any node where three car roads meet (`nearJunction`) or 2 m of another road's kerb (`nearOtherCarriageway`): 129k -> 112k lamps. The deck lamps stand only on a parapet that is there (`kind === 'rail'`): opened parapets had left them in the lanes of the road merging. **Shelters**: the worker places them checking its own tile's carriageways at the back row only; `scripts/city/shelters.mts` (run by `build.mjs` after the tiles) places each stop's shelter as the game does and takes the stop out of its tile if any point of the 7.4 x 2 m footprint lies in a car road of the 3x3 tiles, end caps included: 39 of 4664. `.scratch/sweep/streetposts.test.ts` (`LAMP_M` the lamps' margin) counts lamps, deck lamps and shelters in a carriageway over the whole map: 0 / 0 / 0 (shelters were 37 by footprint). Signal poles stand at the right-hand kerb and have no collider.
- Shop fronts (2026-10-11, the third item of the 「街道太空」 plan). The facade shader already drew shop units on street-facing ground floors (pillars, a lit sign, a glazed or shuttered front; `Materials.ts`, units of ~4.8 m hashed per unit). What was wrong: **(1) a tile only knew its own roads** - a building belongs to the tile its middle is on, so one whose street ran in the next tile got no shops (a 43 m tower on 恒惠西路, whole stretches; tile keys are by building middle - `Geo.tileOf`, not `floor(x/256)` of a wall): `CityStreamer.setNetwork` grids the network and every tile request now carries the edges within 120 m (`near`, as `RoadPiece`s), used by `buildBuildings` only (handed to `placeParking` too, each street's own network copy counted as "another road" and every parked car went); tile build time unchanged. **(2) coverage**: shops also on 75% of `low` buildings, on 60% of housing/offices from 3.3 m (was 7), and on 65% of hutong houses whose wall faces a tertiary-or-better street (`MAIN_STREET`) within 16 m (they stand back behind pavement and green); `gh` for a one-storey shop is its wall height less 0.3 (hutong 0.12), from 2.7 m, and the shader's sign band scales with it (`sTop`/`sBot`: 0.95 m under 4.3 m, smaller under low eaves). **(3) signs**: `visual/ShopSigns.ts` makes 96 (`SIGN_COLS` 4 x `SIGN_ROWS` 24) seeded names the street's way - a family, lucky word or place (place names like 兰州/沙县 only on food) + one of 50 trades with its colour schemes, a second line on 45% (24小时营业, a phone number), three layouts; drawn 384 x 72 (the old 512 x 64 cells stretched every name 1.6x tall; 32 names). **(4) props** (`visual/ShopFronts.ts`, system `shopFronts`, `?shops=0`): the worker lists each shop unit exactly as the shader lays them out (`BuildingMeshes.shops`, `SHOP_STRIDE` 10: wall point, normal, width, gh, sign, flags closed/hutong/taller, hash, `room` = metres to the carriageway in front, from `roadInFront`); near the camera (70/100/130 m by tier, refilled every 12 m) a unit gets by its hash an awning (45% of open ones with room > 1.6 m, eight colours, two-shade panels and a valance, under the sign band), a projecting lightbox with its trade stacked vertically (38%, `VERT_TEXTS` atlas, lit with `uNight`; per-instance cell via `aCell` in `onBeforeCompile`), and on wide pavements (room > 3.5 m, within 70 m, not on low) a table and stools outside food shops, fruit crates, an A-frame board in the sign's colour. Five instanced meshes, no colliders; cost a few draw calls, nothing measurable (`node .scratch/parking/shopcost.mjs quality "4262,4,300" "4295,1.5,370"`). Views: `node .scratch/tam/cityshots.mjs out "4279,1.7,330>4290,2.5,380"` (恒惠西路), 鼓楼东大街 `"-380,1.7,-3402>-300,2.5,-3412"` (its hutong houses mostly stand far back in the data).
