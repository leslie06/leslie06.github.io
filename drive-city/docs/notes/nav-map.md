# Navigation, GPS, map, labels, metro

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- GPS turn-by-turn (2026-09-29): `nav/Turns.ts` `routeTurns` reads a route's decisions (a node where the route had a choice; ones within 32 m merged, the turn measured from 25 m before to 35 m after): left/right, keep left/right, U-turn, exit (a slip road off a trunk, motorway or deck; one onto a main road from a street is just the way on), straight onto another named road, arrive; `NavSystem.nextTurn` gives the next with its distance, and the radar shows it in a strip across its bottom (`Minimap.ts`: arrow, 「290 米 右转」, 「进入 姚家园路」; `nav.turn.*` in I18n). `Turns.test.ts`; `node .scratch/signs/gpsturns.mjs "x0,z0" "x1,z1" [outPrefix]` sets a waypoint, lists the turns and drives it logging the strip.
- `nav/` — A* router over the lane graph (landmark heuristic, one-ways, turn penalties; under 1 ms), the radar in `ui/Minimap.ts`, the full map in `ui/MapScreen.ts` (Tab; pauses the game), and map data prepared in a worker. Two GPS slots: the player's map waypoint wins over a mission target, and each clears on its own (`setTarget` / `clearTarget`).
- The map like 高德 (2026-10-03, the third item): `node scripts/city/metro.mjs` (run at the end of `build.mjs`; needs osmium and the pbf) reads OSM's `route=subway|light_rail` relations into `public/city/metro.json` (82 KB): lines merged by their short name (`shortName`: no 北京地铁, no direction, nothing after a slash), the official colour (`COLOUR` where OSM has none), the track simplified to 4 m, stations from the stop members, one per name; 26 lines, 526 stations, kept where any comes within 3 km of the play area. `ui/MapScreen.ts` loads it and `rail.json` when the map first opens and draws, over the roads, the main-line railways (grey with a white dash, from 0.1 px/m), the lines (a dark casing under the colour) and the stations from 0.3 px/m (interchanges bigger). `WAIT=3000 node .scratch/signs/mapshot.mjs out.png "x0,z0,x1,z1"` (the map redraws only on ticks; the probe ticks after waiting).
- Map labels (2026-09-29, 「在地图上把主要的路或者桥，或者地点都标出来，跟高德地图一样」): the full map (Tab, `ui/MapScreen.ts` `mapLabels`) labels in layers, earlier ones winning the space: rank-0 places, interchanges (a bridge glyph and the name, from `network.json`'s `br` clustered within 700 m, `nav/Places.ts` `bridgesOf`), rank-1 places, expressway/ring shields (amber pills; 东三环 zoomed out, the full name in), then road names along straight stretches (turned to the road, upright, every ~250 px, a name not repeated within 300 px) and smaller places as the zoom allows (`RANK_S`, `ROAD_S`). Roads are whole chains (`roadChains`: canonical named links joined on the straightest continuation within 50°, slip roads and spans named after their bridge left out, resampled every 20 m) - the old labeller put a name on a single junction-to-junction link, which at street zoom was almost never long enough, so the map had next to no street names. Expressways and trunks are drawn amber. Places: `extract-pbf.mjs` also exports named stations, parks, hospitals, schools, malls, hotels, sights, offices, 小区 and 街道/neighbourhoods as GeoJSON (`.cache/osm/places.geojsonseq`), and `scripts/city/places.mjs` (run at the end of `build.mjs`; skipped without the export) writes `public/city/places.json` (1607 places, 73 KB): category, rank by kind and size, one label per name within 500 m, hospitals as people say them (首都医科大学附属北京朝阳医院 -> 朝阳医院), railway stations as 北京站 (OSM says 北京), bus terminals out, English from name:en or pinyin for short names (`scripts/city/pinyin.mjs`, shared with the signs). Each place is a coloured icon with a one-character glyph (铁 地铁, 园, a cross for hospitals, 学, 购, 宿, 景, 政, 楼, 区) and its name to the right or left, or nothing if neither fits; 街道 names are quiet text only. Places with a landmark's name or a sight within 120 m of one are left to the landmark. Clicking a place or an interchange sets the waypoint there with its name. A redraw ~1 ms; the chains are built on the first open (~20 ms). The radar shows them too (「小地图上也显示路名和地点」): the layering is `ui/MapLabels.ts` `Labeller`, shared by both with a `LabelStyle` each (the radar: 10-11 px text, 4.5 px dots without glyphs, at most 6 road names and 6 places; interchanges, shields and main roads always, side streets and rank-2 places only slow or on foot, no district names), set against any world->screen mapping - on the turning radar a name follows its road's direction on screen and stays upright, the steps along a chain are rounded to powers of two so they do not slide while the zoom follows the speed, and the player, the north badge, the turn strip and the blips are kept clear; ~0.1 ms a redraw. `node .scratch/maplabels/radarshot.mjs outPrefix "x,z,yaw[,span]" ...` clips the radar at 2x. `Places.test.ts`; `node .scratch/signs/mapshot.mjs out.png "x0,z0,x1,z1"` frames a box, `LG=en node .scratch/maplabels/mapshot2.mjs ...` also times redraws, `.scratch/maplabels/clickprobe.mjs` clicks a label.
