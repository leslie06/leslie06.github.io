# Police and the chase

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- `police/` — `WantedApi` as system `wanted`: witnessed crimes add heat (stars), police cars (`Pursuit` driver, road routes from `nav`) chase, box in and bust; out of sight they search the last-seen circle. `?wanted=N` starts wanted. 2026-09-24: a crime nobody in uniform saw no longer rolls a die - `people.witness(x, z)` picks the nearest walker (or a runner, once they have run clear), who stops, pulls out a phone (`Gait.prop`) and dials for 3.5-5 s, then `people:report` lands the star; knocking or scaring them off the phone (a car coming at them) drops the call. Each new star toasts what it means (`wanted.up1-5`), pops on the HUD and plays a siren whoop (`wanted:level` -> audio `stinger`); while they search, a bar under the stars fills with `WantedApi.evade`; from three stars `Pursuit` rams instead of matching speed. Pedestrians shout (`people:shout`, HUD bubbles projected from world points: 「哎哟！」「看着点儿！」「我报警了！」) and traffic drivers hit or held up by the player lean on the horn (`traffic:horn`, per-driver cooldown). `node .scratch/witnessprobe.mjs` runs someone down, lets a witness call, and charges at one.
- The GTA-style chase (2026-09-26, 「警察追击感觉车辆很少，也不激烈……追击范围要大」). What made it tame: a new star came with no police in sight (they spawn out of view), so the first line-of-sight check flipped the chase to "searching" round the crime and a player driving on lost them in ~14 s without meeting one; 2-7 cars; and a cop that did see you drove straight at you at 130 km/h through the kerbside trees. Now (`police/index.ts`): **dispatch knows where you are** for `KNOWN_ON_STAR` (8 + 2/star s) after each new star and 3 s after each sighting, so the cars are sent to you and ducking behind one block does not start a search (the underground car park still cuts it at once). Cars per star `COUNT` 3/5/8/11/14 (x0.65 on low), arriving every `SPAWN_GAP` (1.6 s at one star to 0.45 at five); while they can see you, up to half are **interceptors** spawned 150-330 m *ahead* of where you are going, routed to the point `aheadOfPlayer` (your road, 5 s on), who turn into chasers once within 60 m or passed. Chasers left > 250 m behind and out of view are recycled to come in again ahead. Tactics (`Pursuit`): one star boxes in; two stars **PIT** (line up on the rear quarter a car's width out, then turn into it); three and up half PIT and half ram, and a ram drops back 0.9 s after it connects (a hit, not a push); every approach is capped by braking distance (+8 m/s on a ram), and they go straight at you only within 45 m - further out they follow the road route, which now brakes for corners out to 110 m (from 130 km/h a bend needs 75 m). A chaser behind on a straight gets a push (`Vehicle.boost`, up to ~8 m/s²). Engines 1.55x the taxi (230 km/h). Three stars: roadblocks (three cars from four stars, every 20-28 s) and **the helicopter** (`police/Heli.ts`, modelled in Blender since 2026-09-27: `scripts/blender/vehicles/heli.py` - a light twin in the manner of an EC135, rounded fuselage with the wrap-round glazing, cowling and exhausts, a slim boom to a fenestron under a blue fin, stabiliser with end plates, skids, the searchlight pod under the nose, white with a blue band and 警察 POLICE on the boom; groups hull / rotor / fan converted by `scripts/vehicles/import.mjs ... --group` into `police/heli.json`, 6.2k triangles, the rotor and fan moved to their pivots and spun as before; `node .scratch/heli/heliclose.mjs 3 20 high outDir` runs a three-star chase and shoots it from three sides; kinematic, 58 m up and 26 m over any roof by a downward ray, orbits 30 m off your shoulder; its 170 m line of sight counts as the police seeing you; out of sight it circles the search area sweeping a figure-of-eight; searchlight = a soft cone shader that stops 14 m short of the target plus an additive ground pool - a cone reaching the ground washed out the whole frame when the camera sat inside it). Four stars: **特警** black SUVs (`SWAT`, own `CarKit`, 2 then 4) that always ram. Police cars take damage like the player's (knocks under 6 m/s free, at most 50 a crash, 150 hp, SUV 240); wrecked or flipped 2.5 s they stop and pour black smoke, and one wrecked within 25 m of the player emits `police:wrecked` (+0.3 heat, 撞毁警车 300 in the street combo). Search circle 120 + 55/star m (175-395), lost after `evadeTime` 7 + 3.5/star s outside it; sight 80 + 18/star m; a long visible chase climbs a star every 90 s up to four. Traffic pulls over to the kerb and crawls for 2.5 s when a siren comes up behind or at it (`AiDriver.yieldT`, `lineStats.yields`), police are flashing arrows on the radar and map (the heli too), and audio adds a second siren for the second-nearest car and the rotor (`WantedApi.heliDistance`). `node .scratch/chaseprobe.mjs [levels] [seconds] [quality] [shotDir]` drives a real 2 km route from the spawn with the pilot, busting off, and prints the cars, how many within 60/150 m, interceptors, wrecks, the heli, traffic pulled over and ms/step (`COPS=1` lists every car, `PARAMS=tod=22`; with a shotDir it also shoots the heli from the street): high, five stars, 18 cars with 9.5 within 150 m, police 0.29 ms/step; `m4probe`'s bust still lands at 10 s, but its evade step (teleport 900 m and sit in the open) now keeps the stars, by design - `underprobe` hides at two stars in 14 s.
