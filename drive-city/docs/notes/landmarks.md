# Landmarks: pipeline, add-on, every Blender landmark

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- Pitfall (landmarks): a landmark's terraces, steps and bridges are geometry with no collider of their own, so anyone standing on one used to stand on the ground *under* it and look buried. `placeLandmarks` (city/index.ts) builds trimesh colliders from the stone meshes (`marble`, `paving`, `granite`, `stone`), preferring the far LOD. `?nostone` skips them, which is how their cost was measured (nil: 15 colliders, no change to the step).
- Pitfall (landmark stairs, 2026-09-23, 「我走天坛的楼梯上不去，人就埋在台阶里了」): the stone trimesh above is taken from the far LOD, and the kit's `terrace`/`polyTerrace` build their stair flights on the detail LOD only, while the tiers are solid drums or boxes besides - so every flight of 祈年殿, 圜丘, 皇穹宇 and 太和殿 was a picture: the feet walked the ground under the treads and stopped at the tier's face. Both terraces now take `colliders` in their spec and push one walk-only ramp hull per flight (`stairWedge` in `kit/hall.ts`, the villa's trick: steps as boxes are standable but not walkable, and a ramp a person can climb is one a car can drive, so `walkOnly`); a landmark gets them by handing its collider list to the detail build. `landmarks/Stairs.test.ts` rides a ray up every ramp of 圜丘, the last kit landmark with stairs (祈年殿 and 太和殿 are Blender landmarks now, their ramps WALK_ hulls; the kit's polygon-terrace path has no user left), and `node .scratch/templewalk.mjs [S|N|E|W|taihedian]` walks the real character up them tier by tier. A new landmark with stairs must pass `colliders`, or its stairs are scenery. **And a car stopped inside the steps** (2026-09-27, found on 人民英雄纪念碑): it passed through the walk-only ramp and met the tier's face behind it, its nose buried in the drawn flight - every walk-only stair in the city (the kit's, the villa's, every Blender `WALK_`). `city/landmarks/CarStops.ts` `carStops(colliders)` derives a car-only block per walk-only ramp (`ColliderSpec.carOnly`, group `groups(CG.WORLD, CG.CAR)`: the ramp's plan, a `CAR_STOP` 0.9 m face at its foot rising with the ramp to its top; a walk-only box is its own block), and `placeLandmarks` adds them - nothing to re-export. People never meet it; camera and police sight rays (CAR group) do, over the lowest steps. `CarStops.test.ts` drives the real Vehicle at a flight with and without the block (without, the nose reaches the tier face) and checks 圜丘's flights each get one; `Gate.test.ts` builds them too. The villa's garage steps get one at x -15.8, clear of the parking spot (`homedrive.mjs` still parks).
- Landmarks from Blender (2026-09-27, `city/landmarks/glb/`): `node scripts/landmarks/import.mjs model.glb --id <id> --zh .. --en .. --lat .. --lon .. [--heading deg] [--at x,z] [--far m] [--tex px] [--dry]` inspects the export (footprint, height, triangles, materials, colliders, warnings: wrong scale, origin off the ground, no colliders, transmission), optimises it (dedup, prune, webp textures via sharp, meshopt with 16-bit positions) into `public/models/landmarks/<id>.glb` and writes `src/city/landmarks/glb/<id>.meta.json`; `landmarks/index.ts` registers every meta by `import.meta.glob`, so nothing else is edited (re-import with just the file and `--id` keeps name and place). The boot uses only the meta: `LandmarkDef.load` (additive) makes `build` a placeholder carrying footprint/clear/height, and `placeLandmarks` places the loaded model and its colliders when it arrives (`world.landmarksLoaded`; `?glb=0` leaves them out). Blender conventions (object or parent-empty names; `Glb.ts`'s header is the reference, `scripts/landmarks/lib.mjs` must match it): +X east, +Y north, metres, origin on the ground; `COL_` solid (a yaw-only box becomes a box collider, anything else its hull), `WALK_` people only (stair ramps), `COLMESH_` trimesh (new `ColliderSpec` kind), `LOD1_` far level (else the far level shares the detail's geometry), `FOOTPRINT` (several are separate pieces), `CLEAR_`; material custom properties `wet`, `glow` (flood/lamp/none), `glowColor`, `emit: "night"`. Visible meshes are baked and merged per material into one draw call per level, a mesh linked 6+ times becomes an InstancedMesh, and merged meshes are named `glb <material>` so `placeLandmarks`' stone-trimesh-by-name never picks them up. `Glb.test.ts` builds a Blender-shaped document in memory and runs it through inspect, optimise, GLTFLoader and `buildGlbModel` (roles, box/hull/trimesh, merging, instancing, LOD, night/wet hooks). `landmarks.html?id=<id>` previews one (the gallery awaits `load`). Probes: `node .scratch/mkdemo.mjs out.glb` writes a demo pavilion shaped like a Blender export; `[AXIS=x] START=40 node .scratch/glbprobe.mjs <id> [out.png] ["x,y,z" "x,y,z"]` waits for it in the real city, ray-casts its colliders for a person and a car, drives into it and screenshots.
- Blender add-on (2026-09-27, `scripts/blender/bcity_landmark/`, sidebar N > B城): scene settings (id, names, lat/lon as strings - a Blender float rounds a latitude to half a metre - or game x,z, heading, far distance, texture cap, repo and node paths, auto-found from the .blend's folders or the last run, remembered in Blender's config folder), buttons that rename the selection to a role (and draw markers as coloured wire, not rendered), box / hull colliders from the selection, a WALK_ stair ramp from a stair's box (`rise` axis, warns over 50°), FOOTPRINT from the visible meshes' hull, a CLEAR_ zone at the cursor, hide/show helpers (the exporter writes hidden objects), the material's `wet`/`glow`/`glowColor`/`emit` as RNA properties with get/set over the plain custom properties (RNA-registered properties are not exported as extras, plain ones are), a check (units, origin, colliders, triangles, textures, transmission, node >= 22.18) and 导出到游戏: glTF export with extras, applied modifiers and collections as nodes (so a collection named COL or LOD1 marks everything in it; kwargs are filtered by what the exporter has), then `import.mjs`, its output in the text block 「B城导入日志」. `npm run blender-addon` zips it for Extensions > Install from Disk (Blender 4.2+; files at the zip root, as Blender's own `extension build` makes it). Tested headlessly with the `bpy` wheel (Python 3.11, `pip install bpy`): `<venv>/bin/python .scratch/blender/addontest.py [--export] [--keep out.blend]` builds a pavilion, presses every button, draws every panel against a layout that checks property, operator and icon names, and exports (dry unless `--export`; it points `BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES` at a temp folder, or the add-on's config lands in ~/Library/Application Support/Blender).
