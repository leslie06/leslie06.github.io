# 我家 (the villa) and 北京欢乐谷

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- `park/` — 北京欢乐谷 at its real coordinates (39.8669 N, 116.4881 E) on 小武基路. `Osm.ts` is generated by `node scripts/park/extract.mjs` from the OSM cache: the real boundary (956 x 570 m), three lakes, 47 footpaths and the 34 attractions OSM names, in park-local metres. `Layout.ts` holds every position (rides sit on their real attraction points); `Rides.ts` builds the static park and the movers; `Props.ts` fills the other 30-odd attraction points with massing chosen from each name, and plants trees along the real paths - since 2026-09-29 not as its own cones but handed to the city's tree pools (`LandmarkModel.trees`, [x, z, species, scale] in the local frame; `placeLandmarks` turns them to world metres and `CityStreamer` adds them to the tile they stand on: the Blender street trees' models, LOD, shadows and trunk colliders); the rock (`Rides.mountain`) wears a procedural bedded sandstone (`world/Textures.ts` `sandstone`: warped beds with cracks, joints, lichen and streaks, colour and bump, a repeat every 12 m of its metre UVs); `landmark.ts` registers the lot as a landmark (85k tris, 17 draw calls, 76 colliders; far LOD 6.3k/3). Three rides are boardable on foot with F: 水晶神翼 (a 656 m flying coaster with a vertical loop round the 70 m 水晶圣城), 能量风暴 (giant frisbee) and 莓饼海盗船. A ride is kinematic - `advanceTrain` integrates gravity along the spline, the chain lift and brake run override it - and while riding, `CameraApi.override` takes the camera and `PlayerApi.setRiding` parks the character in the seat. `Rides.test.ts` covers track length, crest height, the inversion, frame continuity, clearing the rock, a lap that always comes home and the early get-off; `PROFILE=1 npx vitest run src/park/Trace.test.ts` writes a metre-by-metre lap trace to `.scratch/park/profile.txt`.
- Pitfall (coaster track): the rails need a frame that is parallel-transported along the spline, not a Frenet frame (which flips at every inflection). Two traps followed: a closed circuit does not bring `up` back to where it started, so the residual twist has to be unwound evenly along the lap or the track flips over between the last sample and the first; and banking computed from the full 3-D curvature saturates and changes sign inside the loop, so it is taken from the plan-view turn alone and smoothed. A loop whose top is higher than the lift crest strands the train at the speed floor - the profile test catches it.
- `home/` — 我家, the player's villa: a landmark plus one moving part. The real 中央别墅区 is in 顺义, 18 km past the map's north edge and 8 km past the edge of the ground plane itself, so the plot sits in the city instead — 96 x 50 m of open ground at local (4520, 487), 300 m from the spawn, off 恒惠路 behind 国贸, chosen by searching the tiles for a road-free rectangle and confirmed to hold no buildings, street trees or lamps (the nearest tree is 1.8 m outside the south wall and hangs over it; the drive meets 恒惠路's kerb at x 4453.8). `Layout.ts` holds every position **and the upper floor's room plan and wall/door table** (`UPPER_ROOM`, `UPPER_WALL`, `BEDROOMS`, `BED`), `Furniture.ts` the soft furnishings every room is dressed from (each piece built at the local origin, placed with `P.at`), `Villa.ts` the model, rebuilt 2026-09-12 from reference photographs of a Californian hillside modernist house: a long upper bar clad in pale timber behind dark horizontal louvre screens, cantilevered 3 m south over a glass ground floor so the terrace sits in deep shade; a glass pavilion projecting at the east end; a board-formed concrete entry wall with a tall pivot door over three broad steps and a black reflecting pool beside it; an infinity pool flush with the stone terrace; inside, a curved timber stair against a rough stone wall, a great room under a timber plank ceiling with a stone fireplace wall, formal dining, a study with a black marble fireplace, and upstairs nine rooms off a gallery: a master suite (bedroom on the corner glass, dressing room, ensuite), two more bedrooms sharing a bathroom, and a media room. Every bedroom takes the south glass and the balcony; the rooms with no view to lose take the north. There is no terrain here, so the hillside is a 1.05 m stone plinth with the lawn stepping down from it behind curved retaining walls, `landmark.ts` registers it — which is also what puts 我家 on the map and in the GPS search, since `nav/` builds its pins from `LANDMARKS`. `index.ts` owns the roller garage door (opens within 16 m of the player, shuts past 22 m, collider switched off once a third open) and the safehouse: stopping the car inside the garage banks the day — cash saved, wanted cleared, `PlayerApi.heal` back to 100, the lot written to `drivecity.home.v1`. It is filtered out of `missions/`'s fare destinations, because nobody hails a taxi to your own garage. Poses `home_*`; `Villa.test.ts` covers the clearances and `Stair.test.ts` walks the flight in a real Rapier world. Cheap for what it is: 43.5k triangles in 31 draw calls, far LOD 1.7k in 7, and 144 colliders (122 boxes plus 22 hulls: one per stair tread, and the two walk-only wedges you climb to reach the plinth) - `PROFILE=1 npx vitest run src/home/Villa.test.ts` rewrites the figures to `.scratch/home/stats.txt`.
- Pitfalls (a landmark you can walk inside): `ColliderSpec` boxes carry `yaw` but no pitch, so a stair or ramp has to be a `kind: 'hull'` wedge — the villa's flight is one hull at 29°, inside `OnFoot`'s 50° slope limit and 0.4 m autostep, which is also why its floor sits 0.45 m up behind two 0.15 m steps. `placeLandmarks` builds trimesh colliders from any mesh named `marble|paving|granite|stone` and prefers the **far** LOD, so a landmark wanting explicit colliders must avoid those material keys (every villa key starts with `v`). A footprint only deletes OSM *buildings*: `tileWorker` passes trees and lamps straight through, so a new landmark must be sited clear of them or they grow through its roof (or declare a `clear` zone, below). And `Parts.build` throws on a key with no material while `placeLandmarks` only `console.warn`s, so a typo makes the whole building silently absent — `villaMaterials` is typed `Record<VillaKey, Material>` so tsc catches it instead. Two more, both caught only by screenshotting it: the kit's `facadeMaterial` curtain wall is **opaque** (a MeshStandardMaterial that draws its mullions in the shader), which is the wrong trade for a glass house - you could not see the garden from the sofa, nor the sofa from the garden - so the villa's glazing is one double-sided transparent pane per bay (`depthWrite: false`, and drawn once: transparent geometry must not be drawn twice) with the frame as separate solid mullion boxes. And a flat roof's fascia has to be a ring of four thin boxes - a single box spanning the whole roof hides `vRoof` completely and reads as a black hole from the air. A room's ceiling must be tiled around its stairwell too: one quad over the whole footprint capped the void, so the stair ran into the ceiling and read as leading nowhere - `Villa.test.ts` now asserts the flight comes up inside VOID. The kit's `stairFlight` is a palace-terrace stair with solid 0.6 m side stringers, which indoors hides every tread and reads as a ramp, so the villa draws its own. Two more from rebuilding it as a helix: a `hull` collider is convex, and a spiral is not, so a curved stair needs **one hull per tread** — chunking three treads into a hull makes its outer edge a chord, and the arc bulges 0.22 m outside that chord at mid span, so the outer quarter of every third tread was a hole with the whole 2.3 m drop below it (at one tread per hull the chord error is 1.4 cm, which a +0.03 m on the radii covers); and a stair void that reaches the edge of the slab it rises through leaves a zero-width strip in the slab tiling, which must be skipped or it becomes a degenerate polygon and a zero-half collider. A curved stair has two more traps that a green test suite happily hid until the player tried to walk up it: tread depth must follow the **arc**, not be a constant (a 0.32 m tread against a 0.61 m spacing at the outer radius left gaps you fell through, climbable only hugging the inside), and the flight has to arrive on a landing - ours stopped 1.43 m short of any floor, in mid-void. Both now have assertions, and `node .scratch/stairwalk.mjs` walks the foot capsule up tread by tread and prints the drop at each, because a screenshot cannot show that a stair is climbable. **And the landing must not be a slab over the flight below it** (2026-09-13): `LANDING` was a 0.25 m slab at floor level sitting directly over treads 13-18, and `VOID` was a hand-typed rectangle that stopped at z 0.9 while the helix sweeps to 1.36 — so the climb ended at 3.5 m against the underside of a floor (「这楼梯和人一样高」) with six treads sealed inside it, and the top treads ran under the main slab as well. The landing is now paving laid *on* the slab, the void is sized from the sweep with clearance, and the last tread laps onto the floor. Three more from the same report: the drawn balustrade and the drawn spine wall had no colliders at all, so both edges of a 3.8 m stair were open air (the spine now runs 0.95 m past the first floor as the stairwell's parapet, and the void rail carries a collider too); the treads had no risers and read as twenty floating planks; and none of it is provable by arithmetic — `Stair.test.ts` builds the villa's colliders exactly as `placeLandmarks` does and ray-casts at every tread, across its full width, for what is underfoot and what is overhead. Cast those rays from just above the feet, never from roof height: over a stairwell the first thing a downward ray meets is the rail or the slab the flight passes under, which reads as a floor 3 m above the tread you are standing on. And the one that made the house read as a loft: the upper floor declared three bedrooms but built the dividing walls only where two rooms happened to share an x edge, so most of them were never built and the whole floor was one open space with beds standing about in it. The plan is now data (`UPPER_WALL`, each wall with its doorway) and `Villa.test.ts` walks those doors from the stair landing: every room must be reachable, the rooms must tile the bar exactly with no overlap, and nothing - bed, wardrobe, island - may stand in a doorway. And the one that left every room upstairs unreachable while that door test was green (2026-09-17, 「楼梯和去房间的路是断的」): **a door graph proves topology, not width**. The flight swung round the east of its centre and came off heading west, onto a landing on the far side of the stairwell from the gallery portal; the only way round was the 0.35 m between the void rail and the gallery wall, narrower than the 0.56 m capsule. The stair is now mirrored (`STAIR.hand = -1`; every consumer goes through `stairPoint` / `stairAngle` in `Layout.ts`, and a tread's riser follows `hand` because local +Z is only uphill for one handedness): it starts at the north heading west and comes off at the south heading east, with `LANDING` between the void and the portal. `Stair.test.ts` sweeps a capsule-radius ball from the top tread through the portal (with a control cast that must hit the void rail, so a sweep that can hit nothing does not pass for an open path), and `node .scratch/upstairswalk.mjs` walks the real `OnFoot` controller from the foot of the stair into bed2 and the master (it has to call `engine.input.poll()` itself: the frame loop polls, `stepFixed` does not). Probes must import `/src/home/Layout.ts` in the page rather than copy the stair's numbers - two had stale copies. Same report, the bedrooms: every bed stood head-on to the north wall dead centre and every door was dead centre in that same wall, so you walked in behind the headboard. Beds now stand on a side wall (`BED` in `Layout.ts`, bed2/bed3 back to back on their shared wall, the master on its blind west wall facing the corner glass) with the door at the foot end of the room; `Villa.test.ts` asserts the whole doorway is past the foot. The ground-floor ceiling is also tiled round `VOID` now - it was one quad per room and capped the stairwell from below. And the one that made the house unreachable on foot: **a flight of steps built as stacked boxes is standable but not walkable**. Dropping the foot capsule onto the entry steps landed on every tread (0.39 / 0.74 / 1.09), yet walking into them just slid along the face and stopped dead - the character controller would not autostep a 0.32 m riser even though `enableAutostep(0.4, 0.15)` says it should. Every climb therefore carries a `hull` wedge under the step geometry (`wedge()` in `Villa.ts`), the same trick the curved stair uses, because `ColliderSpec` boxes have no pitch. Two flights were wrong at once and the second was hidden behind the first: the entry steps and the garage steps were both built *inside* the plinth's solid 1.05 m collider (against the door at x -11.3..-8, and east of the garage wall), so the approach was one sheer face and `.scratch/villawalk.mjs` stuck the player on the court at x = -14.3. `.scratch/villawalk.mjs` walks in from the drive and `.scratch/entrysteps.mjs` drop-tests each tread; the climb is now a test (`can be walked onto`), which samples hulls as well as boxes - sampling only boxes would pass a house with no ramp at all. The sting in the tail: **a ramp gentle enough to walk up is gentle enough to drive up**. The taxi went straight up the new entry wedge and parked on the terrace (`.scratch/villacarramp.mjs`), and narrowing the ramp does not help - a car rides on its wheels, track 1.54 m, so anything wide enough to read as a stair carries it. The wedges are therefore `walkOnly` (an additive flag on `ColliderSpec` that `placeLandmarks` turns into `groups(CG.WORLD, CG.ALL & ~CG.CAR)`): the wheel ray is `groups(CG.CAR, CG.WORLD)`, so dropping CAR from the collider's filter makes the ray miss it and the chassis pass through to stop against the plinth face, while `CG.PED` stays in the filter and the player still walks up.
- Pitfall (a landmark's drive, 2026-09-21, 「每次把车开到别墅门口就卡住」): the plot was surveyed clear of trees and lamps, but the drive runs 18 m beyond it (`DRIVE.kerb`, the landmark's `extras`) across 恒惠路's pavement, which OSM plants every 9 m. A street tree stood at local (-64.7, -10.6), 0.6 m off the drive's centre line, with a lamp at (-66.0, -13.6) on its north edge, 2.6 m apart: a car turning in from the north wedged between them at full throttle, while one from the south slipped past through the 3.5 m gap. `LandmarkModel.clear` (additive, passed through `assemble`, `placeLandmarks`, `CityStreamer` and the tile worker) is a list of local polygons the city keeps free of street trees, lamps and kerbside furniture - `city/Clear.ts` filters them in the worker, so the drawn instances and the colliders go together. The villa declares `DRIVE_CLEAR` (the kerb stretch, 3 m wider each side because a car turning in cuts the corner). It hid because **every home probe teleported the car** to x -52 or further in, east of the tree line: `node .scratch/homedrive.mjs [speed] [startZ|drive]` now really drives it off 恒惠路, through the gate and into the garage, and prints what it is touching if it stops (from the north at 8 and 11 m/s and from the south at 8 it parks; at 14 m/s the pilot takes the 12 m corner at 51 km/h, runs 9 m wide and meets the boundary wall - that is the pilot, not the drive). `node .scratch/homeobstacles.mjs` lists every tree and lamp collider in the corridor, `Villa.test.ts` reads the real tile (`t_17_1.json`) and asserts none is within a car's half width of the paving, with a control that the raw tile does have one, and the `home_drive` pose shows the mouth from the road. Trap while verifying: in zsh `for a in "8 -60"; do node probe.mjs $a` passes ONE argument, so speed was NaN, the car coasted to a halt on the road and five runs in a row reported STUCK with `throttle=NaN`; the probe now rejects non-numeric arguments.
- Pitfall (the gate, 2026-09-23, found while chasing 「我把车开到家门口就卡住了」- which turned out to be the traffic hang below, not this): nobody arrives along the drive's axis off 恒惠路 the way every home probe did - the block round the plot is an OSM parking lot and two pitches, flat drivable paving with no colliders, and the taxi in the screenshot was crossing it diagonally from the south-west at 20 km/h (position recovered by rendering candidates with `node .scratch/homeview.mjs car -58 4 145` until the walls lined up). With straight walls and the piers on the plot line, any line that grazes the wall beside the opening puts the nose into the inside corner between the wall's face and the pier's face: the throttle only spins the wheels (the smoke in the screenshot), sliding on along the wall is stopped by the pier, and only reverse frees it - a car pushed into any wall at 45° holds (its tyres' lateral grip, not the wall's friction), so the corner is the trap, not the wall. The mouth is now splayed (`GATE.splay`, `gateWing`, `gateMouth`, `driveInPoly` in `Layout.ts`): the last 4 m of wall each side turn 45° away from the drive and the piers stand at the wings' inner ends, turned with them so their faces continue the wing's, and the paving flares to fill the mouth (15.6 m on the plot line, 7.6 between the wings). `Gate.test.ts` drives the real Vehicle with the PathPilot through the villa's real colliders in a Rapier world along eight plaza lines; with the straight gate three of them stopped at the piers (the screenshot's, its mirror from the north-west, and one up the plot wall), with the splay all reach the court. `node .scratch/homediag.mjs [speed] [startX] [startZ] [heading|gate] [out.png]` drives such a line in the browser and prints what the car is touching when it stops - only pairs with a real contact manifold: the tile's building trimesh is broad-phase paired with everything on the tile and read as "touching" at d=0 the whole way in, which cost an hour. The `home_gate` pose shows the mouth from the plaza.
- Home probe: `node .scratch/homeprobe.mjs` (dev server up) drives the villa end to end and prints what happened: the garage door shut at 300 m and still shut on the drive 26 m out but open inside, parking as the save (health 40 -> 100, wanted 2 -> 0, `drivecity.home.v1` written with the car's body and colours), the car resting at the parking spot rather than fighting a collider, and the feet landing on the first floor (4.89 against a 4.85 slab), the ground floor (1.09/1.05) and the lawn (0.04/0.03) instead of falling through.
