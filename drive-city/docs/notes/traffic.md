# Traffic, signals, trains, parking, distant traffic

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- `traffic/` — `LaneGraph` (right-hand traffic), `Signals` (two-phase clusters; `greenLeft` lets pedestrians cross with the parallel green), `AiDriver` (pure pursuit + IDM), `CarKit` (all cars of one livery as instances, ~20 draw calls). Also holds parked cars: `takeCar/parkCar` implement carjacking.
- Traffic lights (every junction since 2026-09-21, 「在路口都装上红绿灯，汽车和行人都要遵守」): OSM marks 463 signal nodes here, and the first inference (two roads of tertiary class or better crossing on different axes, now `?lights=main`) still left 2,633 of the city's 3,591 street junctions dark - drivers went through them without looking and pedestrians crossed on a gap. `Signals` now lights any node where three or more street arms meet and do not all run the same way (`junctionKind`): 2,096 junctions (475 before), 6,317 approaches, built in 29 ms at boot. Not junctions: service roads and living streets (a driveway's mouth), `_link` ramps where they meet a trunk or motorway (they merge), anything on a motorway, and a fork. Two plans: MAJOR (30 s main road / 16 s side street, cycle 56) wherever a road of tertiary class or better is involved, MINOR (15/12, cycle 37) where only lanes meet (840 junctions) - a 56 s cycle held a hutong car up to 40 s at every corner. `greenSpan(c, phase)` and `cycleOf(c)` are per junction. **Clustering trap:** nodes used to merge into one clock at 45 m single-linkage, but junctions in this city are a median 30 m apart, so with every junction lit whole blocks chain into one; now signal nodes of two main crossings join at 45 m, anything else at 25 m, and nothing joins more than 60 m from the cluster's most important node (largest cluster 73 m across, was 101). The cost, measured with `.scratch/order.mjs` on the same build (`lights=main` against the default; the probe is deterministic, run twice bit-identical): at the spawn arterials 20.8 -> 20.1 km/h, side streets 25.1 -> 18.4; in the lanes of 金鱼池 (`AT=330,2257`) arterials 28.9 -> 28.7, side streets 27.6 -> 22.1. Obedience is counted, not assumed: `traffic.lineStats` counts stop lines crossed and how many on red, `people.debug.stats()` counts crossings started at a light and how many on red - 0 of 50 and 0 of 12 at the spawn, 0 of 60 and 0 of 13 in the lanes; a mutant that ignored red and gave up after 5 s scored 20 of 58 and 5 of 12, so the counters are live. `src/traffic/Order.test.ts` covers which junctions are lit (lanes, a lane into a main road, driveways, ramps, a motorway, a fork, close pairs sharing a clock, a chain of close junctions not merging), that every real junction of ordinary streets is lit, and that a driver stops at the line on red and goes on green. **And the lamps were never visible**: `SignalHeads` put each lamp 0.17 m proud of the housing's centre, right for a 0.32 m deep box, but the box is 0.42 m deep along the traffic it faces, so every lamp in the city sat 4 cm inside it and no light was ever seen to change - `.scratch/lights/lamps.mjs [out.png] [seconds]` prints the nearest heads' lamp positions, facing and colour, and shoots one from 6 m. The heads are now the nearest 240 approaches (sorted: the first 160 the grid listed could leave the junction in front of you dark), down to 8 m long. Once visible, the lit colours (HDR 3.2-3.6) turned out bleached by AgX - red read as salmon, green as mint - so they peak under ~1.8 with one channel dominant. The `traffic_lane_junction` pose shows a short-cycle junction in 金鱼池 at eye height.
- Pitfall (the game hangs, 2026-09-23, 「我把车开到家门口就卡住了……游戏死机了」): 「卡住」 meant the whole game, and every home probe missed it because they ran `stepFixed` only, or `tick` only after arriving. `.scratch/homefreeze.mjs [url] [quality] [sw|north|south|tree|none]` drives to the villa with the REAL frame loop, times every system call of every tick, and when a chunk of frames does not return it pauses V8 through CDP (`Debugger.pause`) and prints the call stack - the only way to name an infinite loop. It sat in `AiDriver.update` -> `fill`: the OSM network has 74 one-way roads that end with no way on (the west end of 景恒街 is 320 m from the villa's gate, inside the 330 m traffic radius, which is why home was where it always happened), and `LaneGraph.next` answered such an end with the link itself, so the driver's advance loop (`s > len - 0.3`) took the same link again, projected the car onto its end again, and spun forever with the main thread. Now `next` returns -1 there (the reverse link at a two-way end, as before), `fill` stops short, the driver brakes for the end of the road as for a red light and goes `lost` (the pool recycles it out of view), the advance loop is capped at 6 hops whatever the data says, and `spawn` skips such links (`endsAfter`). `DeadEnd.test.ts` covers all of it and checks the real network: no link answers with itself except a closed ring (8 loop roads, driven round again by design). Two traps from the hunt: `world.contactPairsWith` reports broad-phase pairs, so a probe that prints "touching" must check the manifold has contacts (the tile's building trimesh otherwise reads as touched at d=0 the whole way); and in headless Chromium `renderFrame` takes ~100 ms once per 30 frames regardless - that is the GPU sync, not the game.
- The city in motion (2026-10-03, 「让城市动起来」). **Trains** (`city/visual/Trains.ts`, system `trains`, `?trains=0`): `railRuns` joins the railway tracks (`rail.json`, decoded) end to end into runs a train can travel - at each end the straightest continuation within 25 degrees of the same kind, yards and sidings left out, cut at every segment set in a road (a train across a road on the level would be OSM's error here, so there are no crossing barriers: nothing would ever close them) - 82 runs of 1.2 km+ (74 main line, 8 subway, the longest 9 km); one train per 2.5 km of run, an 8-car EMU (white, blue stripe, pantograph) or a 6-car subway train (red stripe), its position a function of time running the run back and forth (a nose at both ends), so nothing is simulated far away: trains within 2 km drawn (two instanced meshes a kind, cars and noses; dark glass lit warm at night), and those within 350 m of the player's car get a kinematic box per car the car hits (tag `train`). They start with the railways, after the boot. `node .scratch/trainshot.mjs outPrefix "x,z" [n]` (`KIND=0|1`, `H`, `D` the camera's height and distance) shoots the nearest trains; `npx tsx .scratch/trains-chains.mts` lists the runs. **Buses at stops**: a bus (now 9% of traffic, was 5) looks for a shelter at its link's right-hand kerb 15-85 m ahead once a second (`AiDriver.stopAhead`, `CityStreamer.sheltersNear`), pulls 2.4 m towards the kerb, stops at it for 8-12 s and pulls out, then takes no stop for 30 s; `lineStats.busStops` counts them (`node .scratch/busprobe.mjs [s] [x,z]`, `node .scratch/busshot.mjs out.png` shoots one at its stop). **People out of the subway and over the footbridges** (`people/Walkers.ts`, system `walkers`, `?nowalkers`): from the foot of a kiosk's stair up and out of its open end and along the pavement, or the other way, and over a footbridge stair - deck - stair, either way; spawned among the entrances and bridges within 160 m of the camera, up to 12/24/30 by tier in the crowd's room left over, walked at a stroll and dropped at the path's end; drawn through the crowd after the pedestrians. `node .scratch/walkshot.mjs out.png "from>to" [s]`. Cost: low +3 calls / +0.3 ms, high +16 / +0.85 ms. **Smaller boot** (same day): the game fetches `network.pack.json` (`city/NetPack.ts`: decimetre deltas, class and name tables, parallel arrays; `scripts/city/netpack.mts` packs it at the end of the build and checks the round trip on every value; `loadNetwork()` falls back to `network.json`, which the build scripts and tests still read): 1.70 -> 0.97 MB gzipped; `skyline.json` is written packed (sorted by x, integer deltas; `unpackSkyline`): 0.48 -> 0.36 MB. Real game at 500 KB/s: 17.3 -> 15.5 s to ready, 6.4 -> 5.7 MB before it.
- Parked cars along the kerbs (2026-10-11, 「街道太空，不像真实的街道场景」 - the first item of a plan: kerbside parking, distant traffic, shop fronts, more people, road detail). Measured first (`node .scratch/emptystreet.mjs outPrefix [quality]`, PARAMS=...: traffic within 60/150 m, people within 30/60 m, chase and eye shots): at the spawn low had 3/10 cars and 0/1 people, and no street had a parked car. **Where**: `traffic/Lanes.ts` (shared by the lane graph and the tile worker, so both agree) - `laneLayout`/`laneOffsetOf` (moved out of LaneGraph) and `parkingLine(cls, w, oneway, lanes)`: residential, unclassified and two-way tertiary streets with a pavement get a strip `PARK_W` 1.85 m wide on the right of travel (both sides of a two-way street), its inner edge `GAP` 0.3 m outside the outermost lane's traffic (0.92 half width), against the kerb where the road has room (a 12 m tertiary) or half up on the pavement (a 7 m residential street: 0.97 m up), never more than 1.05 m up nor leaving under 1.4 m to walk; main roads, one-way tertiaries (mostly a main road's 辅路: 建国门外大街's) and narrow lanes get none. `Link.kerbR` (where drivers pulling over and scooter riders keep inside: the strip's inner edge, else hw) and `Link.parkOut` (how far up the pavement; walkers keep `parkOut + 0.4` in from the kerb, `people/` `inFrom`). **Placement** (`city/visual/Parking.ts` `placeParking`, in the tile worker after the furniture, records [x, z, heading, body, colour] in `Furniture.park`; 0.14 ms a tile, ~185k cars on the map, 30 a tile): cars nose to tail with 0.5-1.6 m gaps, saloon/SUV/hatch/MPV 44/28/18/10%, a street's occupancy by class (residential 0.88) times its own mood, 12% of sides empty, 8% facing the wrong way; not on decks, ramps or sunk stretches (±8 m), at zebras (±7), a bus stop's side 45 m before and 12 after it (the bus pulls in), nor near junctions and bends - **on the way out of a junction 20 m past the crossing road's pavement, on the way in 5 m** (cars leaving a turn swing wide towards the kerb and were still finding their lane 20 m on: a traffic SUV turning onto 培新街 clipped the first car), and the same round any bend over 30 degrees, **including where two ways meet end to end at an angle** (a road turning a corner is often two OSM ways, no junction flag: 4374,15); no corner in another carriageway (the neighbours' via `ctx`), none over a tree, lamp, shared bike or bin, `dropParked` against the clear zones (every corner, not just the middle: shortcut mouths, ramp runs, the villa's drive). **Runtime** (`parking/`, system `parking`, after traffic; `?parked=0` off): the streamer tells it when a tile's colliders come and go (`CityStreamer.onParked`, `parkedNow()`), and 0.6 s later each car is checked against the static colliders (a box 0.25-1.5 m up; kiosks, walls, sign posts, footbridge stairs, houses at a hutong's kerb: ~3% rejected) and kept as two fixed boxes (the spec's chassis, tag `parked`) and an instance: within `NEAR` 60 m in traffic's own kits (`TrafficApi.addExternalDraw`, `PARKED_DRAW` 64 extra slots a body; `CarKit.setStill` draws a car at rest with every lamp off - `instLamp.w`, so traffic's headlights at night leave them dark), further out to 190/250/300 m by tier in the far kits (`CarKit` `{ far: true }`: paint and wheels only, simplified to ~6%, no shadows, 2 calls a body); on low hatchbacks are drawn as saloons and MPVs as SUVs. **A car about to be hit becomes a Vehicle** (`promote`): every fixed step each moving car (traffic with a 0.08 m margin, the player's and the police 0.5) has its footprint at now, +0.2 and +0.4 s tested against the parked boxes (`boxesMeet`, separating axes - a first cut with circles promoted every car traffic drove past); the player on foot promotes the nearest within 5.5 m, so F takes it (`addExternalCars`: `take` hands over the Vehicle). Left untouched it turns back into an instance once nobody is within 24 m for 1.5 s; knocked more than 0.25 m it stays a Vehicle until out of drawing range and its place stays empty; `blocking()` (traffic's leader test, `ExternalCars.blocking`) is only those knocked 0.8 m or 0.3 rad out of place - one nudged along the kerb otherwise held up the traffic behind it. 14 Vehicles at most. `ParkingApi.clearAround(x, z, r, hold)` clears the kerb for someone standing on it: the taxi hailers (120 s), the npc encounters' kerb spots, the job posts (for good: they are placed at boot, before their tiles' cars are checked). **Fixed on the way**: scooter riders spawned at `hw - 0.8`, inside the parked row (now `kerbR - 0.8`), the city's motorcycles stand `parkOut` further up the pavement, and `AiDriver.along` no longer moves an ordinary car's lane in by `kerbR - 1.3` (`Math.min(off, ...)`). Cost on 金桐路 lined both sides (`node .scratch/parking/cost.mjs quality "4449,1.3,150" "4449,1.1,60"`): high +0.7 ms, +34 calls, +257k triangles; low +0.3 ms, +18 calls, +131k. Probes: `node .scratch/parking/probe.mjs outDir [traffic|crash|jack|all]` (`AT=x,z0,z1`: 40 s of traffic beside parked rows counting the cars it knocks - 0 at five streets -, the player's car into one at 9 m/s - it and the two in front are shoved -, F on foot beside one), `node .scratch/parking/trace.mjs "camX,camZ" "x,z" s` traces the traffic passing a point, `node .scratch/parking/real.mjs out.png [params] [s]` the real loop from the title. `Parking.test.ts` checks the strip and a real tile (no car in a lane, another road or over a tree). - Distant traffic (2026-10-11, the second item of the 「街道太空」 plan): the physics traffic lives within 330 m (12/26/42 cars by tier), so an arterial ran empty to the horizon. `traffic/Ghosts.ts` `GhostTraffic` (owned by traffic/, `TrafficApi.ghosts`; `?ghosts=0` off, none with `notraffic`): up to 140/260/420 cars by tier from `R_IN` 220 m out to 450/600/750 m with no physics - IDM behind the car in front on their lane (other ghosts and the physics cars' drivers, `drivers` [link, s, lane, v, length] set by traffic each frame), red lights (and amber when they can stop) as a standing car at `STOP_LINE`, turns slowed as AiDriver does, `LaneGraph.next` for the way on (chosen 60 m before the end; the road ending: they stop and go). **Handover both ways**: a ghost coming within `R_IN` becomes a physics car where it is, at its speed and lane (`adoptGhost` in traffic/, same body's free pool slot; while the pool is full it drives on as a ghost and goes at 120 m, or out of view under 180), and traffic's spawn tick first lets the nearest waiting ghost take a free slot (`adoptWaiting`) before spawning one of its own; a physics car despawned for distance past 330 m becomes a ghost (`take`); physics cars treat the ghost ahead on their lane as a leader (`leaderFor`, `AiDriver.nextLink`). **Where new ones go** (`refill`, every 0.4 s, every 0.05 s for 2 s after the start or a camera jump of 150 m): links weighted by length x lanes x class density (`DENSITY` per lane-km: trunk/motorway 30, primary 26, secondary 18, tertiary 8, residential 2) x the share of points along them (every ~50 m) inside the ring, in view (out of view x `BEHIND` 0.25) and nearer (beyond 450 m down to half), scaled to the budget; out of view anywhere, in view only from `IN_VIEW_MIN` 230 m (a car seen from behind is ~7 px there). Two traps on the way: judged by its middle, a long link starting by the camera got no share and the lanes ahead stayed empty to 450 m; and with new ones in view only past 450 m, **the lanes heading away from the camera never filled** - nothing drives into them from the near side (seen from inside the lanes the ghosts that are there also hide each other: `ghostproj.mjs` projects them). When full, the furthest out-of-view ones behind the camera (> 380 m) are let go so the view gets its share. Drawn in far kits that keep their lamps (`CarKit` `{ far: true, lamps: true }`, `setStill(.., lit)`: taxi parts and brake lamps per car, headlights by the kit at night; on low only saloon/SUV/bus kits), and **at night one HDR point a car** (`ghost-lights`, `THREE.Points`, 3 px, additive: white facing the camera, red going away, brighter braking) - their lamps are under a pixel past 300 m. **Perf trap found here**: `Matrix4.toArray` / `InstancedMesh.setMatrixAt` cost 0.4 us a call in the game (polymorphic) - 30x a plain loop; `CarKit` now copies with `put16` (traffic and parked cars gain too), and `paintMetal` is memoised. Cost (`node .scratch/parking/ghostcost.mjs quality "4524,1.6,190" "4200,1.2,186"`): low +0.1 ms, +30 calls (139 ghosts); high ~+0.7 ms (414; the update itself 0.3 ms: `ghostdiag.mjs`). `order.mjs` at the spawn: speeds and red-light crossings as with `?ghosts=0`. Probes in `.scratch/parking/`: `ghostdiag.mjs [q] [s]` (counts by distance, class, road, a strip of 建国门外大街, ms per part), `ghostshot.mjs outPrefix q "from>to" ...` (`FOV=12` zooms, `DRIVE=x0,z0,x1,z1,speed` drives a line first, `PARAMS=tod=21.5`), `ghostproj.mjs`, `ghostprof.mjs`.
- 快速路中段查不到路（2026-10-17，用户要求修「东五环查不到路名」）：`city/Routes.ts`（路名、`nearest`、`ahead`）和 `traffic/LaneGraph.ts`（`near`：R 键回到路上、`VehicleApi.roadPoseNear`、交通生成、警察等几十处）的 64 m 网格**只登记每条路的顶点所在的格子**。快速路直线段的两个顶点能隔几百米，中间经过的格子里没有这条路，查询只看周围 3×3 格，于是在东五环中段查到的是 24.6 m 外一条没名字的支路（东五环本身就在 3.7 m 外）。现在和 `city/DeadEnds.ts` 一样，每段路每 24 m 取一个点，经过的格子都登记。全城普查（`node .scratch/resume/nameaudit.mjs`：沿每条有名字的路每 30 m 取一点查路名）：18.3 万个点里查不到的从 11,603 个（6.3%，最多的是南/西/东/北五环、首都机场高速、通久路、京承高速）降到 1 个；车道图每段中点用 `near(x, z, 10)` 都能查到自己。`city/Streamer.ts` 的 `netGrid`（给分块 worker 的「周边 120 m 内的路」）也是只登记顶点，没改：改了会让分块生成的结果变化（沿街店面等），要另外评估。
