# Traffic, signals, trains, parking, distant traffic

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- `traffic/` — `LaneGraph` (right-hand traffic), `Signals` (two-phase clusters; `greenLeft` lets pedestrians cross with the parallel green), `AiDriver` (pure pursuit + IDM), `CarKit` (all cars of one livery as instances, ~20 draw calls). Also holds parked cars: `takeCar/parkCar` implement carjacking.
- Traffic lights (every junction since 2026-09-21, 「在路口都装上红绿灯，汽车和行人都要遵守」): OSM marks 463 signal nodes here, and the first inference (two roads of tertiary class or better crossing on different axes, now `?lights=main`) still left 2,633 of the city's 3,591 street junctions dark - drivers went through them without looking and pedestrians crossed on a gap. `Signals` now lights any node where three or more street arms meet and do not all run the same way (`junctionKind`): 2,096 junctions (475 before), 6,317 approaches, built in 29 ms at boot. Not junctions: service roads and living streets (a driveway's mouth), `_link` ramps where they meet a trunk or motorway (they merge), anything on a motorway, and a fork. Two plans: MAJOR (30 s main road / 16 s side street, cycle 56) wherever a road of tertiary class or better is involved, MINOR (15/12, cycle 37) where only lanes meet (840 junctions) - a 56 s cycle held a hutong car up to 40 s at every corner. `greenSpan(c, phase)` and `cycleOf(c)` are per junction. **Clustering trap:** nodes used to merge into one clock at 45 m single-linkage, but junctions in this city are a median 30 m apart, so with every junction lit whole blocks chain into one; now signal nodes of two main crossings join at 45 m, anything else at 25 m, and nothing joins more than 60 m from the cluster's most important node (largest cluster 73 m across, was 101). The cost, measured with `.scratch/order.mjs` on the same build (`lights=main` against the default; the probe is deterministic, run twice bit-identical): at the spawn arterials 20.8 -> 20.1 km/h, side streets 25.1 -> 18.4; in the lanes of 金鱼池 (`AT=330,2257`) arterials 28.9 -> 28.7, side streets 27.6 -> 22.1. Obedience is counted, not assumed: `traffic.lineStats` counts stop lines crossed and how many on red, `people.debug.stats()` counts crossings started at a light and how many on red - 0 of 50 and 0 of 12 at the spawn, 0 of 60 and 0 of 13 in the lanes; a mutant that ignored red and gave up after 5 s scored 20 of 58 and 5 of 12, so the counters are live. `src/traffic/Order.test.ts` covers which junctions are lit (lanes, a lane into a main road, driveways, ramps, a motorway, a fork, close pairs sharing a clock, a chain of close junctions not merging), that every real junction of ordinary streets is lit, and that a driver stops at the line on red and goes on green. **And the lamps were never visible**: `SignalHeads` put each lamp 0.17 m proud of the housing's centre, right for a 0.32 m deep box, but the box is 0.42 m deep along the traffic it faces, so every lamp in the city sat 4 cm inside it and no light was ever seen to change - `.scratch/lights/lamps.mjs [out.png] [seconds]` prints the nearest heads' lamp positions, facing and colour, and shoots one from 6 m. The heads are now the nearest 240 approaches (sorted: the first 160 the grid listed could leave the junction in front of you dark), down to 8 m long. Once visible, the lit colours (HDR 3.2-3.6) turned out bleached by AgX - red read as salmon, green as mint - so they peak under ~1.8 with one channel dominant. The `traffic_lane_junction` pose shows a short-cycle junction in 金鱼池 at eye height.
- Pitfall (the game hangs, 2026-09-23, 「我把车开到家门口就卡住了……游戏死机了」): 「卡住」 meant the whole game, and every home probe missed it because they ran `stepFixed` only, or `tick` only after arriving. `.scratch/homefreeze.mjs [url] [quality] [sw|north|south|tree|none]` drives to the villa with the REAL frame loop, times every system call of every tick, and when a chunk of frames does not return it pauses V8 through CDP (`Debugger.pause`) and prints the call stack - the only way to name an infinite loop. It sat in `AiDriver.update` -> `fill`: the OSM network has 74 one-way roads that end with no way on (the west end of 景恒街 is 320 m from the villa's gate, inside the 330 m traffic radius, which is why home was where it always happened), and `LaneGraph.next` answered such an end with the link itself, so the driver's advance loop (`s > len - 0.3`) took the same link again, projected the car onto its end again, and spun forever with the main thread. Now `next` returns -1 there (the reverse link at a two-way end, as before), `fill` stops short, the driver brakes for the end of the road as for a red light and goes `lost` (the pool recycles it out of view), the advance loop is capped at 6 hops whatever the data says, and `spawn` skips such links (`endsAfter`). `DeadEnd.test.ts` covers all of it and checks the real network: no link answers with itself except a closed ring (8 loop roads, driven round again by design). Two traps from the hunt: `world.contactPairsWith` reports broad-phase pairs, so a probe that prints "touching" must check the manifold has contacts (the tile's building trimesh otherwise reads as touched at d=0 the whole way); and in headless Chromium `renderFrame` takes ~100 ms once per 30 frames regardless - that is the GPU sync, not the game.
- The city in motion (2026-10-03, 「让城市动起来」). **Trains** (`city/visual/Trains.ts`, system `trains`, `?trains=0`): `railRuns` joins the railway tracks (`rail.json`, decoded) end to end into runs a train can travel - at each end the straightest continuation within 25 degrees of the same kind, yards and sidings left out, cut at every segment set in a road (a train across a road on the level would be OSM's error here, so there are no crossing barriers: nothing would ever close them) - 82 runs of 1.2 km+ (74 main line, 8 subway, the longest 9 km); one train per 2.5 km of run, an 8-car EMU (white, blue stripe, pantograph) or a 6-car subway train (red stripe), its position a function of time running the run back and forth (a nose at both ends), so nothing is simulated far away: trains within 2 km drawn (two instanced meshes a kind, cars and noses; dark glass lit warm at night), and those within 350 m of the player's car get a kinematic box per car the car hits (tag `train`). They start with the railways, after the boot. `node .scratch/trainshot.mjs outPrefix "x,z" [n]` (`KIND=0|1`, `H`, `D` the camera's height and distance) shoots the nearest trains; `npx tsx .scratch/trains-chains.mts` lists the runs. **Buses at stops**: a bus (now 9% of traffic, was 5) looks for a shelter at its link's right-hand kerb 15-85 m ahead once a second (`AiDriver.stopAhead`, `CityStreamer.sheltersNear`), pulls 2.4 m towards the kerb, stops at it for 8-12 s and pulls out, then takes no stop for 30 s; `lineStats.busStops` counts them (`node .scratch/busprobe.mjs [s] [x,z]`, `node .scratch/busshot.mjs out.png` shoots one at its stop). **People out of the subway and over the footbridges** (`people/Walkers.ts`, system `walkers`, `?nowalkers`): from the foot of a kiosk's stair up and out of its open end and along the pavement, or the other way, and over a footbridge stair - deck - stair, either way; spawned among the entrances and bridges within 160 m of the camera, up to 12/24/30 by tier in the crowd's room left over, walked at a stroll and dropped at the path's end; drawn through the crowd after the pedestrians. `node .scratch/walkshot.mjs out.png "from>to" [s]`. Cost: low +3 calls / +0.3 ms, high +16 / +0.85 ms. **Smaller boot** (same day): the game fetches `network.pack.json` (`city/NetPack.ts`: decimetre deltas, class and name tables, parallel arrays; `scripts/city/netpack.mts` packs it at the end of the build and checks the round trip on every value; `loadNetwork()` falls back to `network.json`, which the build scripts and tests still read): 1.70 -> 0.97 MB gzipped; `skyline.json` is written packed (sorted by x, integer deltas; `unpackSkyline`): 0.48 -> 0.36 MB. Real game at 500 KB/s: 17.3 -> 15.5 s to ready, 6.4 -> 5.7 MB before it.

- Distant traffic (2026-10-11, the second item of the 「街道太空」 plan): the physics traffic lives within 330 m (12/26/42 cars by tier), so an arterial ran empty to the horizon. `traffic/Ghosts.ts` `GhostTraffic` (owned by traffic/, `TrafficApi.ghosts`; `?ghosts=0` off, none with `notraffic`): up to 140/260/420 cars by tier from `R_IN` 220 m out to 450/600/750 m with no physics - IDM behind the car in front on their lane (other ghosts and the physics cars' drivers, `drivers` [link, s, lane, v, length] set by traffic each frame), red lights (and amber when they can stop) as a standing car at `STOP_LINE`, turns slowed as AiDriver does, `LaneGraph.next` for the way on (chosen 60 m before the end; the road ending: they stop and go). **Handover both ways**: a ghost coming within `R_IN` becomes a physics car where it is, at its speed and lane (`adoptGhost` in traffic/, same body's free pool slot; while the pool is full it drives on as a ghost and goes at 120 m, or out of view under 180), and traffic's spawn tick first lets the nearest waiting ghost take a free slot (`adoptWaiting`) before spawning one of its own; a physics car despawned for distance past 330 m becomes a ghost (`take`); physics cars treat the ghost ahead on their lane as a leader (`leaderFor`, `AiDriver.nextLink`). **Where new ones go** (`refill`, every 0.4 s, every 0.05 s for 2 s after the start or a camera jump of 150 m): links weighted by length x lanes x class density (`DENSITY` per lane-km: trunk/motorway 30, primary 26, secondary 18, tertiary 8, residential 2) x the share of points along them (every ~50 m) inside the ring, in view (out of view x `BEHIND` 0.25) and nearer (beyond 450 m down to half), scaled to the budget; out of view anywhere, in view only from `IN_VIEW_MIN` 230 m (a car seen from behind is ~7 px there). Two traps on the way: judged by its middle, a long link starting by the camera got no share and the lanes ahead stayed empty to 450 m; and with new ones in view only past 450 m, **the lanes heading away from the camera never filled** - nothing drives into them from the near side (seen from inside the lanes the ghosts that are there also hide each other: `ghostproj.mjs` projects them). When full, the furthest out-of-view ones behind the camera (> 380 m) are let go so the view gets its share. Drawn in far kits that keep their lamps (`CarKit` `{ far: true, lamps: true }`, `setStill(.., lit)`: taxi parts and brake lamps per car, headlights by the kit at night; on low only saloon/SUV/bus kits), and **at night one HDR point a car** (`ghost-lights`, `THREE.Points`, 3 px, additive: white facing the camera, red going away, brighter braking) - their lamps are under a pixel past 300 m. **Perf trap found here**: `Matrix4.toArray` / `InstancedMesh.setMatrixAt` cost 0.4 us a call in the game (polymorphic) - 30x a plain loop; `CarKit` now copies with `put16` (traffic and parked cars gain too), and `paintMetal` is memoised. Cost (`node .scratch/parking/ghostcost.mjs quality "4524,1.6,190" "4200,1.2,186"`): low +0.1 ms, +30 calls (139 ghosts); high ~+0.7 ms (414; the update itself 0.3 ms: `ghostdiag.mjs`). `order.mjs` at the spawn: speeds and red-light crossings as with `?ghosts=0`. Probes in `.scratch/parking/`: `ghostdiag.mjs [q] [s]` (counts by distance, class, road, a strip of 建国门外大街, ms per part), `ghostshot.mjs outPrefix q "from>to" ...` (`FOV=12` zooms, `DRIVE=x0,z0,x1,z1,speed` drives a line first, `PARAMS=tod=21.5`), `ghostproj.mjs`, `ghostprof.mjs`.

- **车流集中到眼前**（2026-11-10，`docs/street-level-design.md` 第一批 A2 第一步，「只是在地图上开车」「从第一批开始做」）：实体车的名额（12 / 26 / 42，省电档减半）原来谁先占谁用——一辆停在平行小街、镜头看不到的车能占到 330 米外；名额满了，前方的远景车开到 120 米只能消失，所以近处比远处还稀（实测 150 米内高画质 13–40 辆、低画质 5–12 辆，低画质的东长安街只有 5 辆）。现在：
  - `makeRoom()`（每 0.4 秒，名额满时）：镜头里有远景车进了交接圈（`GhostTraffic.waitingInView`），就让最不值的一辆实体车让位——镜头外 90 米以上的，或镜头里比那辆远景车还远 60 米以上的，按「距离 ×（镜头外 2.5）」挑最大的。让位的车变回远景车（`toGhost`，在交接圈里又看不见的会被远景层自己收掉）。玩家 70 米内的、进站的公交、被撞停的、逃逸车、骑手、停着的都不动。
  - `adoptWaiting` 挑远景车时镜头里的优先（镜头外的距离按 2.2 倍算）。
  - 实测（`.scratch/streetlevel/look.mjs`，150 米内）：低画质东长安街 5 → 12、鼓楼东大街 8 → 15；高画质 25–31。高画质离文档的目标（40 以上）还差，要等第二步「轻量车」。
- **轻量车**（2026-11-11，`docs/street-level-design.md` A2 第二步，「下一步做什么」「可以」；`?lightcars=0` 关掉，回到上一条的做法）：远景车（`Ghosts.ts`）不再在 220 米处交给物理、120 米处消失，而是一直开到玩家身边才变成实体车；实体车开远了又变回去。实体车只剩玩家身边的十来辆，路上有多少车由密度表定，不再由名额定。
  - **交接**：玩家身边 `hot.r` 米以内（55 米，省电档 45 米，再加车速 × 1.3、最多加 35 米）的远景车变成实体车（`adoptGhost`，就在它画的位置和朝向上，带着它已经选好的下一段路 `AiDriver.seed`）；实体车离玩家超过 `hot.r + 25` 米、好好开着的（`canGhost`：没被撞、没翻、没在进站、没被扣住、没在让警车、车头顺着路）变回远景车（`toGhost`，带位姿和下一段路）。警车和任务车（`ExternalCars.blocking`）周围 22 米也要实体车（`hots`）。名额满了（12 / 26 / 42，省电档减半）就让比它离玩家远 4 米以上的一辆实体车让位（`evict`），所以实体车总是最近的那几辆。车的型号跟着远景车来：没有空位就现造一辆（`newCar`，不再受每种车几辆的名额表限制），车模的容量按「名额 + 近处轻量车」留（`NEAR_DRAW` 28 / 44 / 70）。这个模式下 `spawn()`、`makeRoom()`、`adoptWaiting()` 都不跑；豪车和跑车改成远景车上色时二十辆里出一辆（`ghostPaint`）。
  - **近处的轻量车**（离镜头 `rNear` 130 / 150 / 170 米以内，低 / 中 / 高）：用实体车的车模画（`CarKit.setRail`：车轮转、前轮打方向、刹车灯），不占新的绘制调用。位姿不贴折线——贴着折线过路口会原地转 90°——而是像 `AiDriver` 那样追着轨道前方 6–20 米的一点开（`pursue`，pure pursuit，落后了快一点），直路上和轨道重合，过路口走弧线。
  - **按位置让行**（离镜头 `rNear + 40` 米以内的，`close`）：车道队列只管同一段路的同一条车道，所以另外按几何位置让——正前方车道宽度里的行人和徒步的玩家（`blockers`）、实体车和玩家的车（`movers`，骑手贴着路边骑、不在车道队列里，也靠这个）、从别的路过来的另一辆轻量车。两辆互相挡着时：停着的那辆让不开，动的让它；都动或都停，离冲突点远的让。小角度汇到同一个路口的两条路（匝道并线、主辅路合流）隔着一条车道就开始让，排成一列。都看路面高度（`y`），高架上下两层互不相干。实体车的司机也按位置看同向的近处轻量车（`leaderFor`）。**让行有耐心上限**：为让人让车停了 8 秒就硬挤过去 4 秒（`wait` / `push`）——探针里一辆轻量车和两个骑手在 10 米长的小路口互相等了 200 秒，后面排了一串。
  - **近处的密度**：预算先紧着 330 米以内（权重 `nearBoost` 1.5 / 1.8 / 2.2，到 450 米线性降到 1），每条路不超过密度表的满值；镜头外 200 米内按 0.6 算（原来 0.25，镜头一转就看见）。新车在镜头外 70 米以外放，镜头里仍然只在 230 米以外放；刚开局或瞬移后 2 秒内近处也放（离玩家 25 米以外）。镜头跳走时玩家身边 260 米内的留着（`KEEP`），过场镜头切回来车还在。
  - **被对向路面占了的车道不用**（`LaneGraph.minLane` / `laneOn`，实体车的司机也用）：数据里有些双幅路画得比两幅的中线间距还宽（安定门内大街两幅中线隔 8 米、每幅 13 米宽；西直门北大街、五四大街也是），两边最靠里的车道叠在一起，对开的车迎面穿过去。全城 32,403 条多车道单行路段里有 2,776 条（367 公里）是这样，以前车少看不出来。现在车道中线落在对向另一条路路面里的就不用，密度也按能用的车道数算。路面本身还是叠着画的，没动。
  - **实测**（`.scratch/streetlevel/lightprobe.mjs`，从标题进城、沿车道自动开约 50 km/h、21 秒；同一份代码开和关对比）：150 米内的车，低画质 8–11 → 20–54（东长安街 29–33、建国门外大街 48–54、鼓楼东大街 20–28），中画质 61–71，高画质 19–34 → 32–118（建国门外大街 67–83、东长安街 64–74、鼓楼东大街 32–34、东三环 111–118）。镜头里 150 米内凭空消失的车：每 21 秒 6–46 辆 → 0。每帧 CPU：低画质持平（6.2 → 6.5 ms、5.8 → 5.9 ms；CPU 降速 4 倍 41.6 → 37.4、27.1 → 27.4 ms，在波动之内），高画质多一成以内；三角形低画质多 0–6%，高画质多 7–17%。也就是同样的 CPU 换来三到五倍的车，CPU 本身没省：低画质的实体车本来就卡在名额上。车流 5 分钟不堵死（`flow.mjs`，西直门、国贸、出生点各盯 150–300 秒），没有车掉下高架（`DECK=1`），警察追捕探针、从标题开始的冒烟、`vitest` 都过。
  - **还有的毛病**：汇入口和复杂立交的节点前，两辆车偶尔挨到 1.1–1.8 米（车道模型在节点前收成一点，实体车在那儿也会剐）；轻量车停下时如果位姿还没追回轨道，会歪着停；省电档只有 6 辆实体车，堵车时第 7 近的车还是轻量车，硬挤能从它身上穿过去；公交只有变成实体车以后才进站。探针里把玩家的车停在人行道上，有时挡住旁边的转弯车道，实体车会一直排在后面（和以前一样）。
  - **探针**（都在 `.scratch/streetlevel/`，真实帧循环）：`lightprobe.mjs <画质> "路名正则@x,z;..." [秒] [CPU 降速] [额外参数]`（`lp.sh` 是它的摘要版；`MINLANES=3 DECK=1` 挑高架主路）、`lightdiag.mjs`（停在一点，按距离分段数车、列叠车）、`junction.mjs`（路口俯拍，`WATCH=40` 找停着叠在一起的）、`flow.mjs <画质> "x,z" [秒] [卡住的秒数]`（会不会堵死，列出卡住的车和它被什么挡着：`GhostCar.why`）、`minlane.mjs`、`linkinfo.mjs <路段号...>`。`TrafficApi.debugPool()` 给探针看实体车的池子。
- **能坐的车**（2026-11-11，「把第二批剩的：坐公交、打车 做完」，玩法在 `gameplay.md`「坐公交、打车」）：`TrafficApi.fareNear(x, z, r, 'bus' | 'taxi', ok?)` 给出一辆实体车的把手 `FareCar`（按司机对象缓存，一辆车一个）：`alive`（还在车流里、司机没 `lost`、没翻）、`stopped`（`AiDriver.atStop`）、`link` / `s`、`left`（路线还剩几段）、`stuck`、`red`、`stop(s?)`（靠边停下等着——借公交进站那套 `busStop`，`dwell` 给 1e9；停稳以前每步都叫，过了路口会重新定位置）、`go(links?)`（接着开，给了路线就 `AiDriver.follow`）、`keep(on)`（`Npc.fare`：不回收、不变回轻量车、不让位；离玩家 150 米外自己放掉，叫停了没人管的也放它走）、`pose(alpha, …)`（插值位姿，`Engine.alpha` 是这一帧的插值系数）、`hop()`（卡死时沿要走的路往前挪：16–68 米处第一个桥面已加载、车道上不和别的车叠的位置）。
  - `AiDriver`：`follow(links)`（路线上相邻两段接不上就从那儿起作废；走完了又随便开）、`planLeft`、`hop(ahead, ok)`、`atStop`、`red`、`kerbside`（公交：`laneOn(l, 7)`，一直最靠右）。`busEvent`（进站报站）只对公交发 `traffic:bus`，出租车靠边不报站。
  - **公交进站改了三处**：`stopAhead` 的左右判反了（见 `gameplay.md`），现在只进自己右手边的站（`DeadEnd.test.ts` 有测试）；公交（轻量车和实体车）一直走最靠右的车道；玩家徒步站在站台 8 米内时那一站每辆公交都进，离得近一点（按车速算刹得住的距离）也进。同一个车流探针（`flow.mjs high "4524,190" 150`）改前改后一样：平均 12–24 km/h，没有 40 秒以上不动的车。
  - `leaderFor` 和轻量车的 `blockers` 里「徒步的玩家」改成 `footOf()`：坐在座位上的（`pl.riding`）不算站在路上。不这样的话公交会把坐在自己车里的玩家当成前面的行人。

- **车流的北京规矩**（2026-11-11，`docs/street-level-design.md` A6，「接着做」）。五样，都在 traffic/ 里：
  - **路况表**（`Flow.ts`，纯函数，`Flow.test.ts`）：有名字的环路、高速、快速路、主干道（motorway / trunk / primary，辅路算主路的；匝道不算）每 2 公里一格、每个方向、每 3 分钟（现实时间）一个时段，是畅通、缓行、拥堵里的一种。机会按钟点：早晚高峰（7–9:30、17–19:30）环路四成拥堵、五成缓行，主干道两成、五成；白天环路 8% 拥堵；22 点到 6 点没有拥堵。分方向（`headingOf`）：一边堵成一串尾灯、对面畅通，也省一半的车。全城的车另外随钟点多少（`hourDensity`：半夜 0.55、高峰 1.2）。
  - **车流读它**：轻量车（`Ghosts.ts` 的 `levelOf`，按路段中点算，一个时段、一个钟点段算一次）的密度乘 1 / 1.5 / 2，车速开到限速的 1 / 0.6 / 0.32；实体车的司机（`AiDriver.pace`）同一个车速。名额有限（140 / 260 / 420 辆），摊到 750 米就稀了，所以缓行、拥堵的路段在镜头 300 米内的那一截先按满值放（最多占六成名额），别的路按比例少一些。东三环往南同一个地方量的（高画质，车停在路沿外，60 秒后）：拥堵 39.6 辆每车道公里、17 km/h，缓行 30.4、31 km/h，畅通 14.4、50 km/h，关掉路况 14.8、51 km/h；低画质拥堵 15.5（名额只有 140）、18 km/h。每帧 JS 时间没变（高 8.1 ms，低 5.6 ms）。
  - **情报板和交通台读同一张表**：`city/visual/InfoBoards.ts` 的 `stretches`（每一段按中点、按板子朝的方向查；`InfoBoard` 多了每座立交的坐标 `at`），`audio/` 的 `roadsNear` 多给一个 `level`（这条路离玩家最近那一点两个方向里差的那个，直接问 `ghosts.levelOf`），`Talk.ts` 身边三条路按它报，凑数的远处的路还是按钟点猜。原来板子和广播各掷各的骰子，路上的车哪个钟点都一样多。
  - **变道、打灯**：轻量车被同车道的慢车挡住（前车不到自己想开的七成、离得近），左右哪条车道前面宽敞 10 米以上、旁边后面没车就变过去（`tryLane`，按车道队列看；离路口 70 米内不变，公交不变），画出来的横向位置每秒挪 1.5 米。实体车一样的条件压了 1.5 秒才变（`laneChange`，按位置看实体车、玩家、警车、近处的轻量车，所以不往并排的玩家身上并），`AiDriver.changeLane` 每秒并 1.3 米——一下子换目标车道，十几米每秒时两秒横甩三米多，冲过头撞飞过中央护栏。要拐弯的车进路口前 45 米打灯。转向灯单画（`Blinkers.ts`）：车模的灯只有按实例开关的刹车灯、倒车灯，九种车身逐个加转向灯太费事，所以变道、拐弯的车那一侧车头车尾各一个琥珀色小块，一个实例化网格、一次绘制调用（64 个，低画质 32），只画实体车和近处的轻量车。玩家在环路车道里停着当慢车：一分钟里后面的实体车变道 6 回，撞懵的司机 5 车·秒（关掉变道是 12）。
  - **红灯倒计时牌**（`Signals.ts`）：每个灯头朝立杆那一侧一块黑底小板，两位七段数码由着色器按每实例的数画（不用贴图），红灯、绿灯显示还剩几秒、和灯一个色，黄灯时灭；一秒更新四回。`Signals.left(l, t)` 给还剩几秒。
  - **绿灯头几秒电动车一拥而上**：玩家往有灯的路口开（150 米内）时，七成的新骑手放在玩家身后同一段路 30–90 米的路边缝里（镜头看不见）；名额满了而玩家身边不到 4 个时，把最远、看不见的一个调过来（`pullRider`）。骑手贴着路边（离路边 0.45–0.6 米）从排队的车旁边挤上来——为了留出这条缝，最右车道的车往左让 0.4 米（`AiDriver` 的 `EDGE_ROOM` / `driveOffset`，轻量车也一样；公交不让，它要靠站台，骑手在它后面等）。等红灯时停在汽车停车线前面 2–4.5 米（`ahead`），四个里三个抢灯（`eager`：自己这边再过 0.9–1.8 秒就绿了就走，落在路口全红的那两秒里）。路边缝里前面停着东西（倒着的电动车、等灯的前一个骑手）两秒半不动，往路中间挪 1.25 米绕过去，走起来六秒后挪回去——等灯的时候后来的就这样停到前一个旁边。探针里（车停在光华路路口前 28 米的中间车道）5 秒内身后来了 7 个骑手，头一个停在离路口 4.3 米（汽车线是 7 米），红灯还剩 2 秒时起步；闯红灯统计 0/70（抢灯的按自己的线、自己的灯算）。
  - 开关：`?rush=0` 关路况表（哪个钟点都一样多、都畅通），`?flow=0|1|2` 把表里的路全按一种路况算（探针、截图），`?lanechange=0` 关变道。shot mode 下路况表默认关（时段跟现实时钟走，截图会不确定），要看加 `?rush=1`。
  - **坑：`LaneGraph.project(l, x, z, hint)` 只在 `hint` 前 25 米、后 40 米的折线段里找**，是给跟着车走的司机用的。拿 `l.len / 2` 当 hint 去问「这个点离这条路多远」，在三百米长、拐了弯的路上会落在中间那一截，离得老远——这回骑手的「玩家在哪段路上」、外卖楼门的「在不在路面上」、合影的「马路在哪边」都栽在这上面。要整段路上最近的点用新加的 `nearestOn(l, x, z)`。顺手把 npc/ 的 `onRoad`、代驾停车、`spawnRunner` 也换了；trials/、people/、online/Feedback 里还有三处老写法没动。
