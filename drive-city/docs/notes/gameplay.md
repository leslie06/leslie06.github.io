# Gameplay: intro, missions, campaign, stunts, story, check-ins, trials, audio

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- Pitfall (the opening getaway sat on 「准备中……」, 2026-09-25, 「现在开局并没有追车」): `CityStreamer.preload` moved the camera for one call and waited for the tiles; in the real frame loop the camera rig put the camera back behind the car every frame and `update` streamed round the spawn, so the getaway's start 2 km away never loaded and the car stayed locked for the whole 60 s timeout. Every probe ticked by hand and never saw it. `preload` now pins streaming to its point (`pin`) until it returns. `node .scratch/introlive.mjs [url] [phone]` starts from the title on a fresh profile with the real loop and prints the intro's step every 2 s.
- `audio/Radio.ts` — the car radio (2026-09-23, 「开车的时候要能听歌，GTA 里的 rap」): real rap is licensed and the game ships no files, so 「B城电台」 composes hip-hop live with Web Audio - kick / snare / hats, an 808 with slides, sawtooth pad chords over a i-VI-III-VII turnaround, a pentatonic hook through a feedback delay - eight titled tracks, each a seed, tempo and key (four boom-bap, four half-time trap), arranged intro / verse / hook / verse / hook / outro (40 bars) and then the next. A second station 「我的歌单」 appears when `public/music/playlist.json` exists (`[{ "title", "file" }]`, files beside it; do not commit copyrighted songs - keep them local). N cycles station → station → off (`radioPressed`), the choice persists in `drivecity.radio`, and it only sounds while the player is in a car and not paused; the HUD toasts the station and track on every change. It sits on its own bus (0.45, compressed) under the master so M mutes it too. `Radio.test.ts` pins the arrangement to its seed; `node .scratch/radioprobe.mjs` unlocks audio in a live page, reads the bus with an analyser and walks through N and getting out.
- The main story as missions (2026-10-12, 「做成 GTA5 那样好玩」, plan in `docs/gta-plan.md`: no guns - Sleeping Dogs' way: cars, fists, the scenery; step 1 a mission framework and chapter 1). `campaign/` (system `campaign`, after story; off in shot mode unless `?campaign=1`, `?campaign=0` off). **Framework**: `Runner.ts` (pure, `Runner.test.ts`) runs a `MissionDef`'s steps (`enter` / `update` -> 'run' | 'done' | {fail} / `exit`); a step marked `checkpoint` is where a failure restarts, with the state as it was when it began, after its `restore` puts back what the mission had built (the mission scope is cleared at a failure); everything a step or the mission spawns goes in a `Scope` (`ctx.step` / `ctx.mission`) and is undone on its end, a failure, an abandon; a `guard` checks mission-wide failures each frame. `Kit.ts` `Env` (the APIs, `objective()` = `MissionApi.story` so events, fares and jobs hold off; `kerbPose(x, z, along, toward)` - a kerb spot facing the way to go; `putIn` / `putOnFoot`; markers, blips) and the steps: `cutscene` (shots of from/to/look/secs/fov eased under letterbox bars, lines queued at prio 4 in the dialogue box lifted above the bar, every HUD piece but the box hidden via `body.cmp-cine`, Space/Enter/tap skips after 0.4 s), `talk`, `getIn` (the mission car takeable with F through traffic's `addExternalCars`, registered once in `index.ts`), `drive` (GPS, corona, radius, `stop`, a `tick` that may end or fail it), `wait`, `call`, and `Gang` (enemy `ScriptCar`s spawned 100-190 m behind the player and driven with `Pursuit` ramming; done when all are wrecked or none within 220 m for 6 s). `Cars.ts` `ScriptCar`: a Vehicle the mission owns, simulated (handbrake, or a driver) and drawn in its own `CarKit` with its livery while it is not the player's car; damage like the police's (hp from impacts, wrecked smoking at 0 or 2.5 s on its roof); `remove()` leaves it to the player if they are in it. `Screens.ts`: the chapter title card, the failed card, the results card (rows, medal, pay, Enter/Space/button; 12 s auto), `Letterbox`. `index.ts`: the next chapter's start corona and a 'target' blip; driving or walking into it while calm starts it; a failure shows why, then (6 s after wasted/busted, 2.2 s otherwise) asks retry / give up in the dialogue box; a pass pays, saves `drivecity.campaign.v1` {done, best: {medal, secs}} and emits `campaign:passed`. `debug.start(id)`, `debug.skipTo(stepId)`, `debug.runner()`. ContactsApi gained `spot(id)` and `actor(id)`. **Chapter 1 《回京》** (`missions/m1.ts`, texts `core/i18n/campaign.ts`; see the plan §5): 老K's stall -> his car (京A·K8888, door 'K') -> Xiaoyu at the kerb within 150 m of her stall that is the shortest drive (her stall's one-way is a 4.1 km loop) -> back to the stall, two Black Panther SUVs (door '豹') ambush 20 s / 350 m into the return -> the stall, outro, results (gold under 9 min, car 70%+, no stars). Voiced (2026-10-12) in CosyVoice, Chinese and English: `scripts/voice/lines.mts` takes the story's spoken keys (`/^cmp\.m\d+\.[kyc]\d+$/`: 老K k, Xiaoyu y, Chen Ye c), `dialogue.mts` gives them to `laok`, `xiaoyu` and a new designed voice `chenye` (his Speaker's VoiceSpec carries `character: 'chenye'`) with an `INSTRUCT`/`INSTRUCT_EN` per line; then synth (zh, `--lang en`) and dialogue-pack for both. `node .scratch/campaign/voicecheck.mjs [zh|en]` starts the chapter in the real loop and lists the voice files fetched. Probe: `node .scratch/campaign/play.mjs outDir [quality] [failOnce]` plays it through in shot mode (`contacts=1`: the contacts are hidden in shot mode otherwise): skips the cutscene, F into the car, drives each leg with the pilot on a route densified to 2 m (to the ring itself: the route ends at the graph; stuck 4 s - the pilot on a kerb corner - it moves the car 30 m on), `failOnce` wrecks the car in the ambush, answers retry, and checks the checkpoint; prints every step change and the results card. `routes.mjs` measures the legs.
- 地标打卡 (2026-10-03, the fifth item, gameplay): `checkin/` (system `checkins`) - every landmark (`LANDMARKS` less 我家: 62) checks in once, either within `REACH` 25 m of one of its footprints (`WorldApi.landmarkAreas`, recorded by `placeLandmarks` with each model's height), or photographed: slower than `SLOW` 12 m/s (or on foot), within its view distance (1.2 x its size, 60-220 m), the camera within `VIEW_ANGLE` 35 degrees of its main footprint's middle (a third of its height up, at most 20 m) and a ray from the camera (`groups(CG.CAR, CG.WORLD)`, the player's car excluded) meeting its own colliders (`landmark:<id>`) first - a third of them stand in squares and parks no lane comes near (the Monument 125 m, 祈年殿 430 m inside 天坛's walls), so the orbit camera (mouse / right stick) is the way to them, and a hint pill says so while one is in range (`checkin.hint`, `.hintTouch` on phones). A check-in flashes the screen, slides in a polaroid card with a snapshot of that very frame cropped round the landmark (`renderFrame` wrapped for one call, the drawing buffer read before it is cleared), its names and 打卡成功, plays a shutter and chime (`checkin` event), pays ¥200 (¥8000 more for all 62) and is saved in `drivecity.checkins.v1`; the map and the radar draw checked-in landmarks as a muted diamond with a tick (`LandmarkPin.visited`, blip kind `landmarkDone`, legend 已打卡地标), the pause screen counts them. Nothing runs off the play (title, pause, garage). Probes: `node .scratch/checkin/drive.mjs out` drives 前门西大街 east past 天安门 at 34 km/h and shoots the card; `node .scratch/checkin/probe2.mjs` puts the car on the lane nearest each landmark, facing it (49 of 62 check in from there with the camera settled; the rest need the square, the park or a walk).
- 计时赛 (2026-10-03, the fifth item, gameplay): `trials/` (system `trials`). `Courses.ts` `COURSES` - 长安街 (复兴门 -> 国贸, 7.8 km), 东二环 (4.1), 东三环 (5.5, from a 13 m deck), 西二环 (4.8), 东四环 (5.5), 北四环 (7.6), 京通快速路 (3.8) - each one way along the road named `key`: `walkCourse` starts on the carriageway nearest `from` heading towards `to` (main lines first by class: a ring's 辅路 carries its name) and at every node takes the straightest continuation (under 35 degrees) that keeps the name, a span named after its bridge or an unnamed piece, never a slip road, until it passes within 90 m of `to` - not routed: the router's shortest way would leave the ring at a slip road. Starts are on plain stretches between interchanges (a gate at 复兴门桥 or 双井桥 held the car at the foot of a deck). Drive through a start gate (a `Marker`, drawn within 700 m; `trial` blips, a stopwatch) the right way at 3 m/s+ and the clock runs (rolling start, traffic and lights as they are); checkpoints every 500 m in turn (the next and the finish glow; the GPS leads to the finish), a panel with the time, the checkpoint and the split against the best run; medals by average speed 45/65/85 km/h paying ¥300/500/1000 the first time each is won; best times and splits in `drivecity.trials.v1`; getting out, 60 m off the line for 3 s or three times the bronze time abandons it. Events `trial:start` / `trial:checkpoint` / `trial:finish` (audio: beeps and a fanfare); the pause screen counts the medals. On the leaderboards since 2026-10-04: a board per course, `trial_<id>` (`online/Leaderboard.ts` takes `COURSES`; the stats server's `BOARDS` has the seven, 40 s - 30 min; the live server needs `stats/worker.js` copied up and restarted, see `../stats/README.md`). `npx tsx .scratch/trials-check.mts` walks every course in Node; `node .scratch/trials-probe.mjs <id> [m/s] [params]` (`SHOT=out.png`) drives one with the pilot from its run-up (`debug.lines()[i].runup`): all seven finish at 79 km/h (silver).
- Train sounds (2026-10-03): `TrainApi.nearest(x, z)` (`Trains.ts`: distance, speed, kind of the nearest train); audio runs a rumble (noise low-passed at 180 Hz) and the wheels' clack (band-passed noise gated by a square LFO at speed / 12.5 Hz, a rail length) on the nearest within 260 m, and a two-tone horn (311/370 Hz) every 25-45 s when a main-line train passes within 90 m.
- The first batch of "make it fun" (2026-09-25, after 「现在游戏场景和玩法感觉都没意思」: money had nothing to buy, a bust cost nothing, the opening was a 1.2 km drive to a fare). Four systems, each with a probe:
  - Nitro: `Vehicle.tune` (per car, because every car of a type shares one spec object: engine `torque`/`top`, tyre `grip`, `nitro` = seconds a bottle burns) and `Vehicle.boost` (a push at the centre of mass through `push`, so the impact detector does not read it as a crash; allowed to 1.3x the limiter). `vehicle/index.ts` burns it while `InputState.nitro` (Shift; pad B or LB; the touch N₂O button, shown only when the car has a bottle) is held with throttle, `NITRO_ACCEL` 7 m/s²; `VehicleApi.nitroActive` drives the FOV kick and rumble (CameraRig), exhaust flames (fx `Flames`), the hiss (audio) and the HUD bottle beside the speedo. `node .scratch/nitroprobe.mjs` - a taxi's 0-100 goes from >6 s to 2.7 s.
  - Street combo (`stunts/`): near misses (the two footprints' gap while alongside, < 0.8 m, closing > 6 m/s, untouched), drifts (`drift:end`), air (`vehicle:land`), oncoming (against a one-way, or on the left of a two-way link - right-hand traffic), red lights (the stop line of the link being driven, `STOP_LINE` from AiDriver, crossed on red), knocked furniture and losing the police. Moves within 4 s chain (x1, x1, x2, x2 ... x5), bank as cash at 1/15 per point and refill the nitro; a crash over 5 loses the chain, getting out banks it. What is bought goes on the car in the garage, which becomes *your car* (`drivecity.garage.v1`, rebuilt at `game:start`); `Parts.ts` holds the stages and prices. - **送豆腐 + 水杯**（2026-10-22，用户贴了一段《头文字D》玩法设想：「凌晨从豆腐店出发，跑山路送货，仪表台上放一杯水，横向 / 纵向 G 值太大水就会洒，洒光任务失败」；商量后「先做水杯和送货任务」，在城里先做，出城路段和妙峰山山路以后做）：
  - **水杯**（`missions/WaterCup.ts`，纯逻辑，`WaterCup.test.ts` 8 条）：水面是一个带阻尼的弹簧（1.5 Hz，阻尼比 0.38，阶跃冲过头约 28%），追着车身坐标系里的水平加速度（`Vehicle.accel`，已平滑，x 左 z 前，单位换成 g，截到 1.4 g；上下颠簸不算）。水面倾斜超过杯沿能兜住的值（满杯 0.7 g，越空越能兜，空杯多兜 0.55 g）就按超出量洒（每秒每 g 0.4 杯）。所以平顺地把力加上去不洒，同样的力猛地打方向、一脚急刹会晃过头洒水。撞车（`vehicle:impact`，强度 2.5 以上）一下洒一截（每 m/s 3.5%，最多 45%），水面也被撞得晃起来。剩 10% 以下算洒光。原来设想里的写法是用速度差算总加速度、超过 0.7 g 就按超出量洒：没有晃过头这回事，平顺和猛打一样；上下颠簸也会算进去，压个马路牙子就洒，所以没照搬。
