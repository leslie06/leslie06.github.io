- Corridors (2026-09-29, 「东四环延长到东风北桥……京通快速往东」): the playable area is now the main box plus two corridor boxes (`region.mjs` `EXTRA`/`REGIONS`, fetched as `chunk-n*`/`chunk-e*`): **n** 东四环北段 from 红领巾桥 past 朝阳公园桥 and 双新桥 to 东风北桥 (39.926-39.963 N, 116.462-116.504 E, with 朝阳公园), **e** 京通快速路 east past 高碑店桥 to the 东五环 interchange (39.897-39.921 N, to 116.550 E); a box to 东风北桥 across the whole width would have doubled the city. 1238 -> 1653 tiles, 11.3 -> 14.3 MB. `build.mjs`: `inRegion` is the union, `eachTile` walks each box, the manifest gets `regions` (bounds stay the union's box; `findDeadEnds` takes the regions for its edge margin), an element already read from the main chunks is never replaced by a corridor's newer copy, and a corridor tree's seed is its position (the main box keeps its trees; species still reshuffled a little near the old edge). **The network is clipped to the drawn stretches** (a segment is in when its midpoint is, as for the tiles' pieces): whole ways used to go in, 东五环 and the airport expressway ran on for kilometres past the data's edge, and the router's seed links (the longest, fastest) were those stubs - its giant component came out one link and no route was found anywhere (the router now tries 32 seeds). Roads that run off the data are closed: a row of red/white water barriers and a 前方道路封闭 board across the end (and across a one-way's start, the way out for anyone driving it the wrong way), solid, from `Signs.ts` `closures`. **Lamps**: 2.5k lamp posts (7%) stood in another road's carriageway (a driveway's mouth, a slip road, a side road alongside) and cars met them head on; `inCarriageway` (ground carriageways only, `carSegs`) keeps them out - 37.6k -> 34.3k. **Empty land**: OSM has residential land and almost no buildings along 东四环's east side; in the corridors only, a residential/commercial polygon less than 6% covered is filled like a 小区 (`filled`: rows of slab blocks long side to the south, one style a polygon - walk-ups, mid-rise slabs or towers - office blocks on commercial land, set back from roads and clear of parks, water, pitches and car parks; 409 blocks), and `landuse=construction|brownfield` is a new area kind `site` (bare earth, `sparse_grass` tinted) with blue hoarding round it (walls, broken where a road crosses the line) - much of the stretch between 朝阳公园桥 and 东风北桥 really is being rebuilt. - OSM source (2026-09-29): the city is built from Geofabrik's Beijing extract, not Overpass. `data/beijing-latest.osm.pbf` (https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/china/beijing-latest.osm.pbf, 35 MB, git-ignored) -> `node scripts/city/extract-pbf.mjs` (needs `brew install osmium-tool`): one `osmium tags-filter` with fetch-osm.mjs's query, then per box of `REGIONS` an `osmium extract -s smart` (multipolygon and building relations whole) turned from OPL into the Overpass `out geom` shape, `.cache/osm/chunk-0-0.json` (main box), `chunk-n0-0.json`, `chunk-e0-0.json`; the Overpass chunks were moved to `.cache/osm-overpass/`. 3 s, then `node scripts/city/build.mjs` (10 s) and `GEN=1 npx vitest run src/stunts/Spots.gen.test.ts`. Against the Overpass cache it is the same elements to within real edits (`node .scratch/osmcmp.mjs`: +68 signal nodes, +77 crossings, a handful of ways deleted since); the rebuild changed 1406 tiles, mostly tree species (seeded in element order); audits unchanged (bridge block 137 / wall 17 / edge 42, wallaudit 15 walls). `fetch-osm.mjs` stays as the fallback without osmium - do not mix the two in `.cache/osm`. Found on the drive to 东风北桥: a bus stop was matched to a carriageway by its lateral offset, which is ~0 past a line's end, so a stop 80 m away took a short road in line with it and its shelter stood in that road - 251 of the city's shelters were in a carriageway; `StreetFurniture.ts` now matches by distance and skips a shelter overlapping another carriageway (a side road alongside) or a second one at the same spot: 1180 -> 1064 shelters, 5 left in a carriageway (a neighbour tile's road). `npx tsx .scratch/shelterdiff.mts out.txt` + `.scratch/shelterroad.mts out.txt` count them.
- Guide signs (指路标志, 2026-09-29, 「增加路牌，不要让用户迷路」): `city/Signs.ts` `placeSigns` (pure; run at build time by `scripts/city/signs.mts`, which `build.mjs` calls at its end, into `public/city/signs.json`, 317 KB / 45 KB gzipped - the game spends nothing placing them) puts up three kinds from the lane graph and `network.json`'s new `br` (named interchanges: every point of a car bridge, signal or motorway junction named 桥, or of the bus stops 东风北桥南/东...; footbridges, railway and bus-only bridges and 金水桥 out) and `en` (every road and interchange in pinyin the Beijing way - proper name run together, generic part after: 建国门外大街 Jianguomenwai Dajie, 双新桥 Shuangxin Qiao; rings in English: 东四环中路 E 4th Ring Rd M; made by `pinyin-pro` at build time, a devDependency - OSM's name:en is a third of the names and 双新桥's is the ring's): **cross** (交叉路口预告) 60-90 m before a junction of named streets, the junction as arrows with the road each leads onto (a dual carriageway's two or four nodes are one junction); **exit** (出口预告) 110-260 m before a slip road leaves a trunk or motorway (or a one-way primary): 出口 EXIT, the interchange (the first named one the slip road's path passes, else the main road's next within 500 m) and where the slip road leads (through 辅路 and the ring's own names); **ahead** 200 m past each interchange on a main road, the road by its ring (`shortName`: 东四环中路 -> 东四环, 京通快速/京通快速道路 -> 京通快速路), the way it runs (北) and the next three interchanges with distances - each carriageway is walked end to end as one chain across section names and across spans named after their bridge (OSM calls 东四环's deck at 红领巾桥 "红领巾桥"). Green on motorways, blue elsewhere; every one hangs from a cantilever at the right-hand kerb (on a deck the pole stands on the parapet, `hw + 0.25`) 5.5 m up, its plate 2 m in from the kerb so the street trees' crowns do not bury it; no pole in any carriageway at its level and nothing overhead (a deck). 1218 signs (979 cross, 181 exit, 58 ahead), 188 closures. `city/visual/GuideSigns.ts` draws them: poles, arms, plates and barriers are four instanced meshes packed within 320 m (+5 draw calls on low, ~0.1 ms), a pole collider each; the faces are text drawn on demand into slots of one 2048 x 1536 DataTexture atlas (the nearest 23 signs facing the camera within 280 m, two new faces a tick, each a row-by-row `addUpdateRange` sub-upload with mipmaps regenerated; the rest show their plain colour). The plate is a touch under white (#d6d6d6) and glows 0.02 at night: square-on in the headlights at night it otherwise saturated, text and ground alike. `?signs=0` for A/B. `Signs.test.ts` checks 东四环 northbound lists 双新桥 and 东风北桥, 京通 eastbound 高碑店桥, every pole off every carriageway, and the closures (`PROFILE=1` writes `.scratch/signs.txt`); `node .scratch/signs/signshot.mjs out.png "regex over kind+names" [n] [params]` (`D=60` for the distance) shoots close-ups of matching signs into a sheet.
- Buildings beyond OSM and the old city (2026-09-29, 「尽量还原北京城的原貌」 - the first two steps of a plan: fill the buildings and give them real heights, then grow the map to the 4th Ring box by box). **Measured first**: inside the 4th Ring OSM holds ~half of the building footprint area (in the first play area 66% by area, 28% by count); two thirds of its buildings had no height and got a random one; and ~3,700 of those that do say `building:levels=1`, most from one mass edit, apartment blocks among them (drawn as 3 m slabs). **Footprints**: `python3 scripts/city/fetch-buildings.py` (`pip install --user duckdb`) reads Overture's buildings theme straight off S3 for the 4th Ring box and keeps the non-OSM ones - Shi et al.'s machine-learnt East-Asian footprints (CLSM, CC BY 4.0, WGS-84, already conflated against OSM by Overture) - into `.cache/buildings/clsm.json`; `build.mjs` adds them with ids from 8e12 where our (newer) OSM has nothing (a learnt footprint more than 12% under an OSM one, any corner or edge midpoint 0.5 m into a ground carriageway, in water, or in the palace/temple zones is dropped); the tile worker also drops a learnt building overlapping a landmark footprint and any building overlapping a `clear` zone (`Clear.ts` `overlaps`). **Heights** (`scripts/city/extra-buildings.mjs`): 3D-GloBFP (Che et al. 2024, tile 2292) and CMAB (Zhang et al. 2025, Beijing; also function and age) from `data/*.zip` are burnt into 2.5 m rasters over the play area and a building reads the cells under its footprint. Against OSM's own heights of 6 m+ (`HEIGHT_EVAL=1` writes `.scratch/heighteval.json`), 3D-GloBFP is off by a median 2.6 m at 6-12 m, ~5 m at 12-30 m and flattens towers (-25% at 30-60, -50% over 60), so it is used first (stretched x1.4 above 22 m) and CMAB (+7.5 m on low buildings, merged outlines) only where it is missing and the footprint is 300 m² or more. **Traps** the measurements found: the learnt sets bottom out near 8 m, so every courtyard house read 8-19 m and the hutongs round 南池子 and 鼓楼 turned into mid-rise blocks - in the old city, where house-sized buildings cover over 15% of the ground within ~60 m (`hutongContext`), what they put under 20 m stays a courtyard house (its default height); a footprint under 150 m² they put over 12 m is a tall neighbour's roof traced over it (the learnt outlines are roofs; towers lean ~20 m); single-storey OSM types (bungalow, house, garage...) never take a measured height; a `levels=1` tag the measurement puts at 9 m+ (15 in the old city, 22 among the houses) is replaced. CMAB's function turns a plain `building=yes` into office/glass or low where it says office/commerce/public service or industry. **Curated**: `scripts/city/heights.json` - 77 buildings (CBD towers and malls, 工体/三里屯, 西直门) that no set covers, looked up with sources and a confidence (A published, B floors, C estimate), matched by a point inside the footprint. Result in the first play area: 17.7k -> 54.6k buildings, 15.2 -> 22.4 km² of footprint, heights with a source 1/3 -> 2/3 (the rest mostly courtyard houses at their default); no frame cost measurable at the spawn (`perf.mjs` low 7.5 -> 7.1 ms GPU, high 13.5 -> 13.4; +4-13% triangles, same draw calls). `NO_EXTRA=1` builds without any of it; `DEBUG_AT=x,z,r` prints each building's height decision; `GUESS_LOG=1` writes the office/glass buildings still guessed to `.scratch/guesslog.json`. Credits: manifest `attribution` and `CREDITS.md`. `Learnt.test.ts` checks they are there, off the carriageways, out of the palace and that 南池子 stays low. **The old city** (`region.mjs` boxes `w` and `u`: 西单, 金融街, 中南海, 北海, 什刹海, 鼓楼, 南锣鼓巷, 雍和宫, 东直门, 工体, 三里屯, to just past the 2nd Ring): 2,691 tiles, 38 MB, 115k buildings, 446 lifted bridges; the full map no longer fetches every tile (it would be 38 MB) but baked blocks of 4 x 4 tiles (`public/city/map/m_<bx>_<bz>.json`: water, green, plazas, outlines in whole metres; 185 blocks, 6.6 MB; `nav/mapWorker.ts`). Found on the way: underground buildings (a metro station's outline over its street, `layer<0`: 太平桥 across 闹市口北街, 北新桥 across 东四北大街) are skipped; park trees are no longer scattered over the lakes, pitches and squares inside a park (北海, 中南海). Drives: 北二环 安定门 -> 东直门 and 东直门 -> 复兴门 arrive; stops left are the pilot cutting ground corners (lamps, a building at a lane corner).
