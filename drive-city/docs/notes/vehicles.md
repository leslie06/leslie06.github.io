# Vehicles: bodies, models, two-wheelers, feel, damage

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-13. Dated entries say when and why (the user's words in 「」).

- `vehicle/` — eight body types: six cars (sedan/taxi, hatch, SUV, MPV, 12 m bus, box truck) built by a procedural cap-and-wall mesher, plus a motorcycle and a bicycle (`TwoWheelers.ts`, primitives: tubes, boxes, tori; `SEAT` is where the rider's pelvis goes). A two-wheeler is the same ray-cast car on a 0.32 m track (`VehicleSpec.single`: the four mounts are a narrow pair per axle, drawn as one wheel at x = 0 by `CarModel` and `CarKit`), its tyre forces act AT the centre of mass (`forceHeight` with no lever: with a lever the friction that stops a sideways creep rolled the narrow body, the rolled springs pushed it on, and it slid at a steady 0.08 m/s standing still), and the lean into corners is drawn, not simulated: `vehicle/index.ts` leans the body up to 32° with the lateral acceleration and publishes it as `VehicleApi.lean`; `player/` draws the character in the saddle with the `ride` gait (Animator: crouched over the tank, or upright and pedalling at the road speed), pelvis rolled by that lean, and hurts the player in a two-wheeler crash. The bicycle's engine is the rider's cadence (40-125 rpm, six gears, 35 km/h) and is silent (`audio/`). Traffic keeps motorcycles (`spawnBike`, `Npc.bike`; 8, or 5 on low - three were too few to come across) standing on pavements 30-200 m from the player to take with F; the only riders in traffic are on electric scooters (below). Bicycles are the shared bikes in the street racks (2026-09-25, 「自行车就骑共享单车就行」): on foot within 1.8 m of one (and nearer than any car), F takes it - `WorldApi.sharedBike` / `takeSharedBike` (city/Knock.ts `nearestBike`, which hides that instance like a knocked one) and `TrafficCars.rentBike` put a bicycle Vehicle where it stood, framed in its operator's colour; `node .scratch/sharedbike.mjs` counts the motorcycles, takes a shared bike with F and rides it off. The motorcycle's body (2026-09-27, it was a stack of boxes) is modelled in Blender: `scripts/blender/vehicles/moto.py` (written in the body frame and turned into Blender's; subdivision-surface cages for the tank, seat and tail, bevelled-curve tubes for the frame, headers and bars, an inline four with finned cylinders, a helix spring) exports a glb, and `node scripts/vehicles/import.mjs in.glb src/vehicle/models/moto.json` bakes the node transforms, turns glTF's frame into the body frame and writes the triangles per material name (integers: millimetres, hundredths, indices; 8k triangles, 203 KB) - imported statically, so `buildBody` stays synchronous and Node-safe; `TwoWheelers.ts` `addModel` maps each material name to a `Surf` (paintU/paintL are the two tone buckets, head/tail/amber lamp kinds). The wheels are still built in code. `Bodies.test.ts` covers them like the cars (0-100 in 5.7 s, 175 km/h; the bicycle 0-25 in 5.5 s); `node .scratch/bikeprobe.mjs [moto|bike]` rides one round the villa's plaza under the real loop and screenshots the rider, `node .scratch/labshot.mjs "view=lineup&types=moto,bike"` renders the models in the lab. (`Mesher.ts`, `Shell.ts`, `Bodies.ts`): faces are classified into paint, glass, trim and lamps by grid position, so seams and frames stay crisp. One kit per type, 4 draw calls each; `SPEC_OF` maps a type to its spec, and `CarLook.body` carries it through carjacking (`swapCar` rebuilds the player's model when the type changes). `src/vehicle/lab.html` renders bodies without the city. The handling. `Vehicle.ts` is a ray-cast car on a Rapier body with its own tyre model (not Rapier's DynamicRayCastVehicleController: its friction-slip tyres drop grip in one step, so slides cannot be tuned). `Spec.ts` holds every tuning number with its meaning. `ControlFilter.ts` turns keys into pedal/steer positions. `Autopilot.ts` (PathPilot) drives for tests, shots and the title screen. `CarModel.ts` builds the taxi from extruded side profiles.
- Car bodies from Blender (2026-09-28): every body but the two-wheelers is modelled by `scripts/blender/vehicles/car.py --type sedan|hatch|suv|mpv|bus|truck` (the sedan is the taxi, private car, police car and the intro's saloon). The shell is a subdivision-surface cage - half a lattice box: floor, side rows (rocker, lower door, colour split, character line, shoulder), top columns (shoulder, deck edge = glass base, rail = glass top, roof edge, crown), two end caps by Coons patch - built from per-type curves (the old `CarParams` numbers ported: `W`, `SH`, `BOT`, `DECK`, `RAIL`, `RIN`, `CROWN`, `SPLIT`; stations generated from every window, pillar and screen edge), a material per cage face, edge creases on the character line, deck edge, colour split and the end rings, mirrored and subdivided (level 2 `hi`, level 1 `lo`). Then as a mesh: the glass regions inset 12 mm into a black frame; booleans cut the wheel arches (the cutter cylinder becomes the well), the grille and lower intake pockets (the cutter's back face, with UVs, is the textured floor); the floor is deleted after the booleans (it only closed the shell) for a flat tray; lamps, seams, plates, reflectors, repeaters, the high brake lamp, SUV arch trims and the door lettering are decals - grids ray-cast onto the body from outside (lamps in angle round a point on the centre line x height, bulged a few mm; a ray that misses takes its neighbour's hit), so their outlines are crisp; mirrors, handles, wipers, fin, roof rails, spoiler are boxes. `node scripts/vehicles/import.mjs .scratch/blender/<t>.glb .scratch/blender/<t>.json --group --simplify hi=0.4,lo=0.7` (the bus `lo=0.45`), then `node scripts/vehicles/pack.mjs .scratch/blender/<t>.json public/models/vehicles/<t>.bin` (meshopt-encoded vertex and index buffers behind a JSON header; `Mesher.unpackModel` reads it back: 1.05 MB for the six bodies and the wheels against 5.9 MB of JSON, ~0.95 MB on the wire where the JSON chunks were 1.3 MB) - `--simplify` runs meshoptimizer per material with `LockBorder`, so colour splits and glass edges stay exact (Blender's Decimate chewed the two-tone line into teeth and wrinkled the glass; shipping no normals and recomputing them in the game left blotches: both tried and dropped). `Bodies.ts` `buildModelBody` maps material names to the palette's Surfs (per-type atlas graphics in `MODELS`), measures the roof for the roof sign / light bar on `hi` (`lo` is too sparse: the first cut put the sign at -Infinity and the city logged NaN bounding spheres), and takes headlamp centres and size from the model. They are fetched, not bundled: `readyBodies()` (Node reads them from disk) (retry, and a body that never arrives is built procedurally) is started first thing in `main.ts` and awaited before the world; tests and the lab await it too. `?cars=old` builds the procedural bodies (and wheels) for A/B. The bus and truck use the same cage with their own section (`B["section"]`, eleven side rows: skirt, stripe, belt, window band, roof band), wall and end-cap materials (`wall_mat`, `cap_mat`, which also gives the cap cells UVs: the destination sign, bus lamps, rear vent, the truck's head lamps and grille), lean and details (`B["details"]`): the bus's kerb-side doors are decals on the -X side only (the cage is mirrored), the truck's cargo box, chassis, tank and lamp bar are parts, its lettering is on the box (`door_fn`). A sign that reads across the centre line takes the other half of its texture on the mirrored half (a UV flip after the Mirror modifier; the mesh is in game coordinates, so the mirrored half is x < 0). The wheels are `scripts/blender/vehicles/wheel.py` -> `wheels.bin` (`alloy_hi/lo`, `steel_hi/lo`, at one nominal size each, scaled per spec by `modelWheel`): tyres with grooves and a lettering band, twin-spoke alloys with nuts and a brake disc, dished steel wheels with hand holes and a ten-nut hub; the low levels are ~300 triangles like the old ones, or the bus's traffic budget (six wheels) breaks. **Trap**: a decal ray that misses everything lands where its origin was - the sedan's high brake lamp, cast from 2 m behind with a 6 m ray, never reached the rear screen and hung 2 m behind every taxi as a red streak (seen at night); rays now reach 16 m, and a scan of every material's extent against the paint's catches strays. Figures: full detail 29.2k -> 22.7k triangles (sedan with wheels), traffic kits 2.8-3.1k a car as before, frame time unchanged (`PARAMS=cars=old node .scratch/rail/frameab.mjs <q>`). `.scratch/car/run.sh tag types...` builds, imports and shoots the lab; the lab takes `?cam=fx,fy,fz>tx,ty,tz` in any view.
- Feel (2026-09-24): a crash over impact 6 hit-stops the world (`engine.timeScale` 0.25 for 40-130 ms of wall clock, never in shot mode), the camera shake is sharper (`/7`, decay 2.4) and the gamepad rumbles on impacts and landings (`Input.rumble`, `vibrationActuator`).
- Electric scooters and delivery riders (2026-10-03, the fourth item of the next-steps list): a ninth body, `ebike` (电动车: `Spec.ts` `EBIKE`, a BIKE with 16-inch wheels, a hub motor on one ratio, 45 km/h, 130 kg laden, 0-40 in 3.9 s; `TwoWheelers.ts` `ebike`, boxes and tubes: leg shield, footboard, long seat, front cowl, bar cover with the headlamp, mudguards, a top box on the rack - the body takes the upper tone, the box the lower), silent like the bicycle (`audio/`), ridden upright with the feet on the board (`Animator` ride `'ebike'`). Traffic rides them (`spawnRider`, `RIDERS` 4/7/10 by tier, never on motorways, trunks or their ramps): `AiDriver.kerb` keeps a rider 0.75-1.15 m in from the right-hand kerb and `vmax` caps it (delivery 29-40 km/h, others 20-31); 55% are delivery riders (a 美团 yellow or 饿了么 blue box, jacket and cap as the helmet, mostly men, some masked), the rest carry a dark or pale case. The riders are drawn in the player's crowd after the player (`TrafficApi.riderCap` comes off the pedestrians' room), within 160 m; anything harder than a nudge, or the scooter going over, throws the rider off (`unseat`: `spawnFleeing`, the scooter left lying), and F takes it like any car. `leaderFor` measures the gap across by each body's half width (0.4 for a two-wheeler), so cars pass a rider where there is room. The ebike kit's cap includes the riders. `node .scratch/riders/ridershot.mjs outPrefix [n] [params]` shoots the nearest riders from beside.
- `damage/` — per-car wear from `vehicle:impact`. One collision fires an impact every step while the car scrapes, so only the first of a 0.35 s burst counts; hits under 8 m/s of unexplained Δv are free, one point a m/s over that, and no single crash takes more than 20 of 100 (2026-10-02, 「车撞几下就冒烟，发动机坏了」: it was (Δv - 6) x 2.2 up to 30, so three 50 km/h knocks had it smoking; now ~10 before it smokes, at under 25, and ~14 before the engine dies). Health drives `VehicleApi.power` (1 / 0.8 under 20 / 0.55 under 8 / 0.3 dead), so a wrecked car still limps. `.scratch/m4probe.mjs`'s dead-engine step fires 12 impacts of 18. Player health lives in `player/`: cars hurt on foot, regenerates to half, 0 is WASTED and a hospital respawn (协和医院) with a bill.
