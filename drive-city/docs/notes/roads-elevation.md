- Dead ends (2026-09-26, 「我把车开到了故宫，发现进了死胡同，开不出去了」): nothing said where a road stopped. `city/DeadEnds.ts` `findDeadEnds` peels the drivable graph (drop every node with one road, repeat): what is left is the core where every road goes on, what was peeled is branches hanging off it, and `out[edge]` is each branch edge's end towards its mouth. Every mouth whose branch holds >= 50 m of road (`MIN_BRANCH`), does not run within 60 m of the data's edge (open ground there) and can be driven into gets a GB 5768 此路不通 sign (blue, white stem, red bar, bilingual) on the right kerb just past the junction, the left if that is another carriageway: 405 in the city, 38 ms at boot. `city/visual/DeadEndSigns.ts` draws them as one instanced mesh (canvas atlas, faint glow at night) with a thin post collider each, and is system `deadEnds` (`DeadEndApi` in Contracts: `inward(from, to)`, `signs`). R (`backOnRoad`) uses it to face the way out of a branch (the lane heading in costs 12 m; a one-way heading in is taken backwards), and the HUD says 「卡住了？按 R 回到路上」 after 3 s of throttle or reverse going nowhere. **Trap:** the palace's service roads are not joined to the city's graph at all (37 components; the one the report came from is 6 nodes, a 1 km lane from the north-west corner down the west side, stopping between two groves) - it is signed at its own little core. `DeadEnds.test.ts` covers the peeling on a hand-built graph and the real city (the palace lane signed, no post on any carriageway); `node .scratch/deadendprobe.mjs [x,z,yaw]` presses R facing into the palace lane and prints the new heading, `node .scratch/stuckhint.mjs` holds the throttle against a palace wall in the real loop until the hint shows.
- Interchanges (立交, 2026-09-25, 「地图都是平面，没有立交桥的立式建筑」): OSM draws them flat with `bridge`/`layer`, and nothing read `br`. `scripts/city/build.mjs` now lifts every car bridge that crosses another kept road or a railway (229; river bridges stay flat): 6.5 m over what it crosses, stacked by layer (东三环's main line runs at 13 m over 国贸桥), bridge ways joined to a lifted one follow it (a viaduct's other spans), and the heights are solved on every car way's points at <= 10 m: down from the decks at 6% (`GRADE`, a max-plus Dijkstra), the crossed road pinned at 0 within 35 m of the crossing, nothing climbing faster than 12% (`STEEP`) from a pin - decks included, or where OSM joins a bridge straight onto the road it crosses (四惠) the deck fell 6.5 m in 9 m. A slip road overlapping a deck where it leaves or joins holds the deck's height until clear (`hugged`) - it used to start down at the shared node, run 2 m under the deck's edge and close the merge with parapets. Written as `h` per point on road pieces and network edges (absent when flat); 90.7 km of road above ground. Lamps and trees are kept off and out from under the decks (a skipped tree still spends its seed, so every other tree keeps its species), buildings a deck passes through are dropped (7), signals/crossings/stops under a deck skipped. Runtime: `Roads.ts` lifts every band by `hAt`, skips pavements on `lifted` stretches and builds `bridgeOf`: parapets, deck edge and soffit, filled embankment walls under 3.2 m (`DECK.fill`), a wall across where one turns into the other, piers every 26 m that never land on a road below, lamps on the parapet every 32 m (`deckLamps`, [x, y, z, yaw], drawn and splatted like the ground's), and the colliders (deck top from where it leaves the ground, parapets, walls, piers) as a second trimesh per tile tagged `bridge`, surface asphalt; the mesh is `bridge` (concrete, casts shadows). A parapet opens wherever another raised carriageway covers it at a similar height (slip roads, merges); **trap:** a tile only knows its own pieces, so each piece is extended by its `a`/`b` neighbour points for that test, or the stretch of 国贸桥 across the tile edge x 4864 was invisible and the slip road's parapet stood straight across the main line. `LaneGraph` links carry `h`/`hmax`/`heightAt`; traffic spawns at the lane's height only once the deck's collider is in (a ray; else it skips the spot), `leaderFor` ignores anything 3 m above or below; no pedestrians, pavement kerbs, fares, addresses, parked bikes or kerb furniture on lifted links; no signal heads at a node over 3 m (7 deck junctions, excluded in `Order.test.ts`). `Interchange.test.ts` checks 国贸桥/建国门桥/东三环's heights, no ramp over 13%, and ray-casts across 国贸桥 at the tile edge for a wall (with the neighbour extension removed it finds one). Probes: `PARAMS=notraffic node .scratch/bridgedrive.mjs x0,z0,x1,z1 [speed] [out.png]` drives a real route with the pilot (densified path) and prints the car's height and what it touched when it stops (start points under a deck pick the wrong level - start clear of one); `node .scratch/bridgetraffic.mjs x,z [s]` checks traffic cars' heights against the lanes; views with `.scratch/lampview.mjs` (from 东三环's ground at `"4935,3,430" "4950,11,300"`, the loop ramp from `"4990,40,400" "4966,11,335"`).

- Pitfall (walls, blocks and gaps all over the interchanges, 2026-09-25, 「高架桥不是通向墙就是构造有问题」): the height rules above were applied one after another (slip roads hug a deck, junctions off the ground made level, roads alongside raised to the higher), each undoing the last, so side streets rode up and down beside the ramps (建国门外大街's side road past 国贸: 0.9 - 2.6 - 0 m; 天坛东路's 0 - 5.9 - 2.4 - 3.5 m) and three conflicts were built into the geometry. The elevation in `scripts/city/build.mjs` is now one model: (1) roads that meet and overlap are **tied** - a zero-length edge in the height graph from each shared node for as long as one is half a metre inside the other's carriageway, spliced in at the exact point across (`denseAt`; tying to the nearest dense point left 0.3 m steps on a 6% ramp) - which replaces hug/flatJ/sideFix; (2) `solve()` takes the higher of GRADE down from the decks *along* the roads (never across a tie, except off a deck) and what the decks need at STEEP (across everything), then `consistent()` makes every point at most STEEP above a neighbour and tied points level, the lower winning - including the ground points absent from HT (the first cut forgot them and left 3.7 m in 1 m); (3) tie points that the decks do not need up are pinned to the ground (merge zones stay at grade, the ramps climb outside them), and so are dead ends (a bridge cut off at the data edge came down to nothing 6.5 m up); (4) carriageways alongside at different heights that overlap are **narrowed** to what their lanes can spare (`minWidth`, lanes counted as LaneGraph does) - OSM draws a ramp and its side road ~9 m apart and our width estimates overlapped them; two ramps still overlapping are tied, a ramp beside a road on the ground is brought down there where the decks allow; (5) decks crossing decks are restacked a DECK apart by layer after the joined spans are known (朝阳路 and 东四环 crossed on the level 6.5 m up); (6) a short constrained smoothing of the raised stretches removes zig-zags; (7) what still overlaps alongside is **moved aside** - the road that gives way (the lower, unless it is a deck) is shifted sideways by the overlap, easing in and out over 30 m, at most 5 m in all, and every way through a moved node moves with it (a moved way's `_pts`/`_ids` become its dense points, non-node ids as `x<key>`, arc lengths recomputed; 358 points, median 0.7 m): 105 conflicting points -> 15, the rest crossings. `ELEV_CONFLICTS=1` prints the measure: pairs overlapping in plan 0.3 to 5.6 m apart in height (`overlapRows`, height interpolated at the projection), by kind, residual classes, raised dead ends and kinks, and writes `.scratch/conflicts.txt` - 581 points at 166 sites before, 105 at 32 now; `CONF_AT=x,z`, `DBG_KEY=key,...`, `ELEV_KEYS=1` with `ELEV_WAY` dig into one. In `Roads.ts`: a parapet opens only where the other carriageway reaches past its outer face (tested at oo + 0.3, not "within 0.8 m", which left a 1.2 m slot between two carriageways of 东三环/国贸桥 to fall through), or where it would stand on another carriageway at its level; it grows out of the ramp from 0.45 to 1.45 m instead of starting full height in the lane of the road the ramp leaves; where two roads part (a carriageway at the same level up to 2.4 m beyond) the wedge between them is paved (`GORE`, drawn and solid) and each parapet starts as a nose rising over `NOSE` 6 m, paved underneath out to the other road while it is low (a nose with nothing at its level beyond stays full height); coverage counts only beside another road, not off its end (two links leaving the end of 通惠河北路 opened onto the hole between them); none over a road only a step (0.3-1.5 m) lower; no embankment end wall or pier cross-head inside any carriageway's headroom (`occupied`, `HEADROOM` 4.3). The audit: `npx vitest run --config .scratch/sweep/vitest.config.ts .scratch/sweep/bridgeaudit.test.ts` samples every car road across its whole width in every tile with raised roads against the drawn bridge and the colliders of its 3x3 tiles and writes `.scratch/bridgeaudit.txt` with a camera pair per finding: block (anything 0.4-1.6 m over a carriageway) 2754 -> 114 samples, low (1.6-4.3 m) 1668 -> 63 (decks near their ends with ~4 m under them), wall (across a lane at 0.6 m, with the triangle hit) 441 -> 11, open edge 403 -> 35, against the build deployed before this work. `.scratch/shots6.sh out.png "from" "to" ...` shoots up to six of those cameras into one sheet; `node .scratch/routeshots.mjs outDir "x0,z0,x1,z1" ...` drives routes with the pilot and screenshots the chase camera every 3 s into a contact sheet; `.scratch/sweep/whose.test.ts` (`AT=tile,x,y,z`, writes `.scratch/whose.txt`) names the pieces at a point.

- Underpasses (下穿, 2026-10-02, the last of step 4): a car road's `tunnel=yes` (not service roads - car park ramps - or living streets) and car roads at `layer<0` are no longer dropped (`roadInfo` marks them `tunnel`/`below`; `NO_TUNNELS=1` for the old build).
