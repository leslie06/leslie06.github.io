# b城追车 / B CITY CHASE (folder: drive-city) — GTA-style open world in Beijing (three.js)

Design doc: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f84d740e-9e09-4de4-8d47-41c433772ad6 (milestones M0–M5).
Milestones: M0 vehicle sandbox done (handling critic 7.0/10). M1–M3 built: central Beijing from OSM (`?world=city` is the default in `main.ts`; any other value, e.g. `?world=yard`, gives the M0 sandbox, which is also the only world where the `ui_hud` / `ui_menu` poses are registered), tile streaming, traffic, getting out and carjacking, pedestrians. M4 in progress: wanted level and police (`police/`), minimap/map/GPS (`nav/`), then missions.

## Stack
- Vite 8 + TypeScript (strict), three r185, @dimforge/rapier3d-compat 0.20. Dev server: `npx vite --port 5195`. Typecheck: `npx tsc --noEmit`.
- `npm install` needs `--legacy-peer-deps` (npm 10's arborist crashes on vitest's optional peers).
- No runtime network requests for content: textures are drawn on canvases (`world/Textures.ts`), sound is Web Audio synthesis (`audio/`). The one exception is the optional leaderboard (`online/Leaderboard.ts`), which never blocks anything.

## Architecture (src/)
- `core/` — copied from gunFight and trimmed: Engine (fixed 60 Hz `fixedUpdate` → `physics.step()` → `postStep`, then per-frame `update(dt, alpha)`), Input (keyboard GTA-V layout + standard gamepad), Physics (Rapier; groups `CG.WORLD/CAR/PROP`), Quality (tiers low/medium/high from the bound GPU), Resolution governor, I18n (zh/en; every on-screen string goes through `t()`), Assets.compact (releases canvas sources after upload).
- `game/Contracts.ts` — the only cross-module interfaces (VehicleApi, CameraApi, WorldApi, HudApi). Extend additively.
- Boot order in `main.ts`: render → world (yard or city) → vehicle → player (camera, on foot) → traffic → people → police → nav → missions → races → park → home → garage → underground → stunts (+ jumps, shortcuts) → collect → checkin → trials → intro → events → leaderboard → story → fx → audio → ui (city-only modules skip in the yard). `install()` may only `engine.get` earlier modules; later ones are looked up lazily at runtime.
- `vehicle/` — eight body types: six cars (sedan/taxi, hatch, SUV, MPV, 12 m bus, box truck) built by a procedural cap-and-wall mesher, plus a motorcycle and a bicycle (`TwoWheelers.ts`, primitives: tubes, boxes, tori; `SEAT` is where the rider's pelvis goes). A two-wheeler is the same ray-cast car on a 0.32 m track (`VehicleSpec.single`: the four mounts are a narrow pair per axle, drawn as one wheel at x = 0 by `CarModel` and `CarKit`), its tyre forces act AT the centre of mass (`forceHeight` with no lever: with a lever the friction that stops a sideways creep rolled the narrow body, the rolled springs pushed it on, and it slid at a steady 0.08 m/s standing still), and the lean into corners is drawn, not simulated: `vehicle/index.ts` leans the body up to 32° with the lateral acceleration and publishes it as `VehicleApi.lean`; `player/` draws the character in the saddle with the `ride` gait (Animator: crouched over the tank, or upright and pedalling at the road speed), pelvis rolled by that lean, and hurts the player in a two-wheeler crash. The bicycle's engine is the rider's cadence (40-125 rpm, six gears, 35 km/h) and is silent (`audio/`). Traffic keeps motorcycles (`spawnBike`, `Npc.bike`; 8, or 5 on low - three were too few to come across) standing on pavements 30-200 m from the player to take with F; the only riders in traffic are on electric scooters (below). Bicycles are the shared bikes in the street racks (2026-09-25, 「自行车就骑共享单车就行」): on foot within 1.8 m of one (and nearer than any car), F takes it - `WorldApi.sharedBike` / `takeSharedBike` (city/Knock.ts `nearestBike`, which hides that instance like a knocked one) and `TrafficCars.rentBike` put a bicycle Vehicle where it stood, framed in its operator's colour; `node .scratch/sharedbike.mjs` counts the motorcycles, takes a shared bike with F and rides it off. The motorcycle's body (2026-09-27, it was a stack of boxes) is modelled in Blender: `scripts/blender/vehicles/moto.py` (written in the body frame and turned into Blender's; subdivision-surface cages for the tank, seat and tail, bevelled-curve tubes for the frame, headers and bars, an inline four with finned cylinders, a helix spring) exports a glb, and `node scripts/vehicles/import.mjs in.glb src/vehicle/models/moto.json` bakes the node transforms, turns glTF's frame into the body frame and writes the triangles per material name (integers: millimetres, hundredths, indices; 8k triangles, 203 KB) - imported statically, so `buildBody` stays synchronous and Node-safe; `TwoWheelers.ts` `addModel` maps each material name to a `Surf` (paintU/paintL are the two tone buckets, head/tail/amber lamp kinds). The wheels are still built in code. `Bodies.test.ts` covers them like the cars (0-100 in 5.7 s, 175 km/h; the bicycle 0-25 in 5.5 s); `node .scratch/bikeprobe.mjs [moto|bike]` rides one round the villa's plaza under the real loop and screenshots the rider, `node .scratch/labshot.mjs "view=lineup&types=moto,bike"` renders the models in the lab. (`Mesher.ts`, `Shell.ts`, `Bodies.ts`): faces are classified into paint, glass, trim and lamps by grid position, so seams and frames stay crisp. One kit per type, 4 draw calls each; `SPEC_OF` maps a type to its spec, and `CarLook.body` carries it through carjacking (`swapCar` rebuilds the player's model when the type changes). `src/vehicle/lab.html` renders bodies without the city. The handling. `Vehicle.ts` is a ray-cast car on a Rapier body with its own tyre model (not Rapier's DynamicRayCastVehicleController: its friction-slip tyres drop grip in one step, so slides cannot be tuned). `Spec.ts` holds every tuning number with its meaning. `ControlFilter.ts` turns keys into pedal/steer positions. `Autopilot.ts` (PathPilot) drives for tests, shots and the title screen. `CarModel.ts` builds the taxi from extruded side profiles.
- Car bodies from Blender (2026-09-28): every body but the two-wheelers is modelled by `scripts/blender/vehicles/car.py --type sedan|hatch|suv|mpv|bus|truck` (the sedan is the taxi, private car, police car and the intro's saloon). The shell is a subdivision-surface cage - half a lattice box: floor, side rows (rocker, lower door, colour split, character line, shoulder), top columns (shoulder, deck edge = glass base, rail = glass top, roof edge, crown), two end caps by Coons patch - built from per-type curves (the old `CarParams` numbers ported: `W`, `SH`, `BOT`, `DECK`, `RAIL`, `RIN`, `CROWN`, `SPLIT`; stations generated from every window, pillar and screen edge), a material per cage face, edge creases on the character line, deck edge, colour split and the end rings, mirrored and subdivided (level 2 `hi`, level 1 `lo`). Then as a mesh: the glass regions inset 12 mm into a black frame; booleans cut the wheel arches (the cutter cylinder becomes the well), the grille and lower intake pockets (the cutter's back face, with UVs, is the textured floor); the floor is deleted after the booleans (it only closed the shell) for a flat tray; lamps, seams, plates, reflectors, repeaters, the high brake lamp, SUV arch trims and the door lettering are decals - grids ray-cast onto the body from outside (lamps in angle round a point on the centre line x height, bulged a few mm; a ray that misses takes its neighbour's hit), so their outlines are crisp; mirrors, handles, wipers, fin, roof rails, spoiler are boxes. `node scripts/vehicles/import.mjs .scratch/blender/<t>.glb .scratch/blender/<t>.json --group --simplify hi=0.4,lo=0.7` (the bus `lo=0.45`), then `node scripts/vehicles/pack.mjs .scratch/blender/<t>.json public/models/vehicles/<t>.bin` (meshopt-encoded vertex and index buffers behind a JSON header; `Mesher.unpackModel` reads it back: 1.05 MB for the six bodies and the wheels against 5.9 MB of JSON, ~0.95 MB on the wire where the JSON chunks were 1.3 MB) - `--simplify` runs meshoptimizer per material with `LockBorder`, so colour splits and glass edges stay exact (Blender's Decimate chewed the two-tone line into teeth and wrinkled the glass; shipping no normals and recomputing them in the game left blotches: both tried and dropped). `Bodies.ts` `buildModelBody` maps material names to the palette's Surfs (per-type atlas graphics in `MODELS`), measures the roof for the roof sign / light bar on `hi` (`lo` is too sparse: the first cut put the sign at -Infinity and the city logged NaN bounding spheres), and takes headlamp centres and size from the model. They are fetched, not bundled: `readyBodies()` (Node reads them from disk) (retry, and a body that never arrives is built procedurally) is started first thing in `main.ts` and awaited before the world; tests and the lab await it too. `?cars=old` builds the procedural bodies (and wheels) for A/B. The bus and truck use the same cage with their own section (`B["section"]`, eleven side rows: skirt, stripe, belt, window band, roof band), wall and end-cap materials (`wall_mat`, `cap_mat`, which also gives the cap cells UVs: the destination sign, bus lamps, rear vent, the truck's head lamps and grille), lean and details (`B["details"]`): the bus's kerb-side doors are decals on the -X side only (the cage is mirrored), the truck's cargo box, chassis, tank and lamp bar are parts, its lettering is on the box (`door_fn`). A sign that reads across the centre line takes the other half of its texture on the mirrored half (a UV flip after the Mirror modifier; the mesh is in game coordinates, so the mirrored half is x < 0). The wheels are `scripts/blender/vehicles/wheel.py` -> `wheels.bin` (`alloy_hi/lo`, `steel_hi/lo`, at one nominal size each, scaled per spec by `modelWheel`): tyres with grooves and a lettering band, twin-spoke alloys with nuts and a brake disc, dished steel wheels with hand holes and a ten-nut hub; the low levels are ~300 triangles like the old ones, or the bus's traffic budget (six wheels) breaks. **Trap**: a decal ray that misses everything lands where its origin was - the sedan's high brake lamp, cast from 2 m behind with a 6 m ray, never reached the rear screen and hung 2 m behind every taxi as a red streak (seen at night); rays now reach 16 m, and a scan of every material's extent against the paint's catches strays. Figures: full detail 29.2k -> 22.7k triangles (sedan with wheels), traffic kits 2.8-3.1k a car as before, frame time unchanged (`PARAMS=cars=old node .scratch/rail/frameab.mjs <q>`). `.scratch/car/run.sh tag types...` builds, imports and shoots the lab; the lab takes `?cam=fx,fy,fz>tx,ty,tz` in any view.
- `world/` — the yard: `Layout.ts` (all positions, shared with poses/tests), paint, props (instanced + small dynamic bodies), structures, skyline.
- Feel (2026-09-24): a crash over impact 6 hit-stops the world (`engine.timeScale` 0.25 for 40-130 ms of wall clock, never in shot mode), the camera shake is sharper (`/7`, decay 2.4) and the gamepad rumbles on impacts and landings (`Input.rumble`, `vibrationActuator`).
- `render/` — sky and time of day (`?tod=17.5`, `?rain=0.8`, `?timescale=N`), CSM shadows, post chain (AO, motion blur, bloom, AgX, SMAA: canvas MSAA is off), rain. Materials opt into the wet look with `userData.wet = 'ground'` (roads: puddles), `'surface'` (paint, glass) or `true` (cloth); `uniforms.uNight` drives lamps, windows and headlights. `RenderSystem.prepare(root)` patches a streamed tile before it is added.
- Street lights (2026-09-25, 「晚上的时候，没有路灯，路面特别黑」): lamps were only on tertiary roads and up, so every residential street and hutong was black, and even where they stood the heads only glowed - nothing lit the road. `scripts/city/build.mjs` now puts lamps on every car road but service roads (`LAMPS`: spacing per class, 24-36 m, starting half a gap in so they do not bunch at corners, skipping spots within 1.2 m of a street tree): 18,334 -> 32,457, nothing else in the tiles changed. It also fixes the arm yaw, which was taken after `side` flipped and pointed every two-way street's arms away from the road. The light itself is `render/StreetLights.ts`: the heads the streamer has loaded (`RenderSystem.setStreetLamps`, pushed from `CityStreamer.pushHeads`) are splatted as additive quads into a 512/1024 RGBA16F top-down map of +-256 m round the camera (R = sum f/d^2, GB = the same weighted by the offset to each head), redrawn only when the set changes or the camera moves 48 m, and `Lighting`'s material scan injects one extra `RE_Direct` into every lit material from it (diffuse + specular, roughness floored at 0.3; no shadows; `|dc-lamp` in the program key). Strength `INTENSITY` 36 with the night exposure at 6.5; `?lamps=0.5` scales it, `?lamps=0` for A/B. The splat quad needs `DoubleSide` (the top-down view mirrors its winding, and a culled splat leaves the map silently empty), and it must be a plain Mesh on an InstancedBufferGeometry, not an InstancedMesh. Probes: `node .scratch/lampview.mjs out.png "cx,cy,cz" "lx,ly,lz" [params]` shoots any camera at night (it sets the camera before preloading and yields between ticks: a synchronous tick loop with the camera still at the spawn unloads everything just preloaded, and worker results never arrive), `AT=x,z node .scratch/lampdebug.mjs [params]` prints the map's texels at the nearest heads (1/64 = 0.0156 right under one), `node .scratch/nightreal.mjs "tod=1&rain=0.8&quality=low" out.png` the real game loop from the title. The low tier is where night is darkest; rain hides the moon and is the case the report came from.
- Street trees from Blender (2026-09-27): the four species (国槐, poplar, 侧柏, ginkgo) are grown in Blender and swapped in over the procedural ones (`city/visual/Foliage.ts`, still built at boot, synchronously, with no network; `?trees=old` keeps them). The pipeline: `scripts/blender/trees/leaves.py` models a sprig per species (pinnate leaflets, the poplar's triangular leaves with silver backs, 侧柏's scale-leaf fronds, ginkgo fans on spurs) and renders it straight down with Cycles; `node scripts/trees/atlas.mjs --no-impostors .scratch/trees/atlas-leaves.png` packs them; `scripts/blender/trees/trees.py` grows each tree (a trunk forking into scaffolds for the round 国槐, a leader with scaffold branches for the others, each branch forking to depth 3 inside the old model's crown envelope) and hangs leaf cards over the crown's shell and at the branch tips, near and mid levels, renders each near tree from the side as the impostor and exports `.scratch/trees/trees.glb`; `node scripts/trees/atlas.mjs` writes the game atlas `public/models/trees/foliage.webp` (2048 x 1024: sprites on top at 82% - they were lit in Cycles and the game lights them again - impostors and bark below, colour bled into the transparent texels; 454 KB); `node scripts/vehicles/import.mjs .scratch/trees/trees.glb public/models/trees/trees.json --group` (objects `<species>_<level>_v<k>__bark|leaf`; fetched with the atlas, 1 MB, not bundled). Three variants per species (their own skeletons, crowns 10% wider or taller) live in one geometry with an `aVariant` attribute: the foliage vertex shader keeps the one the instance's position hashes to and collapses the rest (`uTreeVariants`; -1 on the procedural trees and the impostors is kept by all) - variety for no draw calls. **But the collapsed variants still run the vertex stage** and count as triangles: over 天坛's park (400 near cypresses, 2800 mid) a high-tier frame was 6.9M triangles and 23 ms. On medium and high each species' near and mid pools are now split per variant (`Streamer.ts` `variantOf(x, z)` picks a tree's pool; `variantGeometry` keeps that variant's triangles, marked -1, and `uTreeVariants` goes to 1): the park 6.9M -> 2.7M triangles, 22.7 -> 18 ms; the spawn 1.70M -> 1.27M, same ms; +2 draw calls a pool. Low keeps one pool (its draw calls cost more than triangles there). The variant code is always compiled, not behind a define: `render/Lighting` freezes a material's program key when it first patches it, so a key that changed at the swap would reuse the old program. `city/visual/BlenderTrees.ts` builds the geometries (Foliage.ts's attributes: crown-centre leaf normals, an AO in the vertex colour, `aSway`) and two crossed quads per impostor for the far level; `CityStreamer.treesReady` resolves once the pools' geometries and the material's map are swapped. Traps: glTF UVs run v down, so the importer keeps them as they are and the atlas loads with `flipY = false` (flipping twice put the bark on the leaves); the foliage shader's mip-alpha boost now reads the map's size (`textureSize`), not a square constant; the sprigs' first renders covered only 19-33% of their cells and from the air the crowns showed the pale paving through them - denser sprigs, 29-46%. 1.1k-1.4k triangles a near tree (x3 for the variants in the vertex stage); the same draw calls; A/B (`.scratch/trees/treecost.mjs quality from to`) high +0.4 ms, low +0.1. Tree pits (树池, 2026-09-29): the tiles do not say which trees are street trees, but a tree standing in a car road's pavement (between 0.4 m past the kerb and the pavement's back edge, `Roads.ts` `pavementTrees`, the neighbours' carriageways from `ctx` included - a tree often lines a road the next tile holds) is one; `buildRoads` draws a square pit under each as paint on the pavement (`treePit`: a granite kerb round dark soil and two cast-iron grate halves, turned to the road). ~90 % of the street trees get one, parks none; +~2 ms a tile in the worker; no city rebuild.

- Street furniture from Blender (2026-09-28): `scripts/blender/props/street.py` (reuses `roof.py`'s `Part`) models the shared bike (step-through frame, front basket, thin mudguards, chain cover, the smart lock and QR plate, three-spoke moulded wheels, kickstand; `frame` is white so the instance colour paints the operator's colour), the pair of sorting bins (tapered bodies, lids with lip and handle, labels, pedals, back wheels) and the bus shelter (posts with raked arms, an arched canopy - a closed slab, or its faces came out facing down - and its lit soffit, framed glass back and end, the lightbox, the route board, a slatted bench, the stop's name) -> `node scripts/vehicles/import.mjs .scratch/blender/street.glb src/city/visual/street.json --group`; `FurnitureGeo.ts` `streetGeometry` colours each material (`STREET_LOOK`, with night glow). They are drawn near only - bikes within `BIKE_NEAR` 50 m, bins 45 m, shelters 160 m (`Streamer.ts`) - and the old box versions in their own pools beyond (`pool:furn-*-far`): the 300-triangle bin pair everywhere within 150 m doubled the bins' triangles. Knocked things fly as the near model; the knock pools draw only up to their highest slot in use (`Knock.fitCount`: 16 empty Blender bikes scaled to nothing were 29k triangles a frame), and the far pools cast no shadow (three draw calls on low). - Roof-top things from Blender (2026-09-27): `scripts/blender/props/roof.py` models seven low-poly things in the game's frame - a water tank on its I-beam stand with a ladder, a 太阳能热水器 (evacuated tubes as prisms under the long tank), an AC condenser with its fan grille, an office cooling tower, a satellite dish, an antenna mast, an exhaust fan (32-184 triangles each) - converted by `scripts/vehicles/import.mjs ... --group` into `city/visual/roofprops.json` (per thing, per material name; `Buildings.ts` `ROOF_COLOURS` colours them, `model()` places one; tanks take a tint on their 'tank' material). `clutter` (tile worker) now lays them out the way Beijing roofs are: on the housing, rows of solar heaters facing south along the long axis on the south half and a row of condensers along the north parapet, tanks, dishes; on the offices, cooling towers, blocks of condensers and exhaust fans; masts anywhere; stair houses stay in the facade mesh (a tank on some). They are their own mesh per tile, `roofprops`, with a plain vertex-colour material (`CityMaterials.roofprops`) and their own bucket (`PropBucket`: position, normal, colour in growing typed arrays, a vertex per model vertex) - written through the facade's Sink, 21 floats a vertex a triangle at a time, they made a tile's buildings five times slower to build (3 -> 18 ms; now 1.5-4 ms, `npx tsx .scratch/roof/buildtime.mts`) and the extra CPU tipped `Crowd.test.ts`'s 1 ms timing test in the full suite. Drawn only on the near 3x3 tiles, on low only on the focus tile, no shadows; the far tiles show none - the old boxes are gone (a condenser is under two pixels from there, and keeping boxes for the far tiles cost a draw call per tile). A/B with `?roofprops=0` (`.scratch/roof/roofcost.mjs quality from to`): from the air high +0.1-0.2 ms and +58k triangles, on the street high +0.15-0.3, low ~+0.1.
- Knocking street furniture (2026-09-25, 「开车碰到垃圾箱、共享单车、公路围栏没声效和撞击画面」): bins, shared bikes and railings are instanced pools with no colliders (thousands per tile), so the car went through them in silence. `city/Knock.ts` (`StreetKnocks`, owned by the streamer as `streamer.knocks`, added as its own system) tests the player's chassis footprint (from `spec.chassis`) against the raw furniture of the 3x3 tiles round the car every fixed step (each kind a few circles in its own frame; above 1.2 m/s). A hit hides that instance (`isHidden`; the streamer refills its furniture pools when `knocks.version` changes), puts a dynamic PROP body of the same shape there with the car's velocity plus a shove and a tumble, throws debris (bin rubbish, chips) and HDR sparks off metal above 6 m/s, a dust puff through `fx.smoke`, and emits `prop:hit` - audio synthesises a hollow bonk and rattle (bin), a clatter and the bell (bike), a clang of inharmonic partials and a scrape (rail); the camera jolts a little. It deliberately does not emit `vehicle:impact` (damage, the delivery job's spill, hit-stop). Knocked things go at 150 m and the instance comes back at 250 m. Bus shelters are fixed instead: `addBody` gives each two boxes from the tile's raw furniture (the back row of posts, glass, lightbox and bench; the canopy, 2.54 m up, so a car passes under it and a bus does not), tagged `shelter` - a real crash; `node .scratch/shelterprobe.mjs [outDir] [speed]` drives into one through its open front and into its end. Probes: `SIDE=1 KINDS=bin,bike,rail node .scratch/knockprobe.mjs outDir [speed] [params]` coasts the car into the nearest of each and screenshots it (shot mode starts with no detail tiles: it ticks 90 frames first), `node .scratch/knocksound.mjs` finds the master bus by hooking `AudioNode.connect` and reads each knock's level.
- Dead ends (2026-09-26, 「我把车开到了故宫，发现进了死胡同，开不出去了」): nothing said where a road stopped. `city/DeadEnds.ts` `findDeadEnds` peels the drivable graph (drop every node with one road, repeat): what is left is the core where every road goes on, what was peeled is branches hanging off it, and `out[edge]` is each branch edge's end towards its mouth. Every mouth whose branch holds >= 50 m of road (`MIN_BRANCH`), does not run within 60 m of the data's edge (open ground there) and can be driven into gets a GB 5768 此路不通 sign (blue, white stem, red bar, bilingual) on the right kerb just past the junction, the left if that is another carriageway: 405 in the city, 38 ms at boot. `city/visual/DeadEndSigns.ts` draws them as one instanced mesh (canvas atlas, faint glow at night) with a thin post collider each, and is system `deadEnds` (`DeadEndApi` in Contracts: `inward(from, to)`, `signs`). R (`backOnRoad`) uses it to face the way out of a branch (the lane heading in costs 12 m; a one-way heading in is taken backwards), and the HUD says 「卡住了？按 R 回到路上」 after 3 s of throttle or reverse going nowhere. **Trap:** the palace's service roads are not joined to the city's graph at all (37 components; the one the report came from is 6 nodes, a 1 km lane from the north-west corner down the west side, stopping between two groves) - it is signed at its own little core. `DeadEnds.test.ts` covers the peeling on a hand-built graph and the real city (the palace lane signed, no post on any carriageway); `node .scratch/deadendprobe.mjs [x,z,yaw]` presses R facing into the palace lane and prints the new heading, `node .scratch/stuckhint.mjs` holds the throttle against a palace wall in the real loop until the hint shows.

## Handling critic loop
- M0's bar is a harsh handling critic (a sub-agent) scoring >= 7/10 for GTA-V-style arcade feel. Round 1 (2026-09-11) scored 5.5: rear let go first above ~75 km/h, drifts ignored steering and could not be left with W held, keyboard taps were all-or-nothing, fake wheelspin smoke, no donuts. Report, probes and traces: `.scratch/critic/` (`handling-r1.md`, `probe*.test.ts`, run with `npx vitest run --config .scratch/critic/vitest.config.ts <file>`).
- Every issue it found now has a regression test in `Handling.test.ts` (held key at 100/120 km/h, drift exit with W held, handbrake-90 overshoot, grip-corner skid, launch rpm, donut, key-tap linearity, 120 km/h tap weave, slalom).
- Keyboard steering (`ControlFilter`, exported `KEYBOARD`) is a first-order lag, not a rate limiter: a rate limiter clamps at centre, so taps under half duty average ~duty² instead of duty. A held key also gets less lock at speed, which is only safe with the slow high-speed lag (`tauFast` 0.3); with a short lag the lock returned as the car slowed in a long turn and a held key at 120 km/h slid 32-38° late. `.scratch/sweep/keys.test.ts` sweeps these (`npx vitest run --config .scratch/sweep/vitest.config.ts .scratch/sweep/keys.test.ts` -> `keys.txt`).
- Change one thing, re-run the whole suite: several fixes this round moved a different probe (the drift cap floor trades drift exit against keyboard figure-eight share).

## Measured (M2 Pro, 1600x900, city)
- High: 333 draw calls, 2.1M triangles; low: 154 calls, 0.5M. Both hold 60 fps with no resolution drop (`scaleMin 1`).
- Frame cost measured in-page (300 fixed steps + 120 ticks at 1600x900, `.scratch/phys.mjs`): fixed step 0.4 ms, full frame 8.1 ms on high. The perf script's GPU-synced figure swings from 12 to 24 ms with machine load, so trust it only on a quiet machine and compare runs taken back to back.
- `node .scratch/render/perf.mjs http://127.0.0.1:5195 high,low run "world=city"` measures it; `node .scratch/soak.mjs 90 high` drives the attract loop and logs geometries, textures and JS heap every 10 s (heap settles near 200 MB, no leak).
- Where a frame goes (2026-09-27): `node .scratch/tam/budget.mjs "fx,fy,fz>tx,ty,tz" [quality]` waits for streaming to settle, then hides each category of mesh in turn (by name; unnamed ones by material) and prints the draw calls and triangles it took, shadow passes included. At the spawn it found the traffic cars were 60% of the triangles on high (2.32M) and 35% on low (615k): `CarKit`'s meshes are not frustum culled (their instances span the pool), so every car within 330 m was drawn, behind the camera too, and again into every shadow cascade. Now `CarKit.set` leaves out cars outside the camera's frustum (sphere: half the car's length plus `SHADOW_REACH` 8 m for a shadow falling into view; `CarKit.view(camera)` is set once a frame by traffic, which updates before police, races and events; callers still write 0..n-1 and `commit(n)`), and the kits' paint, trim and wheels are simplified at boot by meshoptimizer (`vehicle/KitLod.ts`, attribute-aware: normals, UVs, colours, paint tone and part flags, so the grille, badge and two-tone line survive; a sedan's paint 5.2k -> 1.0k triangles; `readyKitLod()` is awaited in traffic's install; meshoptimizer moved to dependencies). The 此路不通 signs, 兔儿爷 and shortcut lantern gates were each one mesh for the whole city and are now drawn only near the camera (350 / 250 / 400 m). Spawn frame: high 360 calls / 2.32M triangles -> 324 / 0.82M, low 169 / 615k -> 140 / 338k. `node .scratch/tam/cullab.mjs outPrefix "from>to" [params]` shoots one camera with and without the car culling (0 pixels over 30 apart from above at tod 7.2); the vehicle lab's kit view takes `?lod=0` and `?cam=fx,fy,fz>tx,ty,tz` to compare the simplified kits up close.

## Conventions
- Physics/gameplay in `fixedUpdate`/`postStep`, visuals in `update` (interpolate with `alpha`; `VehicleApi.renderPos/renderQuat`).
- Repeated things are instanced; skid marks and smoke are one draw call each. No per-frame allocation in hot paths.
- macOS filesystem is case-insensitive: never create two files differing only in case (`poses.ts` overwrote `Poses.ts` once).
- Commit messages Chinese, prefixed `driveCity：`, commit only when asked.
