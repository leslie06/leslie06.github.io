# b城追车 / B CITY CHASE (folder: drive-city) — GTA-style open world in Beijing (three.js)

Design doc: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f84d740e-9e09-4de4-8d47-41c433772ad6 (milestones M0–M5).
Milestones: M0 vehicle sandbox done (handling critic 7.0/10). M1–M3 built: central Beijing from OSM (`?world=city` is the default in `main.ts`; any other value, e.g. `?world=yard`, gives the M0 sandbox, which is also the only world where the `ui_hud` / `ui_menu` poses are registered), tile streaming, traffic, getting out and carjacking, pedestrians. M4 in progress: wanted level and police (`police/`), minimap/map/GPS (`nav/`), then missions.

## Stack
- Vite 8 + TypeScript (strict), three r185, @dimforge/rapier3d-compat 0.20. Dev server: `npx vite --port 5195`. Typecheck: `npx tsc --noEmit`.
- `npm install` needs `--legacy-peer-deps` (npm 10's arborist crashes on vitest's optional peers).
- No runtime network requests for content: textures are drawn on canvases (`world/Textures.ts`), sound is Web Audio synthesis (`audio/`). The one exception is the optional leaderboard (`online/Leaderboard.ts`), which never blocks anything.

## Architecture (src/)
- `core/` — copied from gunFight and trimmed: Engine (fixed 60 Hz `fixedUpdate` → `physics.step()` → `postStep`, then per-frame `update(dt, alpha)`), Input (keyboard GTA-V layout + standard gamepad), Physics (Rapier; groups `CG.WORLD/CAR/PROP`), Quality (tiers low/medium/high from the bound GPU), Resolution governor, I18n (zh/en; every on-screen string goes through `t()`), Assets.compact (releases canvas sources after upload).
- `game/Contracts.ts` — the only cross-module interfaces (VehicleApi, CameraApi, WorldApi, HudApi). Extend additively.
- Boot order in `main.ts`: render → world (yard or city) → vehicle → player (camera, on foot) → traffic → people → police → nav → missions → races → park → home → garage → underground → stunts (+ jumps, shortcuts) → collect → intro → events → leaderboard → story → fx → audio → ui (city-only modules skip in the yard). `install()` may only `engine.get` earlier modules; later ones are looked up lazily at runtime.
- `vehicle/` — eight body types: six cars (sedan/taxi, hatch, SUV, MPV, 12 m bus, box truck) built by a procedural cap-and-wall mesher, plus a motorcycle and a bicycle (`TwoWheelers.ts`, primitives: tubes, boxes, tori; `SEAT` is where the rider's pelvis goes). A two-wheeler is the same ray-cast car on a 0.32 m track (`VehicleSpec.single`: the four mounts are a narrow pair per axle, drawn as one wheel at x = 0 by `CarModel` and `CarKit`), its tyre forces act AT the centre of mass (`forceHeight` with no lever: with a lever the friction that stops a sideways creep rolled the narrow body, the rolled springs pushed it on, and it slid at a steady 0.08 m/s standing still), and the lean into corners is drawn, not simulated: `vehicle/index.ts` leans the body up to 32° with the lateral acceleration and publishes it as `VehicleApi.lean`; `player/` draws the character in the saddle with the `ride` gait (Animator: crouched over the tank, or upright and pedalling at the road speed), pelvis rolled by that lean, and hurts the player in a two-wheeler crash. The bicycle's engine is the rider's cadence (40-125 rpm, six gears, 35 km/h) and is silent (`audio/`). Traffic keeps motorcycles (`spawnBike`, `Npc.bike`; 8, or 5 on low - three were too few to come across) standing on pavements 30-200 m from the player to take with F; nobody in traffic rides. Bicycles are the shared bikes in the street racks (2026-09-25, 「自行车就骑共享单车就行」): on foot within 1.8 m of one (and nearer than any car), F takes it - `WorldApi.sharedBike` / `takeSharedBike` (city/Knock.ts `nearestBike`, which hides that instance like a knocked one) and `TrafficCars.rentBike` put a bicycle Vehicle where it stood, framed in its operator's colour; `node .scratch/sharedbike.mjs` counts the motorcycles, takes a shared bike with F and rides it off. `Bodies.test.ts` covers them like the cars (0-100 in 5.7 s, 175 km/h; the bicycle 0-25 in 5.5 s); `node .scratch/bikeprobe.mjs [moto|bike]` rides one round the villa's plaza under the real loop and screenshots the rider, `node .scratch/labshot.mjs "view=lineup&types=moto,bike"` renders the models in the lab. (`Mesher.ts`, `Shell.ts`, `Bodies.ts`): faces are classified into paint, glass, trim and lamps by grid position, so seams and frames stay crisp. One kit per type, 4 draw calls each; `SPEC_OF` maps a type to its spec, and `CarLook.body` carries it through carjacking (`swapCar` rebuilds the player's model when the type changes). `src/vehicle/lab.html` renders bodies without the city. The handling. `Vehicle.ts` is a ray-cast car on a Rapier body with its own tyre model (not Rapier's DynamicRayCastVehicleController: its friction-slip tyres drop grip in one step, so slides cannot be tuned). `Spec.ts` holds every tuning number with its meaning. `ControlFilter.ts` turns keys into pedal/steer positions. `Autopilot.ts` (PathPilot) drives for tests, shots and the title screen. `CarModel.ts` builds the taxi from extruded side profiles.
- `world/` — the yard: `Layout.ts` (all positions, shared with poses/tests), paint, props (instanced + small dynamic bodies), structures, skyline.
- Feel (2026-09-24): a crash over impact 6 hit-stops the world (`engine.timeScale` 0.25 for 40-130 ms of wall clock, never in shot mode), the camera shake is sharper (`/7`, decay 2.4) and the gamepad rumbles on impacts and landings (`Input.rumble`, `vibrationActuator`).
- `render/` — sky and time of day (`?tod=17.5`, `?rain=0.8`, `?timescale=N`), CSM shadows, post chain (AO, motion blur, bloom, AgX, SMAA: canvas MSAA is off), rain. Materials opt into the wet look with `userData.wet = 'ground'` (roads: puddles), `'surface'` (paint, glass) or `true` (cloth); `uniforms.uNight` drives lamps, windows and headlights. `RenderSystem.prepare(root)` patches a streamed tile before it is added.
- Street lights (2026-09-25, 「晚上的时候，没有路灯，路面特别黑」): lamps were only on tertiary roads and up, so every residential street and hutong was black, and even where they stood the heads only glowed - nothing lit the road. `scripts/city/build.mjs` now puts lamps on every car road but service roads (`LAMPS`: spacing per class, 24-36 m, starting half a gap in so they do not bunch at corners, skipping spots within 1.2 m of a street tree): 18,334 -> 32,457, nothing else in the tiles changed. It also fixes the arm yaw, which was taken after `side` flipped and pointed every two-way street's arms away from the road. The light itself is `render/StreetLights.ts`: the heads the streamer has loaded (`RenderSystem.setStreetLamps`, pushed from `CityStreamer.pushHeads`) are splatted as additive quads into a 512/1024 RGBA16F top-down map of +-256 m round the camera (R = sum f/d^2, GB = the same weighted by the offset to each head), redrawn only when the set changes or the camera moves 48 m, and `Lighting`'s material scan injects one extra `RE_Direct` into every lit material from it (diffuse + specular, roughness floored at 0.3; no shadows; `|dc-lamp` in the program key). Strength `INTENSITY` 36 with the night exposure at 6.5; `?lamps=0.5` scales it, `?lamps=0` for A/B. The splat quad needs `DoubleSide` (the top-down view mirrors its winding, and a culled splat leaves the map silently empty), and it must be a plain Mesh on an InstancedBufferGeometry, not an InstancedMesh. Probes: `node .scratch/lampview.mjs out.png "cx,cy,cz" "lx,ly,lz" [params]` shoots any camera at night (it sets the camera before preloading and yields between ticks: a synchronous tick loop with the camera still at the spawn unloads everything just preloaded, and worker results never arrive), `AT=x,z node .scratch/lampdebug.mjs [params]` prints the map's texels at the nearest heads (1/64 = 0.0156 right under one), `node .scratch/nightreal.mjs "tod=1&rain=0.8&quality=low" out.png` the real game loop from the title. The low tier is where night is darkest; rain hides the moon and is the case the report came from.
- Knocking street furniture (2026-09-25, 「开车碰到垃圾箱、共享单车、公路围栏没声效和撞击画面」): bins, shared bikes and railings are instanced pools with no colliders (thousands per tile), so the car went through them in silence. `city/Knock.ts` (`StreetKnocks`, owned by the streamer as `streamer.knocks`, added as its own system) tests the player's chassis footprint (from `spec.chassis`) against the raw furniture of the 3x3 tiles round the car every fixed step (each kind a few circles in its own frame; above 1.2 m/s). A hit hides that instance (`isHidden`; the streamer refills its furniture pools when `knocks.version` changes), puts a dynamic PROP body of the same shape there with the car's velocity plus a shove and a tumble, throws debris (bin rubbish, chips) and HDR sparks off metal above 6 m/s, a dust puff through `fx.smoke`, and emits `prop:hit` - audio synthesises a hollow bonk and rattle (bin), a clatter and the bell (bike), a clang of inharmonic partials and a scrape (rail); the camera jolts a little. It deliberately does not emit `vehicle:impact` (damage, the delivery job's spill, hit-stop). Knocked things go at 150 m and the instance comes back at 250 m. Bus shelters are fixed instead: `addBody` gives each two boxes from the tile's raw furniture (the back row of posts, glass, lightbox and bench; the canopy, 2.54 m up, so a car passes under it and a bus does not), tagged `shelter` - a real crash; `node .scratch/shelterprobe.mjs [outDir] [speed]` drives into one through its open front and into its end. Probes: `SIDE=1 KINDS=bin,bike,rail node .scratch/knockprobe.mjs outDir [speed] [params]` coasts the car into the nearest of each and screenshots it (shot mode starts with no detail tiles: it ticks 90 frames first), `node .scratch/knocksound.mjs` finds the master bus by hooking `AudioNode.connect` and reads each knock's level.
- `city/` — OSM → tiles (`scripts/city/`), worker meshing and streaming around the camera, landmarks in `city/landmarks/`. The region (`scripts/city/region.mjs`) was extended south-east on 2026-09-12 to reach 北京欢乐谷 and the East 4th Ring Road: bbox now s 39.857 / e 116.504, 4x4 Overpass chunks, 718 tiles -> 1238 (7.8 -> 12 MB). The projection origin never moves, so widening the bbox only adds tiles; the spawn is pinned by name near 建国门外大街 and did not shift.
- `traffic/` — `LaneGraph` (right-hand traffic), `Signals` (two-phase clusters; `greenLeft` lets pedestrians cross with the parallel green), `AiDriver` (pure pursuit + IDM), `CarKit` (all cars of one livery as instances, ~20 draw calls). Also holds parked cars: `takeCar/parkCar` implement carjacking.
- Traffic lights (every junction since 2026-09-21, 「在路口都装上红绿灯，汽车和行人都要遵守」): OSM marks 463 signal nodes here, and the first inference (two roads of tertiary class or better crossing on different axes, now `?lights=main`) still left 2,633 of the city's 3,591 street junctions dark - drivers went through them without looking and pedestrians crossed on a gap. `Signals` now lights any node where three or more street arms meet and do not all run the same way (`junctionKind`): 2,096 junctions (475 before), 6,317 approaches, built in 29 ms at boot. Not junctions: service roads and living streets (a driveway's mouth), `_link` ramps where they meet a trunk or motorway (they merge), anything on a motorway, and a fork. Two plans: MAJOR (30 s main road / 16 s side street, cycle 56) wherever a road of tertiary class or better is involved, MINOR (15/12, cycle 37) where only lanes meet (840 junctions) - a 56 s cycle held a hutong car up to 40 s at every corner. `greenSpan(c, phase)` and `cycleOf(c)` are per junction. **Clustering trap:** nodes used to merge into one clock at 45 m single-linkage, but junctions in this city are a median 30 m apart, so with every junction lit whole blocks chain into one; now signal nodes of two main crossings join at 45 m, anything else at 25 m, and nothing joins more than 60 m from the cluster's most important node (largest cluster 73 m across, was 101). The cost, measured with `.scratch/order.mjs` on the same build (`lights=main` against the default; the probe is deterministic, run twice bit-identical): at the spawn arterials 20.8 -> 20.1 km/h, side streets 25.1 -> 18.4; in the lanes of 金鱼池 (`AT=330,2257`) arterials 28.9 -> 28.7, side streets 27.6 -> 22.1. Obedience is counted, not assumed: `traffic.lineStats` counts stop lines crossed and how many on red, `people.debug.stats()` counts crossings started at a light and how many on red - 0 of 50 and 0 of 12 at the spawn, 0 of 60 and 0 of 13 in the lanes; a mutant that ignored red and gave up after 5 s scored 20 of 58 and 5 of 12, so the counters are live. `src/traffic/Order.test.ts` covers which junctions are lit (lanes, a lane into a main road, driveways, ramps, a motorway, a fork, close pairs sharing a clock, a chain of close junctions not merging), that every real junction of ordinary streets is lit, and that a driver stops at the line on red and goes on green. **And the lamps were never visible**: `SignalHeads` put each lamp 0.17 m proud of the housing's centre, right for a 0.32 m deep box, but the box is 0.42 m deep along the traffic it faces, so every lamp in the city sat 4 cm inside it and no light was ever seen to change - `.scratch/lights/lamps.mjs [out.png] [seconds]` prints the nearest heads' lamp positions, facing and colour, and shoots one from 6 m. The heads are now the nearest 240 approaches (sorted: the first 160 the grid listed could leave the junction in front of you dark), down to 8 m long. Once visible, the lit colours (HDR 3.2-3.6) turned out bleached by AgX - red read as salmon, green as mint - so they peak under ~1.8 with one channel dominant. The `traffic_lane_junction` pose shows a short-cycle junction in 金鱼池 at eye height.
- Pitfall (the game hangs, 2026-09-23, 「我把车开到家门口就卡住了……游戏死机了」): 「卡住」 meant the whole game, and every home probe missed it because they ran `stepFixed` only, or `tick` only after arriving. `.scratch/homefreeze.mjs [url] [quality] [sw|north|south|tree|none]` drives to the villa with the REAL frame loop, times every system call of every tick, and when a chunk of frames does not return it pauses V8 through CDP (`Debugger.pause`) and prints the call stack - the only way to name an infinite loop. It sat in `AiDriver.update` -> `fill`: the OSM network has 74 one-way roads that end with no way on (the west end of 景恒街 is 320 m from the villa's gate, inside the 330 m traffic radius, which is why home was where it always happened), and `LaneGraph.next` answered such an end with the link itself, so the driver's advance loop (`s > len - 0.3`) took the same link again, projected the car onto its end again, and spun forever with the main thread. Now `next` returns -1 there (the reverse link at a two-way end, as before), `fill` stops short, the driver brakes for the end of the road as for a red light and goes `lost` (the pool recycles it out of view), the advance loop is capped at 6 hops whatever the data says, and `spawn` skips such links (`endsAfter`). `DeadEnd.test.ts` covers all of it and checks the real network: no link answers with itself except a closed ring (8 loop roads, driven round again by design). Two traps from the hunt: `world.contactPairsWith` reports broad-phase pairs, so a probe that prints "touching" must check the manifold has contacts (the tile's building trimesh otherwise reads as touched at d=0 the whole way); and in headless Chromium `renderFrame` takes ~100 ms once per 30 frames regardless - that is the GPU sync, not the game.
- `audio/Radio.ts` — the car radio (2026-09-23, 「开车的时候要能听歌，GTA 里的 rap」): real rap is licensed and the game ships no files, so 「B城电台」 composes hip-hop live with Web Audio - kick / snare / hats, an 808 with slides, sawtooth pad chords over a i-VI-III-VII turnaround, a pentatonic hook through a feedback delay - eight titled tracks, each a seed, tempo and key (four boom-bap, four half-time trap), arranged intro / verse / hook / verse / hook / outro (40 bars) and then the next. A second station 「我的歌单」 appears when `public/music/playlist.json` exists (`[{ "title", "file" }]`, files beside it; do not commit copyrighted songs - keep them local). N cycles station → station → off (`radioPressed`), the choice persists in `drivecity.radio`, and it only sounds while the player is in a car and not paused; the HUD toasts the station and track on every change. It sits on its own bus (0.45, compressed) under the master so M mutes it too. `Radio.test.ts` pins the arrangement to its seed; `node .scratch/radioprobe.mjs` unlocks audio in a live page, reads the bus with an analyser and walks through N and getting out.
- `player/` — `PlayerApi`: driving or on foot (F gets in/out, taking the nearest car), `OnFoot` (Rapier character controller capsule), on-foot orbit camera in `CameraRig`. Any `vehicle:reset` puts the player back in the car.
- `character/` — one continuous skinned body (`Sdf.ts`/`BodyMesh.ts`) drawn for the whole crowd by instanced GPU skinning: joint matrices and the packed `Look` live in an RGBA32F texture, skinned in `onBeforeCompile` on a MeshStandardMaterial (8 draw calls including shadows, near/far LOD at 22 m). Everyone shares one `Crowd`: the player adds first, then pedestrians, then a taxi fare, and every `add` leaves the meshes drawable (no end() needed). Set `crowd.cam` so the LOD has a camera. `Gait` is procedural with foot-planted IK. **Trap:** the crowd's skinning lives in a chained `onBeforeCompile`; if `render/Lighting`'s material scan ever overwrites hooks instead of chaining, everyone collapses to their bind pose.
- `people/` — walking into someone only makes them step aside; `PeopleApi.shove` (mouse, E or gamepad B, via `OnFoot.shove` and the `punch` gait action) is what puts them down. Pedestrians walk pavement lines (widths mirror `SIDEWALK` in `city/Roads.ts`), cross at junctions, flee fast cars and horns, get knocked over (`people:hit`). A pedestrian waiting at a kerb who is scared by a car steps back onto the pavement they came along (`stepBack`); before, `scare` turned the wait into `flee`, which sprints along the crossing they were waiting to make - on red, in front of the car, and any car passing in the near lane is inside the scare zone. A fleeing pedestrian who reaches a lit kerb stops for the light too; only an unlit corner is run across. At a light the wait's `hold` is a full cycle plus 10 s, so nobody gives up before their green comes round.
- How many and where: `PED_CAP` (24/44/70 by tier since 2026-09-25, was 12/22/34: 「增加路上行人的数量」; `?peds=N` to override) — the crowd texture holds more, but a hundred people inside the 100 m spawn ring read as a crowd scene. Cheap: the people system is 0.09 ms a frame at 34 and 0.15 at 80 (`node .scratch/pedcost.mjs [quality] [N,N]`); at the spawn on foot, people within 60 m went 6.3 -> 16.3 (`node .scratch/peddensity.mjs out.png [params]`), and `order.mjs` at 70 against 34 showed traffic no slower and 0 pedestrian crossings on red. They keep to the middle of the pavement (`frac` 0.3-0.9), and at a kerb they would rather walk on around the corner than cross: crossings are scored by distance and by how straight on they are, or people strike out across the middle of a junction. Every kerb is a stop — for the parallel green with enough of it left to finish the walk, or (unsignalised) for a `GAP`-second hole in the traffic gathered in `threats`, with `hold` seconds before a Beijing pedestrian gives up and goes anyway. A crossing wider than its phase is green for (a six-lane arterial takes ~17 s to walk, the side street's green is 16) would otherwise never fit, so those start on the change and finish into the amber. Anyone with a car bearing down on them runs, whether it is the player's or not (before that, only the player's car scared them and traffic mowed them down at crossings).
- `police/` — `WantedApi` as system `wanted`: witnessed crimes add heat (stars), police cars (`Pursuit` driver, road routes from `nav`) chase, box in and bust; out of sight they search the last-seen circle. `?wanted=N` starts wanted. 2026-09-24: a crime nobody in uniform saw no longer rolls a die - `people.witness(x, z)` picks the nearest walker (or a runner, once they have run clear), who stops, pulls out a phone (`Gait.prop`) and dials for 3.5-5 s, then `people:report` lands the star; knocking or scaring them off the phone (a car coming at them) drops the call. Each new star toasts what it means (`wanted.up1-5`), pops on the HUD and plays a siren whoop (`wanted:level` -> audio `stinger`); while they search, a bar under the stars fills with `WantedApi.evade`; from three stars `Pursuit` rams instead of matching speed. Pedestrians shout (`people:shout`, HUD bubbles projected from world points: 「哎哟！」「看着点儿！」「我报警了！」) and traffic drivers hit or held up by the player lean on the horn (`traffic:horn`, per-driver cooldown). `node .scratch/witnessprobe.mjs` runs someone down, lets a witness call, and charges at one.
- `nav/` — A* router over the lane graph (landmark heuristic, one-ways, turn penalties; under 1 ms), the radar in `ui/Minimap.ts`, the full map in `ui/MapScreen.ts` (Tab; pauses the game), and map data prepared in a worker. Two GPS slots: the player's map waypoint wins over a mission target, and each clears on its own (`setTarget` / `clearTarget`).
- `ui/Touch.ts` — on-screen controls for phones, auto-detected from a coarse pointer with touch points (`?touch=1` forces them on, `?touch=0` off). A floating stick on the left half, pedals and buttons bottom-right, a drag anywhere else to look. It writes into `Input.touch`, which `poll` merges with the keyboard and pad, so no other module knows about touch. The body gets `dc-touch` (never `touch`: the root element uses that class and would pin the body), which shrinks the speedo, radar and top-right readouts; pointer lock is skipped on touch.
- The first batch of "make it fun" (2026-09-25, after 「现在游戏场景和玩法感觉都没意思」: money had nothing to buy, a bust cost nothing, the opening was a 1.2 km drive to a fare). Four systems, each with a probe:
  - Nitro: `Vehicle.tune` (per car, because every car of a type shares one spec object: engine `torque`/`top`, tyre `grip`, `nitro` = seconds a bottle burns) and `Vehicle.boost` (a push at the centre of mass through `push`, so the impact detector does not read it as a crash; allowed to 1.3x the limiter). `vehicle/index.ts` burns it while `InputState.nitro` (Shift; pad B or LB; the touch N₂O button, shown only when the car has a bottle) is held with throttle, `NITRO_ACCEL` 7 m/s²; `VehicleApi.nitroActive` drives the FOV kick and rumble (CameraRig), exhaust flames (fx `Flames`), the hiss (audio) and the HUD bottle beside the speedo. `node .scratch/nitroprobe.mjs` - a taxi's 0-100 goes from >6 s to 2.7 s.
  - Street combo (`stunts/`): near misses (the two footprints' gap while alongside, < 0.8 m, closing > 6 m/s, untouched), drifts (`drift:end`), air (`vehicle:land`), oncoming (against a one-way, or on the left of a two-way link - right-hand traffic), red lights (the stop line of the link being driven, `STOP_LINE` from AiDriver, crossed on red), knocked furniture and losing the police. Moves within 4 s chain (x1, x1, x2, x2 ... x5), bank as cash at 1/15 per point and refill the nitro; a crash over 5 loses the chain, getting out banks it. Events `stunt:event` / `stunt:bank`. `node .scratch/stuntprobe.mjs` does each move (hold the throttle through `Input.touch`: a coasting car engine-brakes below the thresholds before it gets there).
  - Garage (`garage/`): parking in the villa's garage opens an upgrade screen (`UiApi.overlay`: menu hidden, driving off, pointer free, touch pads hidden, the world running). What is bought goes on the car in the garage, which becomes *your car* (`drivecity.garage.v1`, rebuilt at `game:start`); `Parts.ts` holds the stages and prices. Busted (`wanted:busted`, emitted by police) fines `100 + 150/star`, and in your car it is impounded at the respawn (swapped for a stock taxi) until bought back here for ¥300. `node .scratch/garageprobe.mjs` buys through the buttons, gets busted, buys the car back and starts a new session in it.
  - Crazy-Taxi fares (`missions/index.ts`): up to four hailers wave (`Animator` action `wave`) on kerbs 45-260 m out, marked green/yellow/red for 300-800 / 800-1500 / 1500-2600 road metres; the first fare starts a 60 s shift, each passenger has a clock (route / 10.5 m/s + 10 s), arriving with time left pays a bonus and adds 12/8/4 s, stunts aboard are tipped, and the shift ends with a grade (best in `drivecity.taxi.best`). Destinations: a named place in range, else a street address (`missions/Address.ts`, shared with Jobs). The mission HUD now shows the jobs' objective too - it only ever showed the fares', so a delivery's address and clock were never on screen. `node .scratch/taxiprobe.mjs` runs a shift.
  - Opening getaway (`intro/`): a new player (no `drivecity.intro.v1`, no garage save; `?intro=1` forces, `?intro=0` skips, never in shot mode) starts in a black saloon with nitro and two stars on 建国门内大街 heading east (found by name: the nearest link to a point picked a slip road into the railings), held still (`step 'load'`) while those streets stream in; lose the police, drive home, park: ¥800, the car is yours (engine 1, nitro 1) and the garage opens; leaving it, a taxi is parked on the drive. Busted, it starts over. `MissionApi.story` carries its objective line. `node .scratch/introprobe.mjs` - reload after clearing storage (the garage reads its save at boot), and a probe that teleports the car must move the camera too: traffic drops a parked car 420 m from the camera, and the camera follows the interpolated pose, which lags a raw `Vehicle.reset`.

- Home probe: `node .scratch/homeprobe.mjs` (dev server up) drives the villa end to end and prints what happened: the garage door shut at 300 m and still shut on the drive 26 m out but open inside, parking as the save (health 40 -> 100, wanted 2 -> 0, `drivecity.home.v1` written with the car's body and colours), the car resting at the parking spot rather than fighting a collider, and the feet landing on the first floor (4.89 against a 4.85 slab), the ground floor (1.09/1.05) and the lawn (0.04/0.03) instead of falling through.

## Handling tests (the M0 acceptance)
- `npx vitest run` — `vehicle/Handling.test.ts` drives the real Vehicle through the real ControlFilter headlessly (Rapier in Node, <1 s) and writes `.scratch/handling-report.txt` (0-100, braking, skidpad g, handbrake turn, figure-eight drift with gamepad and keyboard-style input, jump, slope hold, self-righting, reverse, burnout).
- Vitest hides console output of passing tests: read the report file.
- `TRACE=fig8|fig8key|powerdrift|park|slope|accel|brake|handbrake npx vitest run src/vehicle/Trace.test.ts` writes a time trace to `.scratch/trace-<name>.txt`.
- Pitfall (static hosting): a dev server swallows sixty parallel image requests; GitHub Pages queues them on one HTTP/2 connection. Unthrottled, the first live load took 178 s to become playable, with single textures stalled over two minutes and one `ERR_HTTP2_PROTOCOL_ERROR`. Three changes were needed, in this order of impact: `city/Materials.ts` no longer awaits the photo sets, so materials start as flat colours and take their maps when they arrive (only the facade's brick and plaster are awaited); `core/Assets.ts` allows four image requests in flight; `nav/index.ts` holds its 718-file map crawl until 20 s after `game:start` or the moment the map opens. `scripts/deploy.mjs` also drops the ao and disp maps and halves normals and roughness, taking the published build from 70.9 MB to 18.7 MB. Measure a deploy with `node .scratch/boottime.mjs <url>`, which prints time-to-playable and the slowest resources; the local dev server will not show this.
- Pitfall (static hosting, round two): after all that, the live build still took 72-85 s to become playable. `node .scratch/waterfall.mjs <url>` prints the live request waterfall (start, queue, duration, size) and what was in flight when a given request went out, which is what found it: the tile worker's 42 KB module script was requested only at 28 s - `city/index.ts` awaited `createCityMaterials`, which waits for the two facade photo sets - and then took 42 s to arrive behind 10 MB of images, finishing 1.5 s before the boot did. No tile can be meshed without a worker, so the whole boot sat on it. `city/index.ts` now calls `spawnTileWorkers()` (exported by `city/Streamer.ts`) before its `Promise.all`, so that fetch goes out while the connection is still idle; the `CityStreamer` constructor takes the workers it made. Cutting the worker count would not have helped: all four share one module fetch and wait on the same response.
- Pitfall (phones): the render budget is `maxPixels`, not `pixelRatio` - capping both starves a phone of resolution. A 3x iPhone in landscape is 750x342 CSS pixels, so `low`'s `pixelRatio: 1` (plus a `coarse ? 1.3` clamp on dpr) drew 0.26 MP and let the browser blow it up 3x onto a 2250x1026 screen - one rendered pixel per nine physical ones, and six times *under* low's own 1.6 MP budget. That was the whole of 「手机上画面特别差，看不清楚」. Every tier now renders at `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)` and `maxPixels` alone does the budgeting, which is bit-for-bit unchanged wherever the window is big enough for that cap to bind (1920x1080 at low is ratio 0.879 either way, and a retina desktop at high still lands on exactly 3.6 MP). The phone buffer goes 750x342 -> 1500x684 at a held 60 fps. `minRenderScale` is 1 on low (0.75 until 2026-09-25) for the same reason: 4x the pixels cost only +32% of a frame there because a low-tier frame is fixed-cost bound (~200 draw calls), so shedding pixels buys the governor almost nothing - and a real phone that could not hold 60 was parked at 0.75, a 2x upscale, which was 「画面特别差，画面不清晰」 the second time. Low also has 1024 photo maps (the deployed files are 1024, so nothing more downloads; was 512), anisotropy 8 and a CAS sharpen (0.3, without the chromatic aberration high adds). `node .scratch/phonelook.mjs out.png [params]` screenshots an emulated iPhone at full device resolution (`SCALE=0.75` forces the render scale). Rotation: the engine follows the container with a ResizeObserver plus orientation / visualViewport events and late re-checks - window 'resize' alone fires on iOS before the page is laid out again, so the landscape picture kept the portrait aspect (the car stretched sideways, 「刚开始车很宽」) until the governor next resized; `node .scratch/rotateprobe.mjs` rotates an emulated iPhone and also changes the container with no event at all. Measure with `node .scratch/mobprobe.mjs` (emulated iPhone/Pixel + a retina desktop: tier, buffer, upscale factor, governor scale) and `node .scratch/mobcost.mjs` (frame cost at ratio 1/1.5/2/2.5/3 on one phone-sized page).
- Pitfall (landmarks): a landmark's terraces, steps and bridges are geometry with no collider of their own, so anyone standing on one used to stand on the ground *under* it and look buried. `placeLandmarks` (city/index.ts) builds trimesh colliders from the stone meshes (`marble`, `paving`, `granite`, `stone`), preferring the far LOD. `?nostone` skips them, which is how their cost was measured (nil: 15 colliders, no change to the step).
- Pitfall (landmark stairs, 2026-09-23, 「我走天坛的楼梯上不去，人就埋在台阶里了」): the stone trimesh above is taken from the far LOD, and the kit's `terrace`/`polyTerrace` build their stair flights on the detail LOD only, while the tiers are solid drums or boxes besides - so every flight of 祈年殿, 圜丘, 皇穹宇 and 太和殿 was a picture: the feet walked the ground under the treads and stopped at the tier's face. Both terraces now take `colliders` in their spec and push one walk-only ramp hull per flight (`stairWedge` in `kit/hall.ts`, the villa's trick: steps as boxes are standable but not walkable, and a ramp a person can climb is one a car can drive, so `walkOnly`); a landmark gets them by handing its collider list to the detail build. `landmarks/Stairs.test.ts` rides a ray up every ramp of 祈年殿 and 太和殿 (the two stair code paths; 太和殿's tiers step back exactly one flight, so its ramps stand on each other), and `node .scratch/templewalk.mjs [S|N|E|W|taihedian]` walks the real character up them tier by tier. A new landmark with stairs must pass `colliders`, or its stairs are scenery.
- Pitfall (on foot): Rapier's `KinematicCharacterController` creeps into very large colliders. The city ground is a 28 km cuboid, so gravity sank the player a few millimetres per step and buried them to the waist after a minute's walk. `OnFoot.step` now resolves the feet against a downward ray (lift only) after `computeColliderMovement`; `.scratch/sink2.mjs` measures feet against the ground collider.
- Pitfalls: Rapier 0.20 integrates in 4 substeps, so a body held up by per-step impulses reports vy ≈ -0.375·g·dt while stationary (use horizontal speed). Impulses change velocity immediately: gather all wheel forces, then apply (see `Vehicle.step`).

## Handling critic loop
- M0's bar is a harsh handling critic (a sub-agent) scoring >= 7/10 for GTA-V-style arcade feel. Round 1 (2026-09-11) scored 5.5: rear let go first above ~75 km/h, drifts ignored steering and could not be left with W held, keyboard taps were all-or-nothing, fake wheelspin smoke, no donuts. Report, probes and traces: `.scratch/critic/` (`handling-r1.md`, `probe*.test.ts`, run with `npx vitest run --config .scratch/critic/vitest.config.ts <file>`).
- Every issue it found now has a regression test in `Handling.test.ts` (held key at 100/120 km/h, drift exit with W held, handbrake-90 overshoot, grip-corner skid, launch rpm, donut, key-tap linearity, 120 km/h tap weave, slalom).
- Keyboard steering (`ControlFilter`, exported `KEYBOARD`) is a first-order lag, not a rate limiter: a rate limiter clamps at centre, so taps under half duty average ~duty² instead of duty. A held key also gets less lock at speed, which is only safe with the slow high-speed lag (`tauFast` 0.3); with a short lag the lock returned as the car slowed in a long turn and a held key at 120 km/h slid 32-38° late. `.scratch/sweep/keys.test.ts` sweeps these (`npx vitest run --config .scratch/sweep/vitest.config.ts .scratch/sweep/keys.test.ts` -> `keys.txt`).
- Change one thing, re-run the whole suite: several fixes this round moved a different probe (the drift cap floor trades drift exit against keyboard figure-eight share).

## Conventions
- Physics/gameplay in `fixedUpdate`/`postStep`, visuals in `update` (interpolate with `alpha`; `VehicleApi.renderPos/renderQuat`).
- Repeated things are instanced; skid marks and smoke are one draw call each. No per-frame allocation in hot paths.
- macOS filesystem is case-insensitive: never create two files differing only in case (`poses.ts` overwrote `Poses.ts` once).
- Commit messages Chinese, prefixed `driveCity：`, commit only when asked.
